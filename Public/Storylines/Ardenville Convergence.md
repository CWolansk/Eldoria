# Ardenville Convergence

## Summary
> Ardenville has become the next crossroads for the campaign. Personal problems, guild politics, suspicious deliveries, and the boat race are all tightening around the same lake town.

## What the Party Knows
- The party chose Ardenville instead of heading straight for the Silverleaf Lands.
- JP still has unfinished ship trouble tied to the town and its dock authorities.
- The Merchants Guild is using Ardenville's docks, fees, and warehouses to expand control over lake trade.
- The Exterminators Guild has business in Ardenville through the package delivery job.
- Fisherfolk, racers, and dockworkers all seem caught in the same growing pressure.

## Open Questions
- Can the party untangle personal stakes from guild politics once they arrive?
- What will the boat race change in the balance of power on Lake Arden?
- How much leverage do the merchants really hold over the town?
- Which Ardenville problem will demand the party's attention first?
