# Exterminators Guild

## Summary
> After the duplirat crisis in Highreach, the Exterminators Guild moved quickly to pull the party into its orbit. What looks like a reward may also be a test, and it now points straight toward Ardenville.

## What the Party Knows
- The Exterminators Guild praised the party after the Highreach rat crisis.
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Don Gnocianno|Don Gnocianno]] is the guildmaster who offered them membership and work.
- The current induction task is a sealed package delivery tied to [[Public/World/Groups/Exterminators Guild|Exterminators Guild]] business in Ardenville.
- The package is heavier and more carefully handled than normal pest-control supplies.
- The party's choice to deliver, inspect, or refuse it will shape their standing with the guild.

## Key People
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Don Gnocianno|Don Gnocianno]] - flamboyant guildmaster and quest giver.
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Pip Whiskerwind|Pip Whiskerwind]] - senior guild figure tied to Ardenville business.

## Key Places
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/Highreach|Highreach]] - where the guild first approached the party.
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/Ardenville|Ardenville]] - destination of the current delivery and next major pressure point.

## Open Questions
- What is actually inside the sealed package?
- Is the guild offering honest work, or something more dangerous?
- Who is waiting for the delivery in Ardenville?
- Is this really an induction, or a loyalty test?
