# The Artifact Wars

## Summary
> The party was pulled into Eldoria, tricked into recovering the Seekers Eye, and learned too late that Jon meant to use the artifacts for his own ends. Now the race is on to reach the remaining artifacts before he does.

## What the Party Knows
- Jon summoned the party to Eldoria under false pretenses.
- The [[Public/World/Items/Seekers Eye|Seekers Eye]] was recovered from [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/Highreach Mines|Highreach Mines]] but stolen by [[Public/World/Orcish Wastes/Wizards Tower/NPCs/Summoner Jon AKA the wizard|Summoner Jon AKA the wizard]].
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Jon Brightman|Jon Brightman]] and Diego's legend revealed that more artifacts exist and Jon is hunting them.
- The next leg of the chase points toward the Silverleaf Lands.
- The disaster in [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/Highreach|Highreach]] proved Jon's plans can damage entire cities.

## Key People
- [[Public/World/Orcish Wastes/Wizards Tower/NPCs/Summoner Jon AKA the wizard|Summoner Jon AKA the wizard]] - enemy pursuing the artifacts.
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Jon Brightman|Jon Brightman]] - ally and source of artifact lore.

## Key Places
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/Highreach|Highreach]] - where Jon's deception unraveled.
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/Highreach Mines|Highreach Mines]] - site of the Seekers Eye recovery and collapse.
- Silverleaf Lands - next destination in the hunt.

## Open Questions
- Which artifact can Jon reach next?
- How much of Diego's trail is still intact?
- Who else knows where the remaining artifacts are hidden?
- Can the party reach Silverleaf before Jon's plans advance again?
