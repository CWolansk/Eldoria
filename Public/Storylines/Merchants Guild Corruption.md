# Merchants Guild Corruption

## Summary
> What started as a simple guild job in Highreach turned into murder, intimidation, and open hostility. The party now has every reason to believe the Merchants Guild's leadership is hiding rot behind polished ledgers.

## What the Party Knows
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Alistair Goldman|Alistair Goldman]] hired the party for a private guild job in Highreach.
- Jarek Ironfist was killed shortly after the party refused to handle the job the way Goldman wanted.
- The party later survived a bandit attack tied to guild interests.
- The Merchants Guild still controls major trade pressure points in Highreach and Ardenville.
- Ardenville's docks, warehouse, and upcoming boat race put the guild back in the party's path.

## Key People
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Alistair Goldman|Alistair Goldman]] - guildmaster at the center of the thread.

## Key Places
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/HighReach Merchants Guild|HighReach Merchants Guild]] - where the trouble began.
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/Ardenville|Ardenville]] - where guild pressure now shapes dock politics and local trade.
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/Merchants Guild Warehouse|Merchants Guild Warehouse]] - physical symbol of guild control on the docks.

## Open Questions
- How much of the guild's leadership is compromised?
- How far will Goldman go to protect his position?
- What role will the guild play in Ardenville's boat race and dock disputes?
- Can the party expose the guild without triggering worse retaliation?
