## Reputation

Gunnar Ironforge has armed three generations of Stonehaven's rangers and guards. His work is known throughout the frontier for being practical, reliable, and built to last. If you want something pretty, go to the capital; if you want something that will survive a cave-in or a monster attack, you come to Gunnar.

## Services & Wares

- **Custom Weapons:** Superior quality arms designed for frontier conditions. More expensive than standard, but known to be more durable and effective.
- **Armor Repair:** Expert maintenance and modification of protective gear. Quick turnaround time.
- **Trap Creation:** Mechanical snares and devices for hunting and defense. Popular with local rangers.
- **Specialized Tools:** Mining picks, craftsman's tools, and survival gear built to withstand heavy use.
- **Unusual Metalwork:** Can work with exotic materials if provided, though he's not a mage himself.

## Current Inventory

- **Weapons:** Longswords, battleaxes, hunting bows, crossbow bolts, spears
- **Armor:** Chain mail, leather armor, shields, helmets
- **Tools:** Mining picks, craftsman's tools, survival gear
- **Traps:** Bear traps, snares, caltrops, specialized hunting devices

## Public Knowledge

- **Quality Over Flash:** Gunnar creates functional weapons, not decorative ones. Don't expect gold inlay or fancy engravings.
- **Dwarf Standards:** His work meets dwarven quality standards, which means it's built to outlast you.
- **No Haggling:** Prices are fair but firm. He knows what his work is worth.
- **Custom Orders Welcome:** Given time and proper materials, he can create unusual items. Expect to wait 1-3 weeks for custom work.
- **Ranger Favorite:** Local rangers swear by his arrowheads and hunting traps.
