## Description
> A dark, ancient forest stretching south from Stonehaven into the highlands. Massive tree trunks and interwoven canopy create perpetual twilight beyond the village's cleared perimeter. Recently there have been unsettling reports from travelers.

## What We Know
- Connects to the Ironpeak Mountain range as the most direct route south
- Ancient trees predate human settlement in the area
- Experienced guides know safe paths through the outer reaches
- Deeper sections are said to hold places where mortal feet should not tread
