## Overview

The Blackwood Forest is an ancient expanse of woodland that serves as a natural border between the human lands of Crestfall and the foothills of the Ironpeak Mountains. While it provides timber and game for nearby Stonehaven, it is widely feared for its confusing geography and the dangerous creatures that stalk its darker depths. Local legends speak of old magic lingering here, and even seasoned rangers refuse to enter at night.

## Key Landmarks

- **The Shadowed Path**
    - The primary trade road cutting through the forest, kept clear by Stonehaven rangers but still dangerous after dark.
- **Old Druid's Circle**
    - A ring of moss-covered stones deep in the woods, rumored to be a place of ancient magic. Few who seek it actually find it.
- **The Whispering Hollow**
    - A natural depression where sound echoes strangely and travelers report hearing voices that aren't there.
