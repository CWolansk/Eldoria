**Fortified Town in the Kingdom of Crestfall**
- **Location:** Eastern frontier, edge of Blackwood Forest
- **Government:** Military Commander & Town Council
- **Population:** ~1,500
- **Defenses:** Stone walls, guard towers, garrison

Banner
- Stone tower on black field with silver border

```
Stonehaven stands as Crestfall's eastern bulwark, where civilization meets the wild darkness of Blackwood Forest. Built around an old fortress, the town has grown into a trading post and staging ground for expeditions into the dangerous woodland. Its stone walls have weathered countless attacks from forest creatures, and the garrison maintains constant vigilance.
```

## Layout

Stonehaven is built in concentric rings around the original fortress. The innermost ring contains military facilities, the middle ring holds commercial establishments catering to adventurers, and the outer ring houses residential areas. Stone walls with guard towers encircle the entire settlement.

**Key Areas:**
- The Fortress
	- Original keep serving as garrison headquarters
	- Commander's residence, war room, armory and training grounds
- Market Ward
	- Shops catering to adventurers and explorers
		- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Stonehaven/Ironforge Smithy|Ironforge Smithy]]
		- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Stonehaven/Marlas Gear|Marla's Gear]]

		- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Stonehaven/Stones Herbs|Stone's Herbs]]
- Residential Quarter
	- Sturdy stone homes built to withstand attacks
	- Community shelters strategically placed throughout
