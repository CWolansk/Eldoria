## Reputation

Elara learned herbalism from the local healers and expanded her knowledge through years of experimenting with plants from the Blackwood Forest. While not a magical healer, her understanding of natural medicine is profound. She's saved lives with her antitoxins, eased countless pains with her salves, and helped the town through more than one outbreak of disease.

She's particularly respected by the town guard and rangers, who rely on her healing kits and poison treatments. Her one rule: she'll heal anyone who needs it, but she won't provide herbs for recreational intoxication or anything that could be used to harm others.

## Services & Wares

- **Healing Supplies:** Bandages, healing salves, antiseptic tinctures, pain-relieving teas.
- **Medicinal Herbs:** Dried and fresh herbs for various ailments, properly labeled with instructions.
- **Antitoxins:** Treatments for common poisons, venoms, and toxins found in the region.
- **Remedies:** Cures for common illnesses—fever reducers, cough syrups, digestive aids.
- **Custom Blends:** Can create specific herbal mixtures for particular needs (takes time).
- **Healer's Kits:** Pre-assembled field medicine kits popular with adventurers and rangers.

## Current Inventory

*Stock varies with seasons and foraging expeditions:*
- Healing kits (always available)
- Common medicinal herbs (lavender, sage, willow bark, chamomile)
- Seasonal specialties (depends on what's currently growing)
- Antitoxins for local threats (spider venom, certain plant poisons)

## Public Knowledge

- **Foraging Expertise:** Elara personally harvests many of her herbs from the Blackwood. She knows which plants are safe and which are deadly.
- **No Magical Healing:** She works with natural herbs, not magic. For diseases or wounds beyond herbalism's reach, she refers people to magical healers.
- **Education:** She's happy to teach people about safe herb identification and basic first aid. Many rangers have learned from her.
- **Consultation Services:** Free advice for minor ailments. Complex issues require purchasing supplies.
- **Custom Orders:** Given a description of symptoms or needed effects, she can create specialized treatments (1-3 days).
- **Ranger Discount:** Active duty rangers and town guard get 10% off, in recognition of their service.
- **Ethical Boundaries:** Won't provide anything used to poison others or create dangerous intoxicants.
