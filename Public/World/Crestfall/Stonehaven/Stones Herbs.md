## Description
> The fragrant aroma of dried herbs fills this section. Shelves lined with jars of alchemical ingredients, healing herbs, and colorful potion bottles. A large counter displays mortar-and-pestle sets and alchemical recipes.

## Proprietor

## Services & Wares
- Alchemist's supplies and herbalism kits
- Healer's kits, glass vials, mortar and pestle
- Dried herbs, potion ingredients, incense
- Herb pouches (common and rare varieties)
- Parchment and quills for formulas

## Reputation
The go-to place for potion makers and healers. Darius's deep love for herbs and alchemy shows in his curated selection.
