## Description
> A two-story building marked by crystal wind chimes. Dark wood with mystical symbols around the doorframe. Soft lights glow within at all hours, and herbal scents drift from the door.

## Proprietor
[[Public/World/Crestfall/Stonehaven/NPCs/Hilda Ravenshadow|Hilda Ravenshadow]] — enigmatic wizard, scholar of magical phenomena

## Services & Wares
- Supernatural detection amulets (50 gp)
- Warding charms (+1 vs enchantment, 75 gp)
- Forest Guardian tokens (adv. Survival in Blackwood, 25 gp)
- Potions of Detect Magic (50 gp)
- Item identification (25 gp + components)
- Curse detection (50 gp)
- Supernatural consultation (10 gp/question)

## Reputation
The only source of magical goods and arcane knowledge in Stonehaven. Hilda is cryptic but genuinely helpful.
