```
Stepping into Mystic Wares is like stepping into a different climate. The air is warm and thick with the scent of dried sage, lavender, and sulfur. The shop is small, essentially the converted front room of a cottage, with bundles of drying herbs hanging from the ceiling low enough to brush the heads of taller folk.

Shelves line the walls, packed with clay jars of monster parts, vials of colored liquids, and baskets of fresh roots. A small reading nook in the corner is piled high with books on botany and basic arcane theory.

Elara Moonwhisper, a High Elf with sharp features and ink-stained fingers, usually sits behind the counter grinding ingredients in a mortar. She treats her wares with academic seriousness, often lecturing customers on the proper use of a healing potion versus a poultice.
```

## Reputation

Elara is an oddity in Stonehaven—an Elf who chose to live among "short-lived" humans. She left the Silverleaf Lands thirty years ago due to a disagreement about "sharing magic with lesser races." While she respects her people's traditions, she believes magical knowledge should be accessible to all who seek it responsibly.

She is respected for her healing salves but viewed with mild suspicion when things explode. She has a soft spot for bards and scholars and is one of the few people in town who speaks Draconic. The town guard consults her about supernatural threats, and rangers rely on her for antitoxins.

## Public Knowledge

- **One-Stop Arcane Shop:** Whether you need a scroll or a salve, Elara has merged the town's magical and medicinal needs under one roof. It's more efficient this way.
- **Discretion Guaranteed:** What you buy and why is your business. Elara doesn't gossip about customers.
- **Fair Pricing:** Magical items are expensive, but her prices match guild standards. Herbal remedies are reasonably priced for locals.
- **Foraging Expertise:** Elara personally harvests many herbs from the Blackwood Forest. She knows which plants are safe and which are deadly.
- **Educational:** She's happy to teach people about safe herb identification, spell component uses, and basic magical theory. Many rangers have learned from her.
- **Special Orders:** Given time and coin, she can acquire specific scrolls, components, or create specialized herbal treatments.
- **Supernatural Consultation:** If something strange is happening—cursed items, magical diseases, unexplained phenomena—she's the first person the guard consults.
- **The Silverleaf Connection:** Elara left Frostglade decades ago. She might have information about the **Shadowstride Boots** or contacts in the elven capital. **Liz** (Elf Bard) might find her particularly helpful.
- **No Dark Magic:** She won't deal in necromancy, demon summoning, or anything that harms innocents. Don't ask.
- **Ethical Herbalism:** Won't provide poisons for assassination or dangerous intoxicants.

## Quest Hooks

- **Component Hunt:** Elara will offer discounts (10-15%) if the party brings her intact organs from magical beasts they slay (owlbear feathers, displacer beast hide, etc.).
- **The Blackwood Expedition:** She needs someone to escort her on a dangerous foraging trip into the deeper Blackwood to harvest a rare luminescent moss.
- **Elven Politics:** Her exile from Silverleaf Lands wasn't entirely voluntary. Someone from her past might come looking for her—or for what she knows.
