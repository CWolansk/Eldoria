```
Stonehaven Supplies is the beating heart of the town's daily commerce. Unlike the specialized shops that cater to adventurers, this is where the locals come for flour, nails, candles, and everything else that keeps a frontier town running. The large, well-lit space feels more like a warehouse than a shop, with wide aisles and sturdy shelves stocked floor to ceiling.

Sacks of grain lean against one wall, barrels of salted fish and pickled vegetables line another, and hanging from the rafters are bundles of rope, dried herbs, and preserved meats. The smell is a pleasant mix of sawdust, leather, and fresh bread from the bakery next door. A community board near the entrance is covered with notices—job postings, items for sale, warnings about the roads.

Harold Stone, a broad-shouldered man with a cheerful demeanor, can usually be found hauling crates or helping customers load wagons. His wife Vera manages the counter with brisk efficiency, her sharp eyes missing nothing. Between them, they seem to know every person in Stonehaven by name.
```

## Reputation

The Stones have run this shop for twenty-five years, and it's become more than just a store—it's a community gathering place. Harold and Vera know everyone, remember everyone's needs, and extend credit to families going through hard times. When supplies are scarce, they ration fairly. When someone is in trouble, they're among the first to organize help.

The shop serves as an unofficial town hall where news spreads, rumors are confirmed or debunked, and neighbors catch up on each other's lives.

## Services & Wares

- **Food Staples:** Flour, salt, sugar, dried beans, rice, preserved meats, cheese.
- **Household Goods:** Candles, lamp oil, soap, cloth, thread, needles, cookware.
- **Basic Tools:** Hammers, saws, nails, farm implements, fishing line.
- **Clothing:** Simple, practical garments—work clothes, winter coats, sturdy boots.
- **Animal Feed:** Grain for horses, feed for chickens, hay bales.
- **Miscellaneous:** Paper, ink, simple toys, tobacco, basic medicines.

## Public Knowledge

- **Community Credit:** The Stones will extend credit to townspeople in good standing, payable when crops come in or wages are paid.
- **Bulk Discounts:** Buying in quantity? They'll work with you on price.
- **Special Orders:** If you need something they don't stock, they'll order it when the next supply caravan arrives (which may be a while with the gates sealed).
- **Information Central:** Want to know what's happening in town? Spend ten minutes in the Stones' shop and you'll hear everything.
- **Lost & Found:** The community board often has notices about lost items, found pets, and job opportunities.
- **Fair Dealing:** Prices are reasonable and consistent. They don't price gouge, even during shortages.
