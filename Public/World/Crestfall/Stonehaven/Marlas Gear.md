## Reputation

Marla is the logistical heart of Stonehaven. Whether you're a ranger heading into the Blackwood for a week-long patrol or an adventurer crossing dangerous territory, Marla ensures you don't starve, freeze, or get lost. She's known for her "Fair Trade" policy—she'll often trade goods for interesting trinkets or news from the road, especially if it helps her stock unusual items other merchants don't carry.

## Services & Wares

- **Travel Rations:** Dried meats, hardtack, preserved fruits, trail mix. Properly portioned and sealed.
- **Exploration Gear:** Torches, lanterns, oil, rope (hempen and silk), bedrolls, tents, climbing equipment.
- **Containers:** Backpacks, sacks, pouches, waterskins, cases for scrolls and maps.
- **Basic Clothing:** Sturdy traveling clothes, cloaks, boots suitable for rough terrain.
- **Miscellaneous:** Chalk, ink, parchment, tinderboxes, fishing tackle, and "I forgot to pack this" essentials.

## Current Inventory

*Stock varies based on season and recent expeditions, but Marla typically keeps:*
- 50+ days worth of rations (various types)
- Multiple lengths and types of rope
- A dozen lanterns and torches
- Assorted containers and carrying equipment
- Maps of local trails (hand-drawn by rangers, sold on consignment)

## Public Knowledge

- **Information Hub:** Marla loves to chat, especially with travelers. Buying supplies here is often the best way to hear the latest rumors about road safety, weather conditions, or creature sightings.
- **"I Know a Guy":** If she doesn't sell it, she likely knows who does. She's connected to nearly every merchant and craftsperson in Stonehaven.
- **Fair Trader:** Will accept interesting items in trade, especially if they're useful to other adventurers. Has been known to buy maps, unusual components, or rare goods.
- **Bulk Discounts:** Orders for expeditions or groups get preferential pricing.
- **Consignment Welcome:** Local rangers and adventurers can sell surplus gear through her shop for a small commission.
