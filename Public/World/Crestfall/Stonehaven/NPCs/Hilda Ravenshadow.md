## Appearance
Slender and enigmatic, long black hair streaked with silver. Pale blue eyes that seem to see beyond the physical. Deep purple and midnight blue robes with silver trinkets that chime softly.

## What We Know
Owns [[Public/World/Crestfall/Stonehaven/Mystic Wares|Mystic Wares]] near town center. A traveling scholar of magical phenomena who settled in Stonehaven eight years ago. Specializes in protective charms and supernatural detection. Speaks in riddles and metaphors.

## First Impressions
