## Appearance
Wiry and athletic, silver-streaked brown hair in a practical braid. Weathered face with small scars from decades of adventuring. Keen hazel eyes. Well-maintained leather armor with guild insignia.

## What We Know
Guildmaster of the Stonehaven Adventurers Guild. Adventured for twenty years before settling here. Mixed heritage (half-elf) gives her perspective on both human ambition and elven patience. Judges members by merit, not background.

## First Impressions
Approachable despite her authority — treats everyone as equals. Sharp strategic mind.
