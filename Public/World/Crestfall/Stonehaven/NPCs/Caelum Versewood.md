## Appearance
Wiry half-elf, sharp cheekbones, grey-green eyes. Worn leather coat, longsword at his hip, battered journal always in hand. Ink-stained fingers, soldier's posture.

## What We Know
Traveling poet and sometime sellsword. Arrived in Stonehaven shortly before the gates were sealed. Wanders town to town collecting stories. Usually found in the tavern, scribbling in his journal and buying drinks for anyone willing to talk.

## First Impressions
Quiet, attentive — actually listens when you talk. Soldier's habits under the poet's manners.
