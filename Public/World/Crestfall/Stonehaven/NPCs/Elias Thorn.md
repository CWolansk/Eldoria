## Appearance
Lean and weathered, short brown hair streaked with gray, rugged beard, keen green eyes. Wears practical leather armor in forest colors with a longbow always at hand. Accompanied by Shadowpaw, a large black wolf.

## What We Know
Born and raised in Stonehaven. Over two decades patrolling [[Public/World/Crestfall/Stonehaven/Blackwood Forest|Blackwood Forest]]. Knows every trail within 50 miles. Senior member of the Adventurers Guild. Instrumental in training the current guard.

## First Impressions
Calm and observant — rarely loses composure. Constantly scanning his surroundings.
