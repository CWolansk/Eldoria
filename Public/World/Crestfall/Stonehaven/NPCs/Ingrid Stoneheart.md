## Appearance
Tall and imposing, long dark hair in a severe style. Sharp angular features and piercing gray eyes. Dresses in practical but fine dark clothing. Silver pendant at her throat.

## What We Know
Mayor of Stonehaven, elected six months ago after the previous mayor died in [[Public/World/Crestfall/Stonehaven/Blackwood Forest|Blackwood Forest]]. Ordered the town gates sealed, citing escalating threats. Authoritative and enigmatic — speaks in careful, measured tones.

## First Impressions
