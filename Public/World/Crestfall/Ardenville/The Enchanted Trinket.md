**Curiosities & Nautical Charms**
- **City:** [[Public/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/Ardenville|Ardenville]]
- **Location:** The Docks, built into the hull of a wrecked ship
- **Proprietor:** [[Marina Tidesinger]] (Water Genasi, Sorcerer)
- **Specialty:** Sailor's luck, nautical magic items, item identification
- **Atmosphere:** Damp, mystical, perpetually wet

```
The Enchanted Trinket is impossible to miss on the Ardenville docks—it's literally built into the hull of a shipwreck that's been hauled onto the shore and converted into a shop. The name "Siren's Lament" is still visible on the weathered wood, though half the letters have faded away.

Inside, the floor is constantly damp, and puddles form in the low spots despite there being no visible leak. The sound of waves crashing echoes from the corners of the room even when the door is closed, and the air smells of salt and ozone. Glass buoys filled with glowing bioluminescent algae provide the only light, casting everything in an eerie blue-green glow.

Shelves made from driftwood line the curved walls of the hull, displaying an eclectic collection of items: barnacle-encrusted compasses that still point true, ropes that tie themselves into perfect knots, bottles containing what might be messages or might be something stranger, and jewelry made from shells and sea glass.

Behind a counter made from a ship's wheel mounted horizontally, Marina Tidesinger floats—literally floats a few inches off the ground, as if standing on invisible water. Her hair flows like it's underwater even in still air, shifting between blue and green. Her skin is a pale, translucent blue, and her eyes are the deep color of the ocean depths. When she speaks, her voice sounds like waves crashing on a distant shore.
```

## Reputation

Marina appeared in Ardenville seven years ago, arriving on a ship that promptly sank in the harbor (all hands survived, mysteriously). She bought the salvage rights to the wreck, had it hauled ashore, and opened her shop the next week.

Sailors consider her both good luck and bad—she sells charms that genuinely work, but her presence is a constant reminder of the sea's power and unpredictability. She's respected, feared, and consulted whenever something strange happens in the water.

The rumors about her past are endless: Some say she's the daughter of a water elemental and a human sailor. Others claim she drowned and was brought back by a sea goddess. She doesn't confirm or deny anything, simply smiling mysteriously when asked.

## Services & Wares

- **Nautical Magic Items:**
    - *Driftglobe* (Uncommon) - 150gp
    - *Cloak of the Manta Ray* (Uncommon) - 500gp
    - *Potion of Water Breathing* - 100gp (she always has 3-5 in stock)
    - *Sailor's Knots:* One-use magical tokens that cast *Feather Fall* or *Gust of Wind* - 50gp each
    - *Barnacle Compass:* Always points to the nearest safe harbor - 75gp
- **Identification Services:**
    - Can identify magical items, especially those with water or nautical origins - 25gp per item
    - Specializes in cursed items recovered from shipwrecks - 50gp per item
- **Divination:**
    - Reads the tides and waves for 10gp, providing cryptic but accurate guidance about sea travel
    - Can perform a water scrying ritual for 100gp (requires a personal item from the target)
- **Custom Charms:**
    - Creates personalized protection charms for sailors - 20-50gp depending on complexity
    - Specializes in anti-drowning and storm-protection magic

## Public Knowledge

- **Genasi Connection:** Marina immediately recognizes others with elemental heritage. She'll offer preferential treatment and information to **Claire** (Water Genasi Cleric).
- **The Tide-Kin:** Marina refers to water genasi as "tide-kin" and treats them like distant family. She might know about Claire's parents or the origin of her powers.
- **Respects Strength:** Marina admires those who face the sea's challenges with courage. **JP** (Sailor background) will earn her respect if he shows proper reverence for the ocean.
- **Hates Pirates:** She'll sell to anyone except pirates and slavers. Those who harm innocents at sea are her enemies.
- **Oracle of the Waves:** Her tide readings are uncannily accurate. Sailors pay for her divinations before major voyages.
- **Cursebreaker:** If you've recovered a cursed item from the sea, she can identify it and often remove the curse (for appropriate fees).
- **The Siren's Lament:** The ship her shop is built from has a dark history—it was a slaver ship that sank during a storm. Marina salvaged it as an act of symbolic justice.
- **Mysterious Past:** She never talks about where she came from or how she got her powers. Pressing the issue will get you politely but firmly shown the door.

## Party Connections

- **Claire (Water Genasi Tempest Cleric):** Marina will recognize Claire immediately as "tide-kin" and treat her with familial warmth. She might:
    - Offer information about other genasi in the region
    - Know stories about Claire's parents or lineage
    - Provide discounted services (10-15% off)
    - Warn Claire about the responsibilities that come with elemental heritage
    - Offer training or guidance on controlling water-based powers
- **JP (Half-Orc Sailor):** If JP shows respect for the sea and its dangers, Marina will respect him in turn. She judges sailors by their seamanship, not their race.
- **Randi (Aarakocra Wizard):** Marina is fascinated by air-aligned creatures and might trade information about elemental planes.

## Quest Hooks

- **The Drowned Whispers:** Marina has been hearing voices in the water lately—not natural, not divine. Something is stirring in the depths of Lake Arden.
- **Claire's Heritage:** Marina might have information about Claire's parents, or know of other water genasi who could provide answers about her powers.
- **The Cursed Cargo:** A recent shipwreck deposited cursed items in the harbor. Marina needs someone to retrieve them before they cause harm (or before rival salvagers sell them to unsuspecting buyers).
- **Elemental Unrest:** The water elementals in the region are becoming agitated. Marina suspects it's connected to the evil wizard's artifact hunt and needs adventurers to investigate.
- **The Harbormaster's Grudge:** Marina and Harbormaster Gregor Woods have a tense relationship. She knows something about the impoundment of JP's ship and might help—for a price or a favor.
