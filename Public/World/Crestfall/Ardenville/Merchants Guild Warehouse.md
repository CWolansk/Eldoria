## Description
Largest building in Ardenville — reinforced stone and heavy timber dominating the main pier. Constant activity: carts rolling, dock workers hauling crates, merchants arguing over manifests.

## Proprietor
[[Public/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/NPCs/Kellen Redshore|Kellen Redshore]] — harbormaster, runs a tight operation from a raised office overlooking the docks

## Services & Wares
- Secure cargo storage (fees by volume and duration)
- Shipping coordination to [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/Highreach|Highreach]]
- Customs processing and manifest verification
- Loading services and dock worker hire
- Guild membership (5 gp/month) for commercial use

## Reputation
The economic engine of Ardenville. Lost cargo is rare under Kellen's watch. Fair rates, no bribes, no favoritism — but every item must be documented.
