## Reputation

The Merchants Guild Warehouse is the economic engine of Ardenville. Every commercial shipment that enters or leaves the port passes through here, making it arguably more important to the town's prosperity than even the Fisherman's Guild. The warehouse handles everything from bulk grain shipments to rare luxury goods destined for Highreach's nobility.

Kellen Redshore is known for running a tight ship (literally and figuratively). Under his management, lost cargo is rare, schedules are maintained, and disputes are resolved fairly. The merchants who work with him appreciate his honesty and efficiency—rare qualities in the sometimes shady world of waterfront commerce.

## Services & Wares

- **Cargo Storage:** Secure storage for trade goods, from overnight to long-term. (Fees based on volume and duration)
- **Shipping Coordination:** Arranging cargo transport to Highreach and other destinations.
- **Customs Processing:** Official inspection and documentation for imported/exported goods.
- **Merchant Services:** Ledgers, contracts, and trade documentation assistance.
- **Guild Membership:** Applications and renewals for Merchants Guild membership.
- **Loading Services:** Dock workers available for hire to load/unload cargo.

## Public Knowledge

- **Guild Membership Required:** To use the warehouse for commercial purposes, you need to be a Merchants Guild member (or hire someone who is). Membership costs 5 gp per month.
- **Security:** The warehouse is well-guarded. Don't try to sneak in or steal. Kellen prosecutes thieves aggressively.
- **Fair Rates:** Storage and services are priced fairly according to guild standards. No bribes needed, no favoritism given.
- **Manifests Are Law:** Every item is documented. If your manifest doesn't match your cargo, expect delays and questions.
- **Highreach Route:** The warehouse coordinates the transport of goods to Highreach, managing logistics that individual merchants would struggle with.
- **Customs Cooperation:** Works closely with town officials to ensure legal trade. Smuggling through here is nearly impossible.
- **Lost Cargo Rare:** Kellen's meticulous organization means lost or damaged cargo is extremely rare. When it happens, the guild compensates fairly.
- **Harbormaster's Word:** Kellen settles disputes about cargo, contracts, and shipping matters. His decisions are respected even by those who disagree.
- **Employment Opportunities:** The warehouse regularly needs dock workers, clerks, and guards. Apply to Kellen if you're looking for honest work.
