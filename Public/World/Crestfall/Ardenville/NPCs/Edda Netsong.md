## Appearance
Stocky dwarf woman with rope-calloused hands. Seventy winters have weathered her face, but her eyes miss nothing. Fingers that never stop mending nets.

## What We Know
Guildmistress of the [[Public/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/Fishermans Guild|Fisherman's Guild]] for forty years. Champions the fisherfolk of Ardenville, provides weather forecasts with uncanny accuracy, and ensures no fisher's family goes hungry.

## First Impressions
Stubborn, sharp, and warm underneath — like the salt-crusted hull of a well-loved boat.
