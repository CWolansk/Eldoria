## Appearance
Large, heavily muscled bald Black human sailor with a spotless coat and a salt-stiff collar. Watches everything with the patience of someone counting mistakes.

## What We Know
Me'Shelle Jones works closely with [[Public/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/NPCs/Richard Vane|Richard Vane]] and the [[Public/World/Groups/Merchants Guild|Merchants Guild]] in Ardenville. He is known around the docks as a competitive organizer with real sailing knowledge.

## First Impressions
Calm, watchful, and ready to correct a mistake before anyone else names it.
