Broad-shouldered human sailor with the same square jaw, waxed mustache, and sharp sideburns as Saber and Caber. His bright guild-blue sash and flashier trim are the main things setting him apart.

## What We Know
Cutlass is part of a Merchants Guild-backed race crew in Ardenville. He favors loud confidence, polished gear, and showy deck work.

## First Impressions
Loud, polished, and eager to be seen as the most impressive sailor on the dock.
