## Appearance
Broad-shouldered human sailor with the same square jaw, waxed mustache, and sharp sideburns as Cutlass and Caber. His immaculate gloves and crisp navy-and-white trim are the main things setting him apart.

## What We Know
Saber sails with a Merchants Guild-backed race crew in Ardenville. He carries himself like a duelist, even when doing ordinary dock work.

## First Impressions
Smug and exacting; even ordinary dock work looks rehearsed when he does it.
