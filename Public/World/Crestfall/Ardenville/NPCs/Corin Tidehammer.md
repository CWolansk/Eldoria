## Appearance
Broad-shouldered, salt-crusted boots, brine-stained leather apron. Thick cracked hands that never stop moving. Always smells faintly of hot metal and seawater.

## What We Know
Ardenville's weapons smith, working out of a forge near the docks. Specializes in maritime metalwork — harpoons, boarding axes, gaffs, and nautical hardware. Also crafts specialty items like enchanted metal nets. Grew up on fishing boats before picking up a hammer. Competes for business alongside [[Public/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/Steelshapers Forge|Steelshaper's Forge]], though they focus on different trades.

## First Impressions
Blunt and practical. Respects people who work with their hands and know the sea.
