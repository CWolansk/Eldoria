## Appearance
Elderly gnome female with an intricate braided beard and traditional leather apron. Always smells faintly of bitter herbs and alchemical reagents.

## What We Know
Pip is a senior member of the Exterminators Guild and one of its most experienced alchemists. She specializes in pest deterrents and traditional extermination methods passed down through generations of her family.

## First Impressions
Methodical and no-nonsense — she sizes you up before deciding if you're worth her time.

## Interactions

The party met Pip at the [[Public/World/Groups/Exterminators Guild|Exterminators Guild]] in [[Public/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/Ardenville|Ardenville]].

Justin told Pip about a gnome outside the guild who had tried to swap packages with the party. Pip sighed and sent two large, muscular gnomes after the stranger.
