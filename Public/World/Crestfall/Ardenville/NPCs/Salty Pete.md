## Appearance
Weathered human with a sailor's permanent squint and salt-stiff clothing. Deep lines from twenty years on merchant vessels. Sea-steady balance.

## What We Know
Veteran sailor and boat captain in [[Public/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/Ardenville|Ardenville]]. Owns *The Barnacle's Revenge*, a well-maintained racing vessel. Member of the [[Public/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/Fishermans Guild|Fisherman's Guild]] in good standing. Currently looking for a crew for the upcoming boat race.

## First Impressions
Straightforward and practical. Judges people by their actions and their hands — calloused means trustworthy.
