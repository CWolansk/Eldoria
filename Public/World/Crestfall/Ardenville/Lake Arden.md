## Overview

Lake Arden is the lifeblood of the region, providing food for Ardenville and a natural defensive barrier for the capital of Highreach. Its depths are rumored to hold ancient secrets from before the kingdom's founding, though most locals know it simply as a rich fishing ground and a vital trade route.

## Key Landmarks

- **The Cliffside Base**
    - The rocky southern shore where the plateau's cliffs descend dramatically to meet the water.
- **Fisherman's Shoals**
    - Shallow waters near the northern bank teeming with the region's famous silver trout.
- **The Kelp Beds**
    - Golden strands of kelp swaying in the protected cove near Ardenville, a natural breakwater.
