## Reputation

Felix Tradewind built his shop from nothing fifteen years ago, and it's become the go-to source for adventuring supplies in Highreach. He's known for three things: knowing his inventory inside and out, stocking items that actually work, and being able to recommend exactly what you need for a specific job.

Unlike some merchants who try to oversell, Felix has a reputation for honesty. If you don't need something, he'll tell you. This has earned him tremendous loyalty from the adventuring community.

## Services & Wares

- **Camping Gear:** Tents (1-6 person), bedrolls, mess kits, water flasks, rations.
- **Tools:** Lockpicks, crowbars, spyglasses, whetstones, climber's kits, thieves' tools.
- **Exploration Supplies:** Lanterns, torches, oil, chalk, rope (hempen and silk), pitons, grappling hooks.
- **Specialized Equipment:** Signal whistles, manacles, bear traps, caltrops, magnifying glasses.
- **Trinkets & Oddities:** Mirrors, bells, marbles, perfume, sealing wax, and other useful small items.
- **Repair Services:** Can fix damaged gear for a small fee.

## Public Knowledge

- **Know-Before-You-Go:** Felix asks about your destination and recommends appropriate gear. Going to the mountains? He'll suggest cold weather equipment. Exploring ruins? He'll point out the importance of good light sources.
- **Quality Guarantee:** If something breaks under normal use, bring it back and he'll replace it at half price.
- **Bulk Discounts:** Outfitting a whole party? He'll work out a package deal.
- **Special Orders:** Need something unusual? Felix can order it through his merchant contacts (though with the current situation, this may take time).
- **Repair Service:** Basic repairs done same-day for a small fee. He's saved many adventurers from having to buy entirely new gear.
- **Guild Connections:** As a successful merchant, Felix has connections to the Merchants Guild and can occasionally help with paperwork or recommendations.
- **Practical Advice:** Free consulting on what you actually need vs. what's just nice to have. He won't let you overspend if you're on a budget.
