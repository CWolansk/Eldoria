## Description
The headquarters of the Exterminators Guild, located beneath Highreach's Noble District. Accessed through a grate near the Royal Gardens. Surprisingly clean for an underground pest control operation — smells of cheese and soap.

## Interior
A central meeting hall with rat-themed banners and a large round table. Equipment storage, a potion-brewing laboratory, and a tunnel command center with detailed sewer maps. Well-lit by magical rat-oil lamps.

## Key Areas
- **Meeting Hall** — guild business and contract discussions
- **Equipment Storage** — pest control gear and supplies
- **Laboratory** — potions and specialized pest control solutions
- **Tunnel Command** — sewer maps and route coordination

## Proprietor
[[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Don Gnocianno|Don Gnocianno]] — Guildmaster

## Services
- Pest extermination contracts
- Tunnel and sewer navigation assistance
- Specialized creature removal

## Reputation
An efficient if eccentric operation. The gnomes keep Highreach's underground pest-free, and they take clear pride in their work.
