## Description
Two formidable statues of armored warriors flank the entrance, gleaming swords pointed skyward. Large wooden doors reinforced with iron bands, carved with the crest of Crestfall — a shield with a roaring lion and crossed swords. A grand archway above features a stained glass window depicting the kingdom's most revered heroes.

## Interior
A vast open hall with high vaulted ceilings and massive stone columns. Polished marble floor reflects light from crystal-adorned chandeliers. A grand staircase splits into two, leading to the upper floors. Tall windows illuminate rich tapestries on the walls.

## Key Areas
- **Reception** — managed by [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Ingrid Hightower|Ingrid Hightower]]; large notice board with quest postings and announcements
- **Main Hall** — grand staircase, tapestries, natural light
- **Church** — non-denominational, run by [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Father Eldon Lightbringer|Father Eldon Lightbringer]]; warm lanterns, healing chamber for the weary

## Proprietor
[[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Thaldrin Stormblade|Thaldrin Stormblade]] — Guildmaster

## Services
- Contract board with bounties, escort jobs, and monster hunts
- Training grounds for guild members
- Non-denominational church with healing services

## Reputation
An imposing landmark in Highreach. Trusted by the city guard and merchants alike.
