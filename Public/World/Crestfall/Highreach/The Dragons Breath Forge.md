## Reputation

Alaric Emberfell is a legend among smiths. Trained in the deepest forges of Ironpeak Mountain and rumored to have learned secrets from a dragon (hence the shop's name), he creates weapons and armor that noble houses pay fortunes to acquire. His ability to work with magical materials and create enchantment-receptive items is unmatched in the human kingdom.

He's known for being selective about his clients—he won't craft weapons for those he believes will misuse them. Money alone doesn't buy his services; he needs to respect the buyer and their purpose. For those he deems worthy, however, he'll create masterpieces.

## Services & Wares

- **Magical Weapons:** +1 to +3 enchanted weapons of all types. Specialized abilities available by commission.
- **Magical Armor:** Enchanted armor providing both protection and special properties (resistance, stealth, etc.).
- **Masterwork Items:** Non-magical but perfectly crafted weapons and armor (+1 to hit/AC without enchantment).
- **Rare Materials:** Can work with adamantine, mithril, dragon scales, and other exotic materials.
- **Custom Commissions:** Will design and create bespoke items for worthy clients (months of work, very expensive).
- **Repairs:** Can repair magical items that other smiths can't touch.
- **Appraisal:** Expert evaluation of weapons and armor, magical or mundane.

## Current Inventory

*Limited selection of finished pieces:*
- 2-3 magical weapons (rotating stock, prices from 500-5000 gp)
- 1-2 suits of magical armor (prices from 1000-10000 gp)
- Masterwork weapons and armor (200-500 gp)
- Specialized ammunition (silvered, adamantine, etc.)

## Public Knowledge

- **Elite Pricing:** Alaric's work costs 10-20 times what you'd pay elsewhere. You're buying the best, and it shows in the price.
- **Reputation Matters:** He investigates potential buyers. If you're known for dishonorable conduct, your gold is no good here.
- **Commission Wait Times:** Custom work takes 3-12 months depending on complexity. There's a waiting list. Important clients (nobles, heroes) get priority.
- **Perfect Quality:** Every piece leaves his forge perfect. If there's even a minor flaw, he melts it down and starts over.
- **Dragon Secrets:** Rumor says Alaric learned his trade from a dragon in his youth. He neither confirms nor denies this, but his forge burns hotter than any dwarf should be able to manage.
- **Material Procurement:** Bring him rare materials (dragon scales, meteorite iron, etc.) and he'll work with them—for the right client.
- **Heroes Welcome:** Despite his intimidating reputation, Alaric has a soft spot for genuine heroes. If you've saved lives or fought great evil, he's more likely to take your commission.
- **Magical Expertise:** While not a wizard, his understanding of how magic interacts with metal is profound. He consults with The Arcane Enclave on complex projects.
- **No Weapons of Evil:** Won't create cursed items, weapons meant for assassination, or anything designed for cruelty. His forge serves honor, not darkness.
