## Description
Modest two-story stone building on a quiet street in the Merchant District. Clean lines, quality stonework, dark wooden trim. Sturdy oak door with polished brass handles. A small plaque bears the guild emblem: a pair of scales overlaid with crossed keys.

## Interior
Warmly lit entry hall with plush chairs and a polished oak counter. Walls adorned with paintings of past guildmasters. Rich dark carpeting mutes footsteps. Tapestries depict prosperous markets and distant lands.

## Key Areas
- **Reception** — managed by [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Evelyn Marroway|Evelyn Marroway]]
- **Main Meeting Room** — polished table, leather chairs, trade documents
- **Members' Lounge** — tea and brandy, quiet business talk

## Proprietor
[[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Alistair Goldman|Alistair Goldman]] — Guildmaster

## Services
- Trade permits and merchant licensing
- Market stall and warehouse access
- Merchant dispute mediation

## Reputation
Understated elegance reflecting financial strength. A powerful institution — membership is practically required for serious commerce.
