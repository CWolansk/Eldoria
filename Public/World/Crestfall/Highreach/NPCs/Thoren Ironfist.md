## Description
A burly man with a broad chest and dark slicked-back hair. Wears a dark steel breastplate etched with an iron gauntlet emblem over his heart and a crimson half-cloak draped over one shoulder. Wields iron-knuckled gauntlets in combat.

## What We Know
Thoren Ironfist is the leader of [[The Nightstalkers]], another adventuring party based out of the [[Sturdy Mugg]]. He appears to be a capable fighter and leader, commanding respect from his party members.

He's married to [[Alma Ironfist nee Brightwater]].
