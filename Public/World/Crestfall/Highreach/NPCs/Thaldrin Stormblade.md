## Description
Tall and muscular with the build of a career warrior. His face bears numerous scars that tell stories of countless battles, and dark hair frames his commanding presence.

## What We Know
Thaldrin Stormblade is the Guildmaster of the [[Highreach Adventurers Guild]], one of the most prestigious positions for an adventurer in the city. He oversees all guild operations.

He appears to be a natural leader with strategic thinking, commanding respect through both his accomplishments and his ability to inspire others. When he enters a room, people naturally pay attention.

Those who've worked with him say he's fair but expects professionalism from guild members. He seems to evaluate adventurers based on results and their ability to handle challenges.

## Interactions
**First Met**: At the [[Highreach Adventurers Guild]]

**Relationship Status**: Neutral - professional guild relationship

**Notes**:
- Commands the Adventurers Guild and all its resources
- Oversees [[Ingrid Hightower]] and other guild staff
- Rose through the ranks through combat skill and leadership
- Can provide high-level quests and contracts
- Has extensive knowledge of threats facing Highreach and the region
- The scars on his face are from notable battles
