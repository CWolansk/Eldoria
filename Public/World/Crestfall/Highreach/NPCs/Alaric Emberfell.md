## Appearance
Stocky, robust build with a well-groomed beard and strong arms. Piercing eyes that size up materials — and customers — with equal scrutiny.

## What We Know
Owner of [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/The Dragons Breath Forge|The Dragon's Breath Forge]] in Highreach. Renowned for crafting rare and very rare magical weapons and armor. Expert in metallurgy and enchantments — the blacksmith the elite trust.

## First Impressions
Proud and meticulous. Speaks with a lisp but carries himself with total confidence.
