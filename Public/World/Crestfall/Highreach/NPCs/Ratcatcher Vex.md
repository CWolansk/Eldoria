## Description
A human male who runs an independent pest control operation.

## What We Know
Ratcatcher Vex runs an unlicensed pest control service that competes with the [[Exterminators Guild]]. Word on the street is that he was once a guild member but left under unclear circumstances.

He undercuts guild prices and seems to have knowledge of the city's tunnel systems. Those who've dealt with him say he's bitter about something and willing to work outside official channels.

Some rumors suggest he's involved in more than just pest control, possibly using his tunnel access for less legitimate purposes.
