## Appearance
Stocky, weathered man with calloused hands and a perpetual scowl. Looked like someone who built his way up from nothing.

## What We Know
A merchant in the [[Public/World/Groups/Merchants Guild|Merchants Guild]] who had a dispute with Guildmaster [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Alistair Goldman|Alistair Goldman]]. The party spoke with him during a guild quest — he claimed Goldman was corrupt. Eventually went to the authorities with evidence after Julie convinced him.

## First Impressions
Guarded and suspicious — took a lot of convincing before he'd trust strangers.
