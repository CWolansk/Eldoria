## Description
A large, imposing man typically dressed in fine riding leathers that display wealth without being ostentatiously flashy.

## What We Know
Lord Frederick Goldman is a wealthy nobleman living in Highreach's rich district. His wealth appears to come from both family inheritance and what he claims are "smart investments."

He's particularly proud of his extensive wine collection, especially vintages from Hillcrest. Those who've interacted with him say he can discourse at length about wine vintages and judges others partly by their wine knowledge.
