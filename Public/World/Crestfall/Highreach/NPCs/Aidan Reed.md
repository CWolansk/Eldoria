## Description
A towering, barrel-chested man with thick, muscular arms and a round, rosy face framed by a dark, bushy beard. His eyes twinkle with genuine mirth, giving him an almost Santa-like appearance.

## What We Know
Aidan Reed is the barkeep at the [[Sturdy Mugg]], one of Highreach's most popular taverns. He's known for his friendly demeanor and excellent brewing skills - his rosy cheeks are said to come from years of tasting his own brews.

He seems to know most of the regulars by name and their preferred drinks. When you walk through the door, he's quick to flash a wide grin and make you feel welcome. Like most good barkeeps, he hears all the local gossip and rumors but seems to know when to share information and when to keep quiet.
