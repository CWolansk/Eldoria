## Description
A powerfully built dwarf with a fiery red beard and a booming voice. Scars from past magical experiments mark his hands and forearms.

## What We Know
Professor Flamehand teaches Evocation magic (destructive spells) at [[The Arcane Enclave|Wizards Tower]]. He embodies the raw power of evocation and believes in hands-on learning, often encouraging students to push their limits.

His passion for the destructive forces of magic is matched by his dedication to ensuring students learn control and responsibility. His bold approach to teaching is a testament to his experience.
