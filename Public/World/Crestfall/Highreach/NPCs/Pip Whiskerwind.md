## Description
An elderly gnome with an intricate braided beard, usually wearing a traditional leather apron.

## What We Know
Pip Whiskerwind works with the [[ExterminatorsGuild]] and appears to be an expert in pest control. She comes across as methodical and traditional in her approach, and her workshop seems well-equipped for dealing with various pest problems.

She's known to be Don Gnocianno's predecessor's daughter and seems to be respected within the guild. Those who've worked with her say she's competent at creating pest deterrents and handling difficult extermination jobs.
