## Description
Elegant and poised with an aristocratic bearing, blonde hair often styled in intricate braids, and sharp, discerning eyes that seem to miss nothing in social situations.

## What We Know
Dame Isolde Swiftwind serves as the kingdom's Ambassador and chief diplomat, handling foreign relations for [[King Alaric Stormhelm]]. She appears to be the face of the kingdom in international matters.

She comes across as supremely skilled in diplomacy and negotiation, able to navigate complex political situations with grace. Her sharp eyes constantly evaluate every word and gesture, and she seems to know when to be firm and when to compromise. Even when genuinely surprised, she never shows it - her diplomatic composure is nearly unshakeable.

Those who've observed her note that she styles her braids differently for different occasions, and she's fluent in multiple languages.
