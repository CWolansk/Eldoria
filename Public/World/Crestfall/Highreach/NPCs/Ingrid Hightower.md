## Description
A younger human woman with a short blonde pixie cut that gives her a practical, no-nonsense appearance while remaining approachable.

## What We Know
Ingrid Hightower is the receptionist at the [[Highreach Adventurers Guild]], serving as the primary contact for adventurers seeking quests and managing guild business.

She appears professional, organized, and efficient, handling the constant flow of adventurers with practiced ease while staying friendly. Those who've worked with her say she can quickly assess party capabilities for appropriate quest levels.

## Interactions
