## Description
Tall and regal with a strong, commanding build, silver hair, and piercing blue eyes that seem to evaluate the true worth of those before him. Carries himself with the bearing of both warrior and diplomat.

## What We Know
King Alaric Stormhelm is the ruler of Crestfall Kingdom, governing from Highreach. He has a reputation for both martial prowess and fair governance, known to balance military strength with diplomatic wisdom.

He is said to judge people by their deeds rather than their station or appearance - a rare quality among nobility. Despite his advisors' concerns, he's known to personally lead troops in major battles when the stakes are high enough. His piercing blue eyes seem to see through deception.
