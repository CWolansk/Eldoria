## Description
A sturdy dwarf woman whose expression can shift from welcoming to intimidating with just a look.

## What We Know
Kara Stonefoot owns and operates the [[Sturdy Mugg]] Inn where the party is currently staying. She runs a tight ship with practiced efficiency.

She's known for being no-nonsense and firm but fair. She doesn't tolerate troublemakers but treats respectful patrons well. Her quick glare and firm hand keep even rowdy adventurers in line. Those who know her say beneath the stern exterior is someone who takes real pride in her establishment.

## Interactions
**First Met**: When the party checked into the [[Sturdy Mugg]]

**Relationship Status**: Friendly - the party are well-behaved guests

**Notes**:
- Owns and runs the Sturdy Mugg Inn
- Works alongside [[Aidan Reed]] (he runs the bar, she manages the inn)
- Provides clean rooms, good food, and maintains strict order
- Hears much of the local gossip through her inn
- Knows most regular patrons and their business
- Can be a source of local information and recommendations
- Can silence a room with just a look
