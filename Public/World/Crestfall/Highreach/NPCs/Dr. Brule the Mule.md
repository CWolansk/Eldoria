## Description
A sturdy mule wearing a distinctive brass bell charm. Well-cared for and strong enough to carry significant loads across long distances.

## What We Know
Dr. Brule is Justin's loyal pack mule. He's known for being both stubborn (in the way only a mule can be) and remarkably dependable.

Those who've traveled with him say he shows surprising intelligence and independence, sometimes acting on his own initiative. He's proven himself more clever than a typical mule - he seems to sense danger and has bolted to safety carrying important cargo.

The brass bell charm jingles when he moves, making him easy to locate.

## Interactions
**Relationship**: Justin's faithful companion and pack animal

**Status**: Ally - travels with the party

**Notes**:
- Stubborn but ultimately reliable when it matters
- Shows surprising intelligence for a mule
- Once bolted from Highreach toward Ardenville during a critical moment
- Carries Justin's merchant goods and apparently knows the routes
- The name "Dr. Brule" suggests Justin's humor and affection
- Could potentially be targeted by those seeking revenge on the party
