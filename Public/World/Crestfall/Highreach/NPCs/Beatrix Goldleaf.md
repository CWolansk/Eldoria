## Description
A woman in her late 30s with warm honey-toned skin and a cascade of thick dark blonde curls, usually wearing deep emerald green gowns with gold embroidery and expensive jewelry that's always coordinated with her outfit.

## What We Know
Beatrix Goldleaf is a wealthy noblewoman living in Highreach's rich district, known for her extensive jewelry collection. She appears passionate about her gems and precious metals - some might say to the point of obsession.

She favors emeralds and gold, often wearing gold leaf-shaped earrings and a delicate bracelet set with small emeralds and diamonds. Those who've spoken with her say she knows the story behind each piece in her collection and becomes visibly distressed at the thought of losing any of them.
