## Description
A mysterious and pale human woman with long black hair that seems to absorb light. She dresses in dark, elegant robes trimmed with silver, and her eyes have a piercing, almost otherworldly quality.

## What We Know
Seraphine teaches Necromancy at [[The Arcane Enclave|Wizards Tower]]. Though her specialty often inspires fear, she appears to be a compassionate figure who emphasizes the ethical use of necromantic magic.

Those who've studied with her say she's soft-spoken but possesses fierce intellect and deep empathy for those seeking to understand the mysteries of life and death. She teaches students to respect both life and death.
