## Description
A half-elf woman with graceful features, typically dressed in neat administrative attire appropriate for her receptionist position at the [[The Arcane Enclave|Wizards Tower]].

## What We Know
Samantha Clerkson is the receptionist at the [[The Arcane Enclave|Wizards Tower]], serving as the first point of contact for visitors.

She comes across as pleasant, friendly, and eager to help, though she seems somewhat absent-minded and occasionally gets confused with appointments or names. Her cheerful demeanor and profuse apologies when flustered make her quite likeable despite her organizational challenges.
