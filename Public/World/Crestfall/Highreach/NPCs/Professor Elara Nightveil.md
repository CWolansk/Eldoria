## Description
A tall, slender elf with deep blue eyes and a calm, soothing presence. Her long silver hair is always impeccably braided, and she wears flowing robes adorned with protective runes.

## What We Know
Professor Nightveil teaches Abjuration magic (defensive spells and wards) at [[The Arcane Enclave|Wizards Tower]]. She's known for her patience and wisdom.

Despite her serene demeanor, she has a reputation for being fiercely protective of her students and standing against any threat to the tower.
