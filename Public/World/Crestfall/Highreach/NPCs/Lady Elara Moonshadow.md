## Description
Graceful and slender with long, flowing black hair that seems to move as if touched by unseen winds. Most striking are her glowing violet eyes, which hint at profound arcane power.

## What We Know
Lady Elara Moonshadow serves as the High Mage and Keeper of Arcane Knowledge at [[The Arcane Enclave]]. She's also a member of the royal council, advising [[King Alaric Stormhelm]] on matters of magic.

She comes across as highly intelligent and deeply knowledgeable in the arcane arts, speaking with careful precision. Her glowing violet eyes seem to intensify when she uses powerful magic, and she appears to see layers of reality invisible to others.

Those who've studied with her say she often references obscure magical texts and theories, and she has a practical understanding of how magic serves the kingdom best when applied wisely.
