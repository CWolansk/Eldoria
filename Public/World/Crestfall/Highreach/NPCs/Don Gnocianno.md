## Description
A flamboyant gnome with a distinctive rat-tail beard who favors pinstripe vests. His appearance walks the line between eccentric businessman and subtle old-fashioned aesthetic.

## What We Know
Don Gnocianno is the guildmaster of the [[Exterminators Guild]]. He presents himself as an eccentric and inclusive guild leader who runs a pest control business.

He comes across as outwardly flamboyant and charismatic, with a welcoming attitude toward new members. He seems to see business opportunities everywhere and runs the guild with an interesting combination of showmanship and sharp business sense.

The guild provides pest control services throughout Highreach, which gives them access to many buildings and establishments across the city.
