## Description
A petite woman with dark hair typically worn in a practical ponytail. She wears a faded dark crimson tunic and a small amulet bearing an iron gauntlet insignia.

## What We Know
Alma Ironfist (née Brightwater) is a skilled cartographer and member of [[The Nightstalkers]] adventuring party. She's married to [[Thoren Ironfist]], the party's leader.

She appears practical and devoted to both her craft and her companions. Her cartography skills seem invaluable - those who've seen her work say her maps are both beautiful and exceptionally accurate.

She's grateful to the party for rescuing Thoren and the others from the [[Highreach Mines]].

## Interactions
**First Met**: At the [[Sturdy Mugg]] after the rescue

**Relationship Status**: Friendly - grateful for the party's rescue of her husband

**Notes**:
- Exceptional cartographer who creates high-quality maps
- Regular patron at the Sturdy Mugg with her husband
- Her maps could be valuable resources for the party's travels
- Might commission exploration and mapping of unknown areas
- The iron gauntlet amulet represents her connection to the Ironfist family and the Nightstalkers
