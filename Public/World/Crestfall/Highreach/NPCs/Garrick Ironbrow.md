## Description
A large, muscle-bound man with a completely bald head and imposing size. Always seen wearing practical combat gear with a greatsword on his back.

## What We Know
Garrick Ironbrow serves as the personal bodyguard and enforcer for [[Alistair Goldman]], Guildmaster of the Merchants Guild. His presence alone is intimidating, and he rarely speaks unnecessarily.

He appears tough and professional, constantly positioning himself to protect Alistair. Those from Stonehaven recognize the harsh edge that comes from growing up in that unforgiving settler town.
