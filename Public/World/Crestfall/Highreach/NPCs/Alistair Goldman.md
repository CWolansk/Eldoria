## Description
A wiry man in his early 50s with graying hair slicked back meticulously. His sharp, calculating blue eyes seem to miss nothing. He wears finely tailored clothes in muted colors that suggest wealth without ostentation.

## What We Know
Alistair Goldman is the Guildmaster of the [[HighReach Merchants Guild]] and one of the most powerful economic figures in Highreach. He appears to have built his position through calculated business dealings.

He comes across as formal and controlling, rarely making direct eye contact and maintaining a detached demeanor in all interactions. He has a reputation for not forgiving slights easily and remembering those who cross him. When thinking, he tends to tap his fingers rhythmically and frequently adjusts his collar and cuffs.
