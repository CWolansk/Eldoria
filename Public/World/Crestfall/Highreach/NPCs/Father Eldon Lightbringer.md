## What We Know
Father Eldon Lightbringer is the High Priest of Pelor at the Adventurers Guild Church. He comes across as genuinely warm and compassionate, treating everyone with kindness regardless of their station or background.

Those who know him say he's an early riser who greets each dawn as a gift from Pelor, and he views physical labor as sacred as prayer.
