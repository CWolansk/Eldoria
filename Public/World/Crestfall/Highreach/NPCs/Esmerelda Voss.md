## Appearance
Silver-haired woman in her late 50s, still sharp-eyed and steady-handed. Wears simple clothes but carries herself like someone who's seen the worst the world has to offer and walked away from it.

## What We Know
A retired adventurer living quietly in Highreach with her cat. Whatever she did before retirement, she did well — she gave the party Daern's Instant Fortress as a reward for rescuing her pet, the kind of item most adventurers never see in a lifetime.

## First Impressions
Warm but guarded. Generous in a way that suggests she has plenty more where that came from.
