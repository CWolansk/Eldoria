## Description
A kindly older gentleman with twinkling, knowing eyes and a warm shawl. His gentle elderliness puts people at ease, though his eyes suggest wisdom and hidden depths.

## What We Know
Elder Rowan appears to be a simple kindly old man, but there seems to be more to him than meets the eye. He has a way of appearing at seemingly chance moments and observing people carefully.

He's mentioned something called "the Foundation" and has given plain rock badges to those who aided civilians. He seems to value selfless acts and the protection of innocents above all else.

Those who've spoken with him say his twinkling eyes seem to see through pretense, and he tests people through their actions rather than their words.
