## Description
An enigmatic half-elf with an air of sophistication and subtle charm. His dark hair is always perfectly groomed, and he favors finely tailored robes adorned with delicate patterns that shimmer under the right light.

## What We Know
Mordecai teaches Enchantment magic at [[The Arcane Enclave|Wizards Tower]]. He's known for his silver tongue and captivating presence.

Many students claim that even his lectures feel like a spell, drawing them in and leaving them eager to learn more. He emphasizes the importance of subtlety and finesse in enchantment and persuasion.
