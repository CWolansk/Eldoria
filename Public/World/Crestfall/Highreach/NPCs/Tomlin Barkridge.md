## Description
A lean, wiry man with sandy blond hair and a patchy beard. He wore deep forest green armor etched with thorn-like engravings, with a bow resting on his back.

## What We Know
Tomlin Barkridge was a member of [[The Nightstalkers]] adventuring party, married to [[Brenna Barkridge nee Ironfoot]]. He was known as a skilled ranger and archer.

He perished in the [[Highreach Mines]] before the party could reach [[The Nightstalkers]]. His loss was a tragedy for his wife Brenna and his companions.

**In Memoriam**: Tomlin was a skilled ranger who preferred to strike from range. He was devoted to his wife Brenna and his companions in the Nightstalkers.
