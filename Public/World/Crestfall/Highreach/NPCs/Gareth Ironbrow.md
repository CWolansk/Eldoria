## Description
A large, muscle-bound bald man with a large tribal tattoo covering his left arm. He carried a greataxe on his back.

## What We Know
Gareth Ironbrow worked as muscle for the [[HighReach Merchants Guild]], doing the dirty work that included shakedowns and dealing with "problem merchants."

He was known as a natural fighter and was under the employ of [[Alistair Goldman]].
