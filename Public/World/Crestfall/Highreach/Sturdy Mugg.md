## Reputation

The Sturdy Mugg is THE gathering place for adventurers in Highreach. Kara has run it for thirty years, and in that time it's become legendary for three things: the quality of the ale, the size of the portions, and the fact that it's where you go to hear about jobs, form parties, and swap tales of adventure. More dungeon-crawling parties have been formed over Kara's tables than anywhere else in the kingdom.

Kara herself is known for her no-nonsense attitude, her surprising strength (she's personally thrown out troublemakers twice her size), and her soft spot for young adventurers just starting out.

## Services & Wares

- **Lodging:** Common room (5 sp/night) and private rooms (2 gp/night). Clean, secure, and warm.
- **Meals:** Hearty fare—meat pies, stews, roasted joints, fresh bread. Portions are generous. (3 cp to 5 sp depending on fanciness)
- **Drinks:** Wide selection of ales, meads, and wines. Kara's house ale is particularly renowned. (2 cp to 5 sp per mug)
- **Entertainment:** Aidan Reed performs most evenings. Other traveling bards and entertainers often stop by.
- **Message Board:** Unofficial job postings, party-seeking notices, and warnings about dangerous areas.

## Current Services

*Always available:*
- Room and board
- Hot meals from dawn till midnight
- Information about local adventuring opportunities
- Safe storage for gear (for paying guests)
- Aidan's performances (evenings, free with drink purchase)

## Public Knowledge

- **The Chair Rule:** Break furniture during a fight? You buy a replacement. Kara's serious about this. The posted rule isn't a joke.
- **Adventurer Friendly:** If you're just starting out and broke, Kara's been known to extend credit or throw in an extra meal. She remembers being young and hungry herself.
- **Information Hub:** Spend an evening here and you'll hear about every active quest, dangerous area, and available job in the region.
- **Party Formation:** Looking for a group? Post a notice on the board or just sit at the bar and talk to Kara. She's an expert matchmaker for adventuring parties.
- **Aidan's Knowledge:** The bard Aidan Reed knows every song, legend, and rumor in the kingdom. Buy him a drink and he'll share.
- **Loyalty Program:** Regular customers get their tenth night free. Kara keeps track in her head—don't try to cheat her.
- **Safe Haven:** Kara doesn't allow the city guard to arrest people inside her establishment without proof of serious crimes. It's neutral ground.
