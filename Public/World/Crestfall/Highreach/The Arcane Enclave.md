## Reputation

The Arcane Enclave is the premier source of magical goods in Highreach, and Magistrix Velanna is considered one of the most knowledgeable mages in the human kingdom. While the Wizard's Tower focuses on education and research, the Enclave serves the practical needs of spellcasters—scrolls for adventuring wizards, components for rituals, and consultations on magical mysteries.

Velanna is known for being brilliant, exacting, and expensive. She doesn't suffer fools, but she respects genuine seekers of knowledge regardless of their experience level. Her consultations have solved magical problems that stumped entire councils of wizards.

## Services & Wares

- **Spell Scrolls:** Common to rare spell scrolls, with emphasis on utility and combat magic. Custom transcription available.
- **Spellbooks:** Blank spellbooks, pre-filled beginner tomes, and occasionally rare grimoires.
- **Magical Components:** Comprehensive stock of material components, from basic bat guano to powdered diamond.
- **Enchanted Items:** Rotating selection of common and uncommon magical items. Rare items by special order only.
- **Potions:** Healing potions, spell enhancement elixirs, and specialized concoctions.
- **Arcane Consultation:** Magical identification, curse analysis, ritual design, and problem-solving.
- **Scroll Transcription:** Can copy spells into your spellbook or onto scrolls (fees apply).

## Current Inventory

*Stock rotates but typically includes:*
- 5-10 spell scrolls (levels 1-3, occasionally higher)
- Material components for most common spells
- 2-3 enchanted items (wands, rings, cloaks, etc.)
- Healing and utility potions
- Spellcasting focuses and implements

## Public Knowledge

- **Premium Pricing:** The Enclave is expensive. You're paying for quality, expertise, and Velanna's reputation. 
- **Identification Services:** 50gp per item for magical identification, with detailed analysis of properties, history, and potential risks.
- **Consultation Fees:** 100gp per hour for arcane consultation. Complex problems may require multiple sessions.
- **Respect Required:** Velanna has little patience for time-wasters. Come prepared with specific questions.
- **Academic Connections:** She has ties to the Wizard's Tower and can arrange introductions to researchers or specialists.
- **Custom Enchantments:** Velanna can enchant items, but it's prohibitively expensive for most adventurers (thousands of gold). She works with the nobility more often than common folk.
- **Rare Item Acquisition:** Given time and sufficient payment, she can locate and acquire rare magical items through her network.
- **No Dark Magic:** She won't deal in necromancy, demon summoning, or items designed purely for evil. Don't insult her by asking.
- **Spellcaster Discounts:** Wizards Tower graduates get 10% off scrolls and components—professional courtesy.
