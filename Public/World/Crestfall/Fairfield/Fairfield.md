**Town in the Kingdom of Crestfall**
- **Location:** East of Highreach, along the trade road toward Silverleaf Lands
- **Government:** Town Council led by Mayor Olin Fairchild
- **Population:** ~2,000 (Mostly Human, some Halflings)
- **Defenses:** Town militia, wooden palisade
- **Primary Export:** Grain, wool, ale

Banner
- A golden wheat sheaf on green field

```
Fairfield sits astride the main trade road between Highreach and the Silverleaf Lands, its prosperity evident in well-maintained stone buildings and bustling market square. The town earned its name from the broad agricultural plains that surround it, and serves as a crucial waystation for merchants traveling between the human and elven kingdoms.

The air smells of fresh bread and hay, with the constant background sound of commerce—wagons creaking, merchants haggling, and the rhythmic clang from the smithy. The town is peaceful, prosperous, and deeply superstitious about weather and harvests.
```

## Layout

Fairfield stretches along both sides of the main trade road, its market square serving as the commercial heart. Well-maintained stone buildings line the central streets, with wooden structures in the residential areas. The town is surrounded by a wooden palisade with gates at cardinal directions.

**Key Areas:**
- **Market Square**
	- Central hub with permanent merchant stalls and weekly market days
	- Town hall overlooks the square
	- Home to Bramblefoot's Exchange
- **The Golden Sheaf (Inn & Tavern)**
	- Massive tavern serving as unofficial town hall
	- Famous stew, cheap ale, straw beds
	- Community gathering place
- **Grain District**
	- Massive granaries and silos (recently targeted by goblin raids)
	- Mills powered by the nearby creek
- **Residential Quarter**
	- Comfortable homes for merchants and craftspeople with well-maintained gardens
	- Halfling neighborhood known for excellent baking

## Establishments & Services

### Bramblefoot's Exchange
**Type:** General Store, Trading Post & Basic Smithy  
**Proprietor:** [[Barnaby Bramblefoot]] (Halfling, retired Quartermaster)  
**Location:** Market Square, massive converted barn

```
The largest building in Fairfield's market square is a converted barn that serves as the town's primary trading post. Inside, the space is organized chaos—barrels of nails alongside crates of rations, farming tools hung next to travelers' gear, and the smell of fresh bread mixing with leather and oil.

A stone forge occupies one corner, sending warmth throughout the building. The proprietor, a round-bellied Halfling with a magnificent mustache, can usually be found either at the counter tallying accounts or at the anvil fixing a plow blade.
```

**Reputation:**  
Barnaby Bramblefoot served twenty years as a quartermaster for a mercenary company before retiring to "somewhere boring." He chose Fairfield for its peace and stayed for its people. He's seen the world, doesn't judge by race or appearance, and has stories that rival any bard's repertoire.

**Services & Wares:**
- **General Supplies:** 
    - Travel rations (Halfling-made, high quality) - 5sp per day
    - Rope, torches, bedrolls, and adventuring gear
    - Farming implements and bulk goods
- **Weapons & Armor:**
    - Simple and martial weapons (basic quality)
    - Light and medium armor (no heavy plate)
    - Weapon and tool repairs (1-2 days, reasonable prices)
- **"Barnaby's Specials":**
    - *Barnaby's "Magic" Salve:* Just honey and herbs, but heals 1d4 HP. He'll wink when he sells it. 10gp.
    - Bulk discounts for organizations or large orders
    - Will accept trade goods instead of coin from farmers

**Party Connections:**
- **JP (Half-Orc Monk):** Barnaby doesn't care about tusks or green skin. He's worked with orcs, half-orcs, and stranger things. He treats everyone with the same gruff respect.
- **Justin (Guild Merchant):** Barnaby respects the Merchants Guild and offers professional courtesy to members.
- **Liz (Bard):** He loves swapping stories and will offer free drinks to anyone who entertains the evening crowd.

**Public Knowledge:**
- **No Prejudice:** Barnaby judges customers by their coin and character, nothing else.
- **Veteran's Wisdom:** Has practical advice about the road to Silverleaf Lands and the dangers between here and there.
- **Credit for Locals:** Will extend credit to farmers and townspeople, payable after harvest.
- **Story Time:** Buy him a pint and he'll regale you with tales from his mercenary days.

### The Golden Sheaf
**Type:** Inn & Tavern  
**Proprietor:** Marta Goldwheat (Human)  
**Location:** Town Square, adjacent to the market

The largest tavern in Fairfield, serving as the unofficial town hall. The ale is cheap, the beds are straw, but the stew is famous across the kingdom. This is where news spreads, deals are made, and travelers rest.

**Services:**
- Room and board (2sp per night)
- Marta's Famous Stew (always simmering, always delicious)
- Local ale and Halfling cider
- Gossip and rumors (free with purchase)

## Key NPCs

- **Mayor Olin Fairchild:** A nervous man in his fifties, worried about recent goblin raids on the grain silos and the impact on this year's harvest. Desperately needs adventurers' help.
- **Barnaby Bramblefoot:** Proprietor of the Exchange, retired quartermaster, has seen the world.
- **Marta Goldwheat:** Owner of The Golden Sheaf, knows everyone's business, mother figure to the town.
- **Old Man Ricketts:** Village eccentric who claims to have seen a "Dragon made of Sand" in his youth. (Foreshadows the **Orcish Wastes** and **Sand Dragon Valley**).
- **Sister Amelie:** Local priestess of the harvest goddess, leads weather prayers and crop blessings. Would be interested in **Claire's** divine connection.

## Quest Hooks

- **The Grain Rot:** A magical blight is affecting the crops in specific fields. **Justin** (Guild Merchant) would recognize this as potential economic sabotage. Investigation reveals fungal spores with faint necromantic signatures.
- **Goblin Raids:** The recent attacks on grain silos aren't random—they're coordinated and suspiciously well-informed. Someone is feeding the goblins information.
- **The Missing Sheep:** Sounds like a simple retrieval job, but actually involves a young Wyvern that's learning to hunt. Could be scared off or befriended.
- **Weather Worries:** The farmers are superstitious about a "dry season curse." **Claire** could investigate and discover it's natural—or something more sinister affecting the region's weather patterns.
