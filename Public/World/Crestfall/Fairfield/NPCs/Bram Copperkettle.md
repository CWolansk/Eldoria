## Appearance
Stocky, weathered human with a patchy beard and ink-stained fingers. Wears a coat with too many pockets. His cart is a chaos of crates, bundled tools, and canvas-wrapped weapons with hand-scrawled price tags.

## What We Know
A traveling peddler who sets up camp just outside [[Public/World/Crestfall/Fairfield/Fairfield|Fairfield]]'s customs gate, selling what he calls "genuine dwarven surplus." Most of it is not. Loud about his prices, quiet about his suppliers.

## First Impressions
Every sentence sounds like the opening of a sales pitch.
