## Overview
> The human kingdom of Crestfall stretches across fertile plains and forested hills, anchored by the capital city of Highreach. A prosperous realm known for its strong military and bustling trade, though war with the orc tribes and recent crises test its stability.

## Geography
- Temperate plains and rolling hills, bounded by Blackwood Forest to the south and the Silverleaf border to the west
- Major trade roads connect all five settlements
- The Highreach Mines beneath the capital — recently collapsed
