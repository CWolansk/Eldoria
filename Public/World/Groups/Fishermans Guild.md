## Overview
The oldest organization in Ardenville. Helps fishermen survive the dangerous work of harvesting Lake Arden, providing equipment, weather forecasts, and mutual aid when the catch is poor.

## Reputation
Respected by locals as the backbone of Ardenville. Edda Netsong's weather predictions have saved countless lives — if she says a storm is coming, believe her.

## Known Members
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/NPCs/Edda Netsong|Edda Netsong]] — Guildmistress, 40-year veteran
- [[Public/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/NPCs/Salty Pete|Salty Pete]] — experienced sailor and guild member

## Services
- Guild membership (10 sp/month) — equipment repair, weather forecasts, emergency aid
- Fishing licenses for Lake Arden
- Boat blessings, apprenticeships, and community fish-smoking facilities
- Maritime safety advice and daily fishing forecasts
