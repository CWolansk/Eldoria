- **Type:** Swamp / Bog
- **Travel Difficulty:** Extremely Difficult
- **Key Hazards:** Quicksand, poisonous gases, hostile creatures, dark magic, the Witch herself

```
The Witch's Bog announces itself long before you see it. The air grows thick and humid, carrying the stench of rot and sulfur. Twisted trees emerge from the mist like skeletal hands clawing at the sky, their bark blackened and diseased. The ground becomes treacherous—what looks like solid earth often gives way to sucking mud that pulls at your boots with hungry persistence.

Glowing lights dance above the stagnant pools, beautiful and hypnotic, luring the unwary into the deepest, darkest waters. Strange sounds echo through the fog—whispers in languages long dead, the splash of something massive moving through the murk, and occasionally, a cackling laugh that raises the hair on the back of your neck.

Those who have escaped the bog speak of a ramshackle hut deep within, built on stilts above the festering water, where the Witch practices her vile craft.
```

## Overview

The Witch's Bog is a place of dark magic and death, avoided even by the orcish tribes who claim dominion over the surrounding wastes. Legend speaks of a powerful hag who dwells at its heart, brewing potions of terrible power and consorting with creatures from beyond the veil. Whether the bog's corruption spawned the Witch or she created it is unknown, but the two are now inseparable.

## Key Landmarks

- **The Witch's Hut**
    - A decrepit structure on stilts, accessible only by those who know the safe paths through the bog. Smoke of strange colors rises from its chimney.
- **The Drowning Pools**
    - Deceptively calm areas of deep water that have claimed countless victims. Bones can be seen beneath the surface.
- **Will-o'-Wisp Grove**
    - A cluster of dead trees where the ghostly lights congregate, especially during the new moon.
