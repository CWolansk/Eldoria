**Dragon Territory**
- **Location:** Deep southern Orcish Wastes
- **Type:** Rocky Valley / Dragon Lair
- **Travel Difficulty:** Suicidal
- **Key Hazards:** Sand dragons, extreme heat, territorial guardians, dragon cultists

Banner
- A narrow canyon with walls of sandstone, massive claw marks scarring the rock, and the glint of treasure in shadowed caves

```
Sand Dragon Valley cuts through the heart of the southern wastes like a wound in the earth. The canyon walls rise hundreds of feet on either side, their surfaces scored with claw marks the size of wagon wheels. The ground is littered with sun-bleached bones—some humanoid, some much, much larger—half-buried in drifts of rust-red sand.

The air here tastes of sulfur and old magic. You can feel it prickling against your skin, raising goosebumps despite the oppressive heat. The silence is unnatural, broken only by the whisper of sand cascading down the canyon walls and the occasional distant rumble that might be thunder or might be something far more terrifying.

Dark openings pierce the valley walls at irregular intervals—cave mouths large enough to swallow a house. From within comes the faint glimmer of reflected light, as if something precious catches the sun. But approaching those caves means entering the domain of creatures that have ruled these lands since before the kingdoms existed.
```

## Overview

Sand Dragon Valley is home to a population of ancient dragons who have adapted to the desert environment. These massive predators burrow through sand like water and breathe superheated air that can melt steel. The orcish tribes give the valley a wide berth, and even the most foolhardy treasure hunters rarely return. Those who have glimpsed the dragons from afar describe serpentine forms covered in scales the color of sandstone, nearly invisible against the canyon walls until they move.

## Key Landmarks

- **The Dragon Caves**
    - Massive cavern systems carved into the valley walls, serving as lairs for individual dragons. Their hoards are legendary, but so is their wrath.
- **The Offering Stone**
    - A flat rock at the valley's entrance where cultists leave tributes to appease the dragons. The bones scattered around it suggest the dragons are rarely satisfied.
- **The Bone Garden**
    - A section of the valley floor where centuries of dragon kills have accumulated, creating a macabre forest of bleached skeletons.
- **The Sunstone Spire**
    - A natural pillar of rock that glows with inner fire at sunset, rumored to be a dragon roosting spot for observing their territory.
