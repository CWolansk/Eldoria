**Scorching Wasteland**
- **Location:** Far western Orcish Wastes
- **Type:** Desert
- **Travel Difficulty:** Extremely Difficult
- **Key Hazards:** Extreme heat, dehydration, sandstorms, desert predators, minimal water sources

Banner
- Endless dunes of rust-red sand beneath a merciless sun, with heat shimmer distorting the horizon

```
The Red Dune Desert earns its name from the iron-rich sand that stretches in every direction like a sea of dried blood. The sun beats down with relentless fury, turning metal too hot to touch and making the air shimmer with heat. Each breath feels like inhaling from a forge, dry and scorching.

The dunes shift constantly, erasing any trace of passage within hours. Navigation is nearly impossible without magical aid—the featureless landscape offers no landmarks, and the heat-induced mirages show oases and cities that vanish as you approach. By night, the temperature plummets, the burning sands giving way to bitter cold that seeps into your bones.

Only the hardiest creatures survive here: sand scorpions the size of horses, serpents that swim through dunes like water, and things that lurk beneath the surface, waiting for vibrations that signal prey.
```

## Overview

The Red Dune Desert is one of the most inhospitable regions in all of Eldoria. It forms a natural barrier in the western Orcish Wastes, protecting the orc territories from invasion but also isolating them from potential allies or trade. Few dare to cross it, and fewer still survive the attempt. Ancient ruins occasionally emerge from the shifting sands, hinting at civilizations that failed to tame this unforgiving land.

## Key Landmarks

- **The Burning Stones**
    - A field of black volcanic rock that radiates heat even at night. Local orcish legends claim they fell from the sky in ancient times.
- **Oasis of Bones**
    - A small spring surrounded by the skeletal remains of countless creatures who reached water only to die from exhaustion or predator attack.
- **The Red Citadel**
    - Ruins of an ancient fortress that occasionally appears from beneath the dunes, only to be swallowed again within days.
