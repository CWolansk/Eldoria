## Overview
> A harsh and hostile territory east of Crestfall, dominated by warring orc tribes. Arid deserts, treacherous bogs, and windswept valleys make travel dangerous even without the ever-present threat of orcish raiders.

## Geography
- Arid deserts (Red Dune Desert), swamplands (Witch's Bog), and rocky valleys
- Ruins of an ancient pre-orc civilization scattered across the wastes
- Separated from Crestfall by contested borderlands
