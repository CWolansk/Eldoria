## Overview

The Cannibal Camp is a cursed place in the high eastern mountains where a degenerate tribe has made their home. Cast out from civilized lands generations ago, they have descended into barbarism and cannibalism, preying on travelers and each other. The surrounding kingdoms consider the camp a blight to be eradicated, but its remote location and the ferocity of its inhabitants have made it a persistent threat.

## Key Landmarks

- **The Bone Circle**
    - A ceremonial ring constructed entirely from human remains, where dark rituals are performed under the full moon.
- **The Larder Caves**
    - Natural caverns where the tribe stores their grisly provisions. The less said, the better.
- **Lookout Peak**
    - The highest point of the camp, where watchers scan the mountain passes for potential prey.
