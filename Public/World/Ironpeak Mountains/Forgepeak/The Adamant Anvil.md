> Descending into the fifth level of ForgePeak, a wave of heat rolls up. The Adamant Anvil sits at the heart of the Forges district — a massive stone building carved from the mountain itself, with rivers of molten rock flowing through channels in the floor.

## Proprietor
Thorin Ironheart (Mountain Dwarf, Master Smith) — 12th-generation master of the Anvil. Gruff, demanding, and utterly uncompromising about quality.

## Services & Wares
- **Heavy Armor:** Plate (1,500 gp), Splint (200 gp), Half Plate (750 gp), Shields (standard prices)
- **Masterwork Weapons:** Any martial weapon (standard + 50 gp), dwarven-forged quality
- **Specialty:** Adamantine armor, mithral armor (long wait lists)
- **Repairs:** Armor repair (10-50% cost), weapon resharpening (5-25 gp)
- Custom-fitted armor and commissions available

## Reputation
Thorin won't sell his best work to anyone who can't prove they'll use it honorably. No haggling, no rush orders. His standard: if it's not perfect, it gets melted down.
