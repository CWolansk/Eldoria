>## Layout
>
>ForgePeak descends through seven major levels carved into the mountain, each serving distinct purposes. The uppermost levels handle trade and diplomacy, while the deepest sacred forges and royal vaults lie near the mountain's heart. Massive stone pillars support cathedral-like halls, and rivers of molten rock are channeled to power the legendary forges.
>
>**Key Areas:**
>- The Great Hall (Level 1)
>	- Massive entrance chamber with throne room and diplomatic halls
>	- Bronze doors depicting dwarven history
>- Merchant Quarter (Levels 1-2)
>	- Trade halls connecting to surface routes
>	- Shops selling dwarven craftswork to surface dwellers
>- Residential Levels (Levels 2-4)
>	- Family clanholds carved into the mountain stone
>	- Community halls and underground gardens lit by magical crystals
>- The Forges (Levels 4-6)
>	- Master smithies producing legendary weapons and armor
>	- Apprentice workshops and guild halls
>- The Sacred Deeps (Level 7)
>	- Ancient forges blessed by the mountain gods
>	- Royal vaults and ancestral crypts
>	- Access restricted to the High King and master crafters
