## Overview
> The ancient elven kingdom of the Silverleaf Lands is a realm of natural beauty and arcane mastery. Snow-dusted forests, crystal springs, and luminescent pathways wind through settlements that blend seamlessly with the living landscape.

## Geography
- Dense enchanted forests (the Everwood), shimmering winter landscapes, sacred groves
- Crystal-clear springs with rumored healing properties
- Western border shared with Crestfall, connected via the Silverleaf Trade Route through Fairfield
