To your left, you see the busy market platforms of the Mid-Forest, where elven merchants display exquisite woodcarvings, shimmering fabrics, and gently glowing enchanted items. On your right, a graceful bridge of living branches leads to what appears to be an inn, its warm golden light spilling from windows shaped by the tree itself.

Looking up, you can barely make out the High Canopy far above, where noble estates perch on the highest platforms like jeweled ornaments. The crown of the eldest tree rises even higher, its massive branches supporting what must be the Council chambers.

Below, root-carved archways descend into the twilight depths of the Root-Halls, where soft blue luminescence hints at sacred groves and ancient libraries hidden beneath the forest floor.

Elven rangers move silently through the branches above, keeping watch with an ease that speaks of centuries of practice. The city feels alive, not just inhabited—as if the trees themselves welcome visitors into their embrace.

>## Layout
>
>FrostGlade exists on three distinct levels: the High Canopy where nobles and scholars dwell, the Mid-Forest containing markets and artisan workshops, and the Root-Halls housing archives and sacred groves. Living bridges of woven branches connect the massive silver-bark trees, while magical lights illuminate the perpetual twilight beneath the canopy.
>
>**Key Areas:**
>- The High Canopy
>	- Council chambers built into the crown of the eldest tree
>	- Noble estates perched on the highest platforms
>	- Observatory platforms for stargazing and weather-watching
>- The Mid-Forest
>	- Market platforms where elven goods are traded
>	- Artisan workshops specializing in woodcraft, weaving, and enchantment
>	- The Greenleaf Inn, one of few establishments welcoming shorter-lived races
>- The Root-Halls
>	- Ancient libraries preserving thousands of years of elven knowledge
>	- Sacred groves tended by druids and priests
>	- The Pool of Reflection, a natural spring with prophetic properties
>- The Ranger's Ring
>	- Training grounds and barracks circling the city's perimeter
>	- Concealed watchtowers monitoring forest approaches
