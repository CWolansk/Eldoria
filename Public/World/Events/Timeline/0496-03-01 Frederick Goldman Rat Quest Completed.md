## What Happened
- The party cleared [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Lord Frederick Goldman|Lord Frederick Goldman's]] cellar of rats, killing all doppelrats but damaging about 20% of the wine collection
- The guild refused to pay for mutated rat tails but paid out for the 2 doppelrat tails
- The party convinced the butler to send the repair bill to [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Jon Brightman|Jon Brightman]] at the Wizard's Guild, who sent it back to the Adventurers Guild, who the party then convinced to cover it

## Key Takeaways
- The party completed their first quest but made a messy impression on the Goldman household
- The Adventurers Guild covered the bill — but the party's reputation took a small hit
