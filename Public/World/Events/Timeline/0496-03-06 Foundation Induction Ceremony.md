## What Happened
During the post-crisis recovery in Highreach, an elderly soup vendor named [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Elder Rowan|Elder Rowan]] approached Justin and Vanessa at his "Foundation Soup Kitchen" cart.

## Details
- Each received a bowl of soup with a small, plain rock pendant at the bottom
- Elder Rowan explained the Foundation's mission: protecting common folk from the shadows
- Justin and Vanessa accepted membership and learned the organization's recognition phrases
- Gained Foundation pendants and access to a network of civilian protectors
