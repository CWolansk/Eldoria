## What Happened
- The party took on the Lost Pet quest from the Adventurers Guild to find a missing housecat in the forest
- Julie turned into a rat to lure predators, Claire killed the forest animals that showed up, and they caught the cat
- Returned the cat to [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Esmerelda Voss|Esmerelda Voss]], a retired adventurer
- Esmerelda rewarded the party with Daern's Instant Fortress — a rare magic item, surprisingly generous for a cat rescue

## Key Takeaways
- Esmerelda must have been a formidable adventurer to give away such a powerful item so casually
