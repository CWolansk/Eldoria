## What Happened
- The party entered the [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/Highreach Mines|Highreach Mines]] and cleared all of Level 1
- Fought goblin archers, an ogre with casters, solved a pressure plate trap and a cryptographic puzzle
- Defeated hobgoblins in the boss room (Room 10)
- Found a journal entry from a previous adventurer who didn't make it out

## Key Takeaways
- The mines go deeper — a downward slope leads to Level 2
- Notable loot: Boots of Striding and Springing, a magic pickaxe, a Burning Hands spell scroll, and crude maps of Levels 1 and 2
- Something darker than goblins may lurk in the lower levels
