## What Happened
- As the party exited the mines, a group of bandits ambushed them
- The lead bandit demanded their valuables, then attacked regardless
- The party suddenly felt sick mid-fight — some kind of delayed poison kicked in
- The party fought back: one member disarmed the bandit leader, another dropped the Instant Fortress on the group
- The bandits were crushed; the party looted a +1 greataxe from the leader

## Key Takeaways
- Someone sent these bandits specifically after the party — this wasn't random
- The poison was timed to the ambush — someone planned this carefully
- The party's resourcefulness (Instant Fortress) saved the day
