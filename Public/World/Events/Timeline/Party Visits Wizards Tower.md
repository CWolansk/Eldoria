## What Happened
- After resting at the [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/Sturdy Mugg|Sturdy Mugg]], the party visited the Highreach Wizard's Tower for information
- The tower glows during the day, has ornate marble flooring and a receptionist — the inside is larger than the outside
- Met [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Jon Brightman|Jon Brightman]], an evocation wizard
- Jon shared the legend of the Seeker's Eye — an ogre's eye turned into an artifact of great power that can locate other artifacts like a compass, created by a wizard named Jon

## Key Takeaways
- The Seeker's Eye is in the Highreach Mines
- Jon Brightman is a knowledgeable contact at the Wizard's Tower
