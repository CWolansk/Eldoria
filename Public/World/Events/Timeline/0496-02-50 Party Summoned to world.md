## What Happened
- A flash of white light transported the party to a forest clearing in Eldoria, surrounded by glowing red symbols
- Immediately attacked by spined devils and imps — the party fought them off, though one imp escaped into the trees
- A ghostly illusion of a wizard appeared and explained he had summoned the party from their world to help retrieve an artifact called the [[Public/World/Events/Timeline/0496-03-01 Party Visits Wizards Tower|Seeker's Eye]] from the Highreach Mines
- The party reluctantly accepted the quest and traveled to [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/Highreach|Highreach]]
- At the city gates, a guard misread JP's name as "Drumm Ironjaw" instead of Grumm
- The party retired to the [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/Sturdy Mugg|Sturdy Mugg]] inn for the night

## Key Takeaways
- The party was summoned to Eldoria by a mysterious wizard to retrieve the Seeker's Eye
- The wizard claims only the "pure of heart" can bypass the artifact's protections
- Highreach is the party's base of operations
