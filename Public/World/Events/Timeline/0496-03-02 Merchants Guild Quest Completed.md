## What Happened
- The party took a private job from [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Alistair Goldman|Alistair Goldman]], guildmaster of the [[Public/World/Groups/Merchants Guild|Merchants Guild]]
- They signed a magical contract forbidding them from discussing the quest
- They investigated [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Jarek IronFist|Jarek IronFist]] and spoke with other small merchants
- Julie broke the NDA to convince Jarek to go to the authorities — the magical contract punished her with pain
- Jarek went to the authorities with his evidence against Goldman

## Key Takeaways
- The party earned 100 gold and 10 guild points
- Goldman is not someone to cross lightly — he has resources and holds grudges
- Breaking a magical contract has real, painful consequences
