The party visited [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Jon Brightman|Jon Brightman]] to learn about the fly demon and the lich.

- Jon revealed the legend of Diego — a hero summoned from another world during the Wizarding Wars
- Diego defeated the lich Jon and sealed his power into artifacts hidden across the world
- The artifacts can only be unlocked by someone from Diego's world
- The next artifacts are located in the Silverleaf Lands (Elven Kingdom)
