The party reached the deepest chamber of [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/Highreach Mines|Highreach Mines]] and faced something far worse than goblins.

- Solved the barrier riddle to reach the inner chamber
- Found a pulsating orb on a stone pedestal
- The wizard appeared and demanded the orb — the party tried to hold onto it but he ripped it away with dark magic
- He crushed the orb and absorbed its power, then vanished
- A massive fly-demon and its larvae attacked — the party defeated them
- The mine began collapsing after the demon's death
