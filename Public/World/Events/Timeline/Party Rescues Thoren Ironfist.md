## What Happened
- The party descended to Level 3 of the [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/Highreach Mines|Highreach Mines]]
- Found a note left by a previous adventurer in the first room
- Solved a dwarven door puzzle to continue deeper
- Discovered [[Public/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Thoren Ironfist|Thoren Ironfist]], leader of the Nightstalkers, alive in a hidden bunk room
- The party reluctantly brought him back to the surface

## Key Takeaways
- Thoren survived his party's destruction by hiding — the rest of the Nightstalkers weren't so lucky
- The mines are even more dangerous than Level 1 suggested
