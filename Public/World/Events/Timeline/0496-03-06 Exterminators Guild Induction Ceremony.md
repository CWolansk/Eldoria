## What Happened
Don Gnocianno formally inducted the party into the [[Public/World/Groups/Exterminators Guild|Exterminators Guild]]. The ceremony took place amid Highreach's rebuilding after the Duplirat Crisis.

## Details
- Rat-themed headdresses presented to each party member — grant darkvision
- Ceremony intended to prove the guild's inclusivity to the crown
- Party recognized for their role in ending the Duplirat Crisis
