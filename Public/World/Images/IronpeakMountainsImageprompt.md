# Ironpeak Mountains Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create a sweeping fantasy mountain range landscape for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, wide panoramic bird's-eye view, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is the Ironpeak Mountains, the ancestral dwarven homeland — a colossal range of jagged granite peaks veined with precious metals and rare gems. Show the viewer looking across a vast mountain range from a high aerial vantage, with dozens of sharp snow-capped peaks filling the frame. The mountains should feel ancient, massive, and mineral-rich, with exposed rock faces showing veins of glinting metal and crystalline deposits.

Most important: the Ironpeak Mountains must read as an inhabited mountain kingdom, not empty peaks. Show five distinct dwarven settlements worked into the mountains: a colossal fortified gate carved into the largest peak (ForgePeak, the capital) with forge-glow spilling from its entrance, a glittering crystal-rich village on a mountainside (Gemstone Hollow), a serene shrine village with carved temple facades (Ironroot), a village wreathed in natural steam from hot springs (Steamhammer), and a mining village with visible mine shafts and ore carts on tracks (Stoneforge). Connect them with carved mountain roads, stone bridges spanning chasms, and switchback paths.

The range should feel imposing and vertical: sheer cliff faces, deep valleys between peaks, waterfalls cascading down rock faces, and snow on the highest summits. The treacherous eastern slopes should look wilder and more forbidding — rockslides, narrow passes, and sparse scrub. Forge-smoke and steam should rise from several peaks, hinting at industry within.

Mood: industrious, ancient, and formidable. Overcast weather with shafts of golden light breaking through heavy clouds, illuminating specific peaks. Use a palette of dark granite grays, iron blacks, warm forge-orange glows, cool snow whites, and occasional glints of gem-blue and gold from mineral deposits. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No flat plains, no tropical mountains, no single cave entrance with nothing else, no futuristic mining equipment, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no empty mountain range with no signs of habitation.
```
