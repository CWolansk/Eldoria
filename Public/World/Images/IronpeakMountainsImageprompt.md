# Ironpeak Mountains Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create a sweeping fantasy mountain range landscape for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, very high oblique bird's-eye view, atlas-like composition, camera pitched steeply downward about 55 degrees, extremely wide framing, north at the top of the image, west on the left, east on the right, south at the bottom, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is the Ironpeak Mountains, the ancestral dwarven homeland. The image must read like a breathtaking 3D watercolor-painted version of a world map, showing the entirety of the mountain range from above as a coherent regional overview. The camera should be high enough that the viewer understands the geography immediately. This colossal range of jagged granite peaks should form a massive mountainous spine stretching diagonally across the composition from the lower-left (southwest) up toward the upper-right (northeast).

Most important: lock the composition to these exact geographic relationships across the mountain range. Deep in the lower-left (southwest), place Stoneforge as a rugged mining village with visible mine shafts and ore carts carved into the rocky foothills. Moving slightly north and east along the range from Stoneforge, place Steamhammer, a village wreathed in natural white steam rising from hot springs.

Further north along the western edge of the mountains (middle-left of the image), place the colossal peak of Ironpeak Mountain itself, and beside it, ForgePeak — the dwarven capital. ForgePeak should feature a massive fortified stone gate carved directly into the largest mountain, with brilliant orange forge-glow spilling from its entrance.

Continuing the diagonal line northeast toward the exact center of the image, place Gemstone Hollow, a glittering crystal-rich settlement nestled high on a mountainside, sparkling with blue and violet mineral hues. Finally, far to the upper-right (northeast) where the mountains begin to taper off toward the Silverleaf border, place Ironroot, a serene and ancient shrine village with carved temple facades built into the high passes.

Connect these five settlements with a visible network of carved mountain roads, stone bridges spanning deep alpine chasms, and switchback paths. The eastern slopes of the mountain spine should look wilder, more forbidding, and arid as they face the distant Orcish Wastes. The western and southern lowlands bordering the mountains should hint at the distant fertile green plains of Crestfall.

Mood: industrious, ancient, and map-like in its clarity. Bright overcast weather with shafts of golden light breaking through heavy clouds, illuminating specific peaks and settlements. Use a palette of dark granite grays, iron blacks, warm forge-orange glows, cool snow whites on the highest peaks, and occasional glints of gem-blue and gold. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No flat plains dominating the center, no tropical mountains, no single cave entrance filling the frame, no reversed placements (ForgePeak must be west, Stoneforge southwest, Ironroot northeast), no futuristic mining equipment, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no empty mountain range with no settlements visible.
```
