# Orc City C Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create an orcish wasteland city for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable city layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is Orc City C, a mesa-top orcish stronghold rising dramatically above the flat Orcish Wastes. Show the viewer looking at a massive flat-topped mesa whose sheer cliff sides have been carved with defensive ramps, switchback roads, iron gates, and cliffside barracks, while the city sprawls across the entire mesa plateau above. The mesa city should fill the upper two-thirds of the image, with the cliff face, defensive ramps, and harsh desert floor visible below.

Most important: Orc City C must clearly read as a complete mesa-top city, not one ridge-top fortress. Show 80 or more structures packed across the plateau top — utilitarian yurt camps with sprawling hide awnings, war workshops, beast corrals, and jagged bone and iron stakes. The centerpiece is a massive, sun-scorched gladiator pit encircled by towering red-dyed leather yurts and jagged bone stakes. — with defensive ramps and a single heavily guarded switchback road spiraling down the cliff face to the desert below.

The mesa should make this city feel dramatically different from the Orc Capitol in its canyon and Orc City B straddling its river. Emphasize the height and isolation: cliffside barracks carved into the mesa walls, beast corrals on lower cliff ledges, signal towers at the mesa edges visible for miles, and the fortified ramp road as the only way in or out. Below the mesa, flat harsh badlands stretch to the horizon with dust haze, sparse scrub, and ancient broken stone remnants reclaimed by the orcs.

Mood: an atmosphere of raw, unyielding brutality. Blistering hot desert weather. The height should feel dangerous and commanding. Use scorched reds and intense oranges, dusty air, leather awnings, firelight, and smoke against a vast sweltering sky. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No cartoon orcs, no futuristic fortress, no gore, no random camp sprawl with no planning, no photorealism, no anime style, no single mesa fortress with no city around it, no tiny camp pretending to be a city, no readable text, no written signs.
```
