# Moonshade Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create a hidden elven forest village for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable village layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is Moonshade, a secluded elven village deep within the Everwood where elven elders study ancient lore and magic. Show the viewer looking down through a gap in the towering forest canopy so the village is fully visible and readable from above, even though it would be invisible from ground level. The settlement should fill most of the image, with the enormous ancient trees framing the scene rather than hiding it.

Moonshade must read as a complete scholar village, not a single shrine in the woods. Show 30-45 structures — tree-integrated homes, hanging bridges, study halls, libraries, rune-marked stone circles, and residential platforms — linked into a coherent pattern around a quiet central court paved with moonstone. The viewer should clearly see how the buildings connect via bridges, stairs, and glowing lantern paths, not just scattered platforms lost in foliage.

Moonshade should feel secretive, ancient, and wise, not like a busy market town or an open elven capital. Use sweeping Art Nouveau structures gracefully built into the trunks of enormous trees, adapted for extreme cold, The centerpiece landmark is a magnificent, multi-level archives woven into the snowy canopy, boasting sweeping white-wood balconies and plush frost-warded reading lounges. The forest should tower above and around the settlement, making it clear why travelers would walk right past without seeing it.

Include subtle artifact-hunt atmosphere through ancient stone ruins partially reclaimed by roots, ceremonial platforms, and sacred relic architecture woven into the trees. The surrounding forest should feel ancient and vast, with shafts of silver-blue moonlight cutting through the canopy. Mood: an air of elven superiority and comfort. Snowy weather. Use a palette of brilliant whites and light blues, deep forest greens, and quiet magic. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No open farmland, no dense human city blocks, no industrial smoke, no modern fantasy neon, no futuristic elements, no photorealism, no anime style, no isolated temple in empty forest, no single treehouse with no settlement around it, no readable text, no written signs.
```
