# Red Dune Desert Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create a vast red sand desert for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, wide panoramic elevated view looking across the dunes, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is the Red Dune Desert, a vast expanse of crimson sand within the Orcish Wastes. Show the viewer looking across an endless sea of towering red sand dunes stretching to the horizon under a punishing sky. The dunes should be massive — cathedral-sized ridges of deep red and burnt orange sand sculpted by relentless wind into sharp crescent shapes and long sinuous ridgelines.

Most important: the desert must feel immense, treacherous, and utterly inhospitable — a place where water doesn't exist and travelers die. The sand should be distinctly red, not yellow or white. Show wind whipping sand off the dune crests in long streamers. Scattered across the landscape, include subtle signs of danger and history: bleached bones of large creatures half-buried in sand, the tip of an ancient pre-orc stone monolith protruding from a dune, a dried-out oasis bed (cracked earth, dead palms), and distant dust devils spiraling on the horizon. A faint trail of footprints should be visible on one dune face, already being erased by the wind.

The sky should be harsh — a washed-out pale orange near the horizon deepening to a heavy brass color overhead, with no clouds and a blazing white sun. Heat shimmer should distort the horizon line.

Mood: merciless, vast, and ancient. Extreme heat and wind. Use a palette of deep crimson reds, burnt oranges, pale bone whites, and harsh brass-yellow for the sky. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No green oasis with water, no snow or ice, no lush vegetation, no coastal dunes, no Sahara-yellow sand (must be red), no futuristic elements, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no people prominently displayed.
```
