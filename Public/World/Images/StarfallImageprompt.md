# Starfall Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create an elven artisan border settlement for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable town layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is Starfall, an elegant Silverleaf border settlement and sister city to Fairfield, known for exquisite jewelry, magical artifacts, and refined elven craftsmanship. Show the viewer looking down over a graceful settlement at the northern edge of the Silverleaf Lands, where the enchanted snow-dusted forest begins to thin toward the green rolling farmland of Crestfall to the north. Refined roads and small bridges connect artisan courtyards, luminous workshops, and tree-integrated elven homes.

Most important: Starfall must unmistakably read as a complete inhabited elven town, not a single artisan court or one magical tower. Show 40-60 distinct buildings and workshops arranged along 2 or 3 graceful roads with a central artisan plaza, residential courts, guest houses for traders, small market lanes, and enough homes that the settlement feels wealthy, busy, and fully lived in.

Starfall should feel smaller than a capital but wealthier and more magical than a normal village, not like a dense human trade town or a fortified border fort. Use sweeping Art Nouveau architecture of pale stone and frost-glass, adapted for extreme cold with magical heating. Delicate arches, jewel-cutting studios, enchanters' ateliers, and tasteful market stalls displaying ornaments, gems, magical trinkets, and elegant crafted goods. Make the town blend with its landscape through carefully shaped trees, gardens, reflective pools, and silver-leaf foliage.

The centerpiece landmark is a sweeping Art Nouveau tower of pale white stone and frost-glass, glowing with internal warmth and intricate light-blue enchantments., but the real focus should be artisan life: visible craft halls, magical displays, jewelers working in open courtyards, trade wagons arriving from Crestfall to the north, and a refined road leading back toward the human border. The settlement fills most of the image, with the Silverleaf Lands' snowy forests stretching south and a hint of distant green human lands visible to the north.

Mood: an air of elven superiority and comfort. Snowy weather. Use a palette of brilliant whites and light blues, luminous crystals, and touches of warm magical heating. The image should feel like a place players would want to walk through and browse. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No giant capital city scale, no harsh fortress walls, no industrial forge city, no grimdark decay, no neon fantasy, no futuristic elements, no photorealism, no anime style, no isolated pavilion in empty forest, no tiny hamlet with only a handful of buildings, no readable text, no written signage.
```
