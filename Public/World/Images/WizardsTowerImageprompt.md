# Wizard's Tower Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create a lone mysterious wizard's tower in a wasteland for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable landscape layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is the Wizard's Tower, a mysterious structure rising from the southern Orcish Wastes west of Ironroot, said to belong to an ancient wizard. Show the viewer looking down from above at a tall, impossibly slender dark tower standing alone in a barren wasteland. From above, the circular footprint of the tower should be clearly visible, along with a radiating pattern of magical corruption spreading outward from its base — cracked black stone in a perfect circle, as if the earth itself was scorched or transmuted. The tower should be built of dark stone — near-black with faint arcane runes or geometric patterns barely visible on its surface.

Most important: the tower must feel ancient, powerful, and deeply unsettling from above — not a cozy wizard's home. The architecture should be unnervingly precise — perfectly smooth dark stone in sharp geometric lines, no visible mortar, no weathering on the upper sections despite the harsh desert wind. A faint cold light should emanate from narrow slit windows at irregular intervals up the tower's height. Strange geometric patterns should be scorched into the ground around the base, visible from the aerial perspective as arcane sigils. Faint magical distortion — like heat shimmer but with a violet tint — should surround the upper portion of the tower.

The surrounding wasteland should be flat, cracked earth with sparse dead scrub stretching in every direction. The tower should be the only vertical element for miles, making it feel alien and isolated. On the distant horizon to the east, the faint outline of Ironroot's settlement should be barely visible. The tower's long dramatic shadow should stretch across the cracked earth.

Mood: isolated, arcane, and ominous. Late afternoon with long dramatic shadows. Use a palette of near-black stone, violet-tinged magical glow, dusty wasteland browns, cracked gray earth, and a heavy amber sky. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No cozy cottage, no cheerful wizard's study, no bright magical fireworks, no ruined crumbling tower, no castle or fortress, no green surroundings, no futuristic elements, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no visible wizard or people.
```
