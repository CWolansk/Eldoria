# Wizard's Tower Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create a lone mysterious wizard's tower in a wasteland for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, ground-level perspective looking up at the tower, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is the Wizard's Tower, a mysterious structure rising from the barren expanse of the Orcish Wastes, said to belong to an ancient wizard. Show the viewer approaching from ground level, looking up at a tall, impossibly slender dark tower that rises far higher than any structure built on such barren ground should. The tower should be built of dark stone — near-black with faint arcane runes or geometric patterns barely visible on its surface, as if carved long ago and partially eroded.

Most important: the tower must feel ancient, powerful, and deeply unsettling — not a cozy wizard's home. The architecture should be unnervingly precise for the wasteland setting — perfectly smooth dark stone in sharp geometric lines, no visible mortar, no weathering on the upper sections despite the harsh desert wind. A faint cold light should emanate from narrow slit windows at irregular intervals up the tower's height. The entrance should be a single tall narrow doorway at the base — no door visible, just darkness within. The ground immediately around the tower's base should be different from the surrounding wasteland: cracked black stone in a perfect circle, as if the earth itself was scorched or transmuted.

The surrounding wasteland should be flat, cracked earth with sparse dead scrub and distant red-rock ridges. The tower should be the only vertical element for miles, making it feel alien and out of place. Faint magical distortion — like heat shimmer but with a violet tint — should surround the upper portion of the tower.

Mood: isolated, arcane, and ominous. Late afternoon with long dramatic shadows stretching from the tower across the cracked earth. Use a palette of near-black stone, violet-tinged magical glow, dusty wasteland browns, cracked gray earth, and a heavy amber sky. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No cozy cottage, no cheerful wizard's study, no bright magical fireworks, no ruined crumbling tower, no castle or fortress, no green surroundings, no futuristic elements, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no visible wizard or people.
```
