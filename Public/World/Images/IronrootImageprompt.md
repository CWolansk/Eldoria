# Ironroot Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create a dwarven shrine village for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable village layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is Ironroot, a village built around ancient dwarven shrines where religious ceremonies, rituals, and meditation shape daily life. Show the viewer looking down over a broad mountain shelf where the village rises in stair-stepped terraces around a central shrine complex. The settlement should fill most of the image, with snow-dusted peaks and misty valleys visible in the distance beyond the terraces.

Most important: Ironroot must clearly read as a full inhabited shrine village, not just a single monastery. Show 25-40 homes, monks' quarters, prayer halls, shrine courts, kitchens, storehouses, stairways, bridges, and communal buildings arranged around the sacred core so the settlement feels real and inhabited.

Ironroot should feel ancient, disciplined, and spiritual, not like a busy mining town or a forge city. The architecture is hardcore Brutalist, prioritizing function over form. The centerpiece is a staggering, purely functional black-iron monument-shrine defying the howling winds, draped in heavy red chains and soot-stained banners., and a few modest homes and communal buildings tucked among the sacred architecture. The shrines must dominate the composition and give the village a feeling of deep age and reverence.

Mood: a vibe of relentless hard work and grit. Severely rainy and windy weather. Use intense black and red colors, blocky concrete, black iron, and heavy rain. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No bustling trade city, no huge forge smoke, no desert fortress, no futuristic temple design, no photorealism, no anime style, no isolated shrine with no village around it, no tiny hamlet with almost no buildings, no readable text, no written signs.
```
