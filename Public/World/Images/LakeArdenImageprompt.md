# Lake Arden Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create a vast misty lake scene for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, elevated three-quarter view looking across the water, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is Lake Arden, a vast briny lake in northern Crestfall shrouded in persistent mist. Show the viewer looking across the lake from an elevated vantage above the northern shore, where the fishing town of Ardenville sits along the waterline with wooden piers, docks, and small boats. The lake should stretch wide and far, with visibility dropping sharply as thick mist obscures the middle and far reaches. The southern shore should be barely visible through the haze — a dramatic cliff face rising steeply, hinting at the Highreach plateau above.

Most important: Lake Arden must feel vast, mysterious, and slightly unsettling — not a calm pastoral pond. The water should be dark and briny, with a greenish-gray tint. Kelp-choked shallows should be visible along the near shore — tangled masses of dark aquatic vegetation forming a natural breakwater. Beyond the shallows, the water deepens rapidly and the mist thickens. A few fishing boats should be visible on the water, some close to shore with nets out, others fading into the mist further out.

The northern shoreline should show Ardenville's docks and watchtowers stretching along the water's edge, with the town extending slightly inland. Defensive watchtowers should be positioned along the shore, their tops disappearing into low-hanging fog. The surface of the lake should have an oily stillness broken only by the wakes of boats and occasional dark shapes beneath the surface that could be kelp — or something else.

Mood: atmospheric, vast, and eerie. Overcast and misty, with diffused light creating no clear shadows. Use a palette of slate grays, deep blue-greens, misty whites, and the warm brown of wooden docks and boats as the only warm accent. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No tropical blue lagoon, no clear sunny water, no small pond, no ocean with waves, no sandy beach resort, no futuristic boats, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no sea monsters prominently displayed.
```
