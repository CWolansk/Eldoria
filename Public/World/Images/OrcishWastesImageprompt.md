# Orcish Wastes Image Prompt

## Prompt — Primary (Wide Aerial Regional Overview)

```text
Create a sweeping fantasy wasteland landscape for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration. Warm ink linework with aggressive, high-contrast watercolor washes — more saturated and harsh than the muted Crestfall palette. Wide panoramic bird's-eye view, camera pitched about 40 degrees from the horizon to show both the terrain and the sky. Extremely wide framing. North at top, west on left, east on right, south at bottom. Blistering hot daytime, harsh orange sun at upper center. Detailed but not photorealistic.

This is the Orcish Wastes — a vast and violent territory stretching across the entire eastern expanse of the known world, dominated by warring orc tribes. The image reads as an immediate, visceral overview: not beautiful, not peaceful. Hostile terrain that has been CLAIMED.

TERRAIN LAYOUT (must show clear transitions between zones):

WESTERN EDGE (left side) — TRANSITION ZONE: The left edge of the image shows the abrupt border where the Ironpeak Mountains' dark gray foothills and Crestfall's distant green plain give way to barren wasteland. This transition should be a hard, visible line — living terrain ending, cracked dead earth beginning. No gradual fade.2

WEST-CENTER — THE MAIN WASTELAND INTERIOR: Cracked red-brown earth with visible desiccation polygons (cracked mud patterns where water once pooled), dust devils visible as small spinning funnels crossing the plain, scattered clusters of jagged reddish-brown rock outcroppings, sparse dead scrub. The OROG CAPITOL is here: a canyon city built into a rocky valley in the center-west of the wasteland. Show the canyon as a deep gash in the terrain, with buildings of packed clay and large stacked boulders (no mortar) filling the canyon floor in dense rows. A bone-and-skull-adorned timber gate straddles the canyon's entrance, flanked by two 30-foot watchtower poles with red-and-black orcish banners. Smaller war camps — clusters of 10–15 hide-and-pole tents arranged in rough circles — are scattered across the terrain between here and the mountain border, each with a central fire pit.

NORTHWEST (upper-left area) — THE WITCH'S BOG: A roughly circular area of sickly dark terrain, clearly differentiated from the surrounding wasteland by its wet, black-green coloration. From above, the bog surface appears as dark near-black water covered in thick pale-green algae, interrupted by clusters of pale dead trees (bare, skeletal, branching widely). A green-tinted mist is visible rising from the bog's surface, catching the orange sunlight and looking sickly. The bog is clearly visible but not the dominant feature — it should feel wrong and contained within the larger wasteland.

FAR NORTHEAST (upper-right) — THE RED DUNE DESERT: The entire upper-right quadrant transitions abruptly from cracked red-brown earth to true crimson sand dunes — massive crescent-shaped barchan dunes of deep red-orange, their slip faces casting sharp shadows. The dunes increase in size toward the far northeast corner, implying an immense desert continuing beyond the image edge. The transition from flat wasteland to dune field is abrupt and clear.

FAR EAST (right side, center) — SAND DRAGON VALLEY: A narrow canyon cutting from north to south in the eastern portion of the wasteland. From above, the canyon appears as a dark irregular gash in the red terrain, its floor partially visible as pale sand. No other identification needed — the scale of the canyon and its isolation speak for themselves.

SOUTHERN CENTER — THE WIZARD'S TOWER: A single unnaturally tall, perfectly slender dark spire rising from flat cracked earth in the south-central wasteland. Its shadow stretches dramatically northeast across the terrain (as a long sharp dark line) due to the low sun angle. A perfect circle of scorched black earth surrounds its base, visible from above as a dark disc.

Mood: severe, vast, and ancient. Heat haze visible as slight blurring along the horizon. Orcish war banners flutter at the canyon gates and larger camps. Palette: scorched red-brown for the main wasteland, burnt orange for exposed rock, sickly green-black for the bog, deep crimson and burnt orange for the dune field, near-black for the Wizard's Tower, pale bone-white for the canyon floor. A brassy orange-gold sky with no clouds at the zenith fading to pale orange-white near the horizon. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No lush green paradise, no frozen tundra, no ocean coastline, no single fortress in empty sand, no futuristic elements, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no comedy orcs, no gore, no dark night sky, no pleasant or inviting atmosphere.
```

## Alternative POV A — Ground-Level Vista from a Wasteland Ridge

*Use this if the full regional overview produces an unsatisfying result. This POV focuses on the most immediately striking quality of the Orcish Wastes: the horizon-filling scale of hostile red terrain, with the Red Dune Desert visible as a distant crimson wall.*

### Prompt

```text
Create an atmospheric fantasy landscape for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration. Warm ink linework with bold atmospheric watercolor washes. Ground-level wide horizontal panorama, standing on a low rocky ridge, looking east. Eye-level perspective, camera completely horizontal. The horizon is visible. Extremely wide format. Blazing midday. Detailed but not photorealistic.

The viewer stands on a shallow ridge of cracked reddish-brown rock in the foreground — loose gravel and flat stone plates underfoot, a single knee-high dead scrub bush to the left, a crumbling old pre-orc stone monolith (a rough stone pillar, 8 feet tall, partially toppled, covered in angular geometric carvings barely legible under wind erosion) to the right. The ridge drops away in the immediate foreground.

The MIDDLE GROUND shows the main Orcish Wastes stretching away: flat cracked earth in a scorched red-brown, with three or four dust devils visible as spinning columns of pale dust at different distances (the largest is 100 feet tall in the center distance). A cluster of five hide-and-pole tents — an orcish war camp — is visible at roughly the 2-mile distance mark, small but clearly recognizable by the red-and-black banner on a pole above the central tent. One faint dark smoke smear rises from the camp.

The HORIZON (about 15 miles out) is dominated by the Red Dune Desert: a dramatic wall of deep crimson rising from the flat wasteland as an abrupt elevated landscape, the dune ridgelines forming a jagged crimson silhouette across the entire width of the horizon, dunes clearly massive at this distance. Heat haze softens the dunes' base slightly. The sky above is a harsh brass-gold deepening to deep amber at the zenith, no clouds.

Mood: exposed, alien, and oppressively hot. The silence implies danger. Palette: scorched red-brown for the foreground ground, dusty tan for the distant dust devils, small muted red-black for the war camp banner, deep crimson for the far dune wall, and a blisteringly hot brass-gold sky. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

### Negative Prompt

```text
No green terrain, no water, no friendly figures, no safe atmosphere, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no nighttime setting, no ice or snow.
```

## Alternative POV B — The Orc Capitol Canyon from Above (Close Aerial)

*Use this for a focused look at the Orcish Wastes' most populated location — the canyon city.*

### Prompt

```text
Create a detailed fantasy settlement scene for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration. Heavy ink linework with aggressive, high-contrast watercolor washes. High oblique bird's-eye view at 50 degrees, camera pointing straight down into a canyon from a slight northwestern angle. Medium-close framing — the canyon fills the majority of the frame. Harsh bright midday sun from almost directly overhead. Detailed but not photorealistic.

The canyon is a ragged gash in red-brown cracked earth, roughly 200 feet wide and 400 feet long within the frame, walls 80 feet tall and nearly vertical, composed of stacked layers of reddish-brown and pale orange sandstone. The canyon floor is entirely packed with buildings — a dense, chaotic city with no streets wider than 6 feet. Buildings are uniform in material: rough packed clay walls the same color as the canyon walls, flat roofs of salvaged wood and animal hide. Some roofs have red-black orcish banners staked to their tops. Buildings range from 10 to 25 feet tall, packed tightly together with no formal planning — organic settlement filling every available square foot. From above, the rooftops form a jumbled patchwork.

THE MAIN GATE (at the north — top of the image — where the canyon opens): A timber-and-bone gate structure spans the canyon entrance. Two 30-foot-tall poles rise from large stone base plinths on each side, lashed with rope and adorned with rows of animal skulls at intervals. A heavy crossbar of black-tarred logs runs between them. The gate stands open. Two orcish guards are visible as small dark silhouettes flanking it.

The canyon floor between buildings shows packed dirt pathways and a central water distribution channel (a carved stone trough running the length of the canyon floor, now nearly empty, showing cracked dried mud at the bottom). Several fire pits on rooftops show rising thin smoke.

Mood: crowded, grim, and functional. Pure efficiency, no aesthetics. Baking hot with sharp midday shadows. Palette: red-brown clay buildings blending with canyon walls, black and red banners, pale bone-white skull decorations, packed dark earth floor, and the near-black of deep shadows in the canyon's narrowest alleys. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

### Negative Prompt

```text
No ornate fantasy architecture, no clean streets, no modern city planning, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no castle or fortress walls surrounding the canyon, no friendly market atmosphere.
```
