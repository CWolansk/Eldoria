# Stardew Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create a tranquil elven agricultural village for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable village layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is Stardew, a rustic, druidic elven farming village in the heart of the Silverleaf Lands. Show the viewer looking down over a snowy, serene valley. The elves live as one with nature, never forcing or conquering the land. Instead of traditional ploughed wheat fields, the agriculture consists of beautifully organic patches of resilient winter vegetables, frost-berries, and orchards of cold-weather fruits that blend naturally into the snowy environment. Dense ancient forest borders the village on two sides.

Most important: Stardew must unmistakably read as a simple, ancient elven druid settlement, not a futuristic alien city or a generic human farm town. Show 35-60 organic shelters made directly from living winter trees that have been gently coaxed and bent to form beautiful, natural wooden huts and woven canopies. The structures are literally made of living trunks, woven branches, and thick pine boughs granting shelter from the snow, seamlessly integrated into the natural winter landscape.

Stardew should feel humble, intensely rooted in the earth, and deeply attuned to natural druidic magic rather than high-science or glittering crystal sorcery. The architecture entirely lacks cut lumber, square corners, glasshouses, or traditional barns. The centerpiece is a large, ancient living-tree grove where the thick branches of massive pines have been carefully woven together by druid magic into a grand, beautiful communal longhouse. Instead of typical forges or metal structures, include beautifully natural root-cellars, woven-branch storehouses, and delicate wind-chimes hanging from the boughs.

The village center should be peaceful, with ancient elves tending to the winter earth by hand in deep reverence. Mood: an air of peaceful coexistence, ancient druidic tradition, and deep connection to nature. Snowy weather. Use a palette of brilliant whites, earthy wood browns, pine greens, and the soft, natural firelight from small communal hearths casting a warm glow on the snow. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No sci-fi glasshouses, no futuristic alien structures, no glowing crystals everywhere, no metallic domes, no high-tech elements, no generic human medieval houses, no traditional wood barns, no dirt ploughed fields, no Tudor architecture, no straight square crop rows, no muddy dirt roads, no thatched roofs, no cartoonish style, no simple vector art, no giant capital city, no grim fortress, no industrial smoke, no photorealism, no anime style, no isolated farmhouse scene, no tiny hamlet with almost no buildings, no readable text, no written signs.
```
