# Orc Capitol Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create a major orc capital city for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable city layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is the Orc Capitol, the seat of power in the Orcish Wastes. Show the viewer looking down into a harsh rocky canyon where the Orc Capitol fills the entire valley floor and climbs the canyon walls on both sides. The city should feel brutal, strategic, and organized — a true capital of a warlike people, not a random camp. The settlement fills most of the image, with cracked wasteland and distant badlands ridges visible at the canyon mouth and horizon.

Most important: the Orc Capitol must unmistakably read as a full capital city, not one fortress surrounded by tents. Show a very large urban footprint with many districts, 100 or more structures, broad roads, dense barracks quarters, market sectors, industrial yards, pens, ramparts, and layered neighborhoods surrounding the central stronghold.

Build the city using utilitarian yurt camps with sprawling hide awnings and jagged bone and iron stakes. The centerpiece is a monumental, sprawling fortress of colossal yurt pavilions and jagged red stakes, sweltering under the desert sun with raw, brutalist presence. Surround the citadel with crowded districts of barracks, war camps, forge yards, beast pens, training grounds, and market spaces built for survival and conquest. Work in ancient ruined megaliths or remnants of a pre-orc civilization incorporated into the city's defenses and foundations, giving the place deep age and weight.

The wider landscape should reinforce the Orcish Wastes: cracked earth, rocky ridges, dust haze, sparse scrub, and distant badlands. Mood: an atmosphere of raw, unyielding brutality. Blistering hot desert weather. Use scorched reds and intense oranges, dust haze, cracking desert heat, and weathered leather. The city should look dangerous and hostile, but still coherent and believable as the center of an empire.

Mood: severe, powerful, militarized, and ancient. Keep it grounded and imposing rather than cartoonish. No gore, no piles of skulls, no readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No comedy orcs, no random chaotic tent camp with no urban structure, no futuristic wasteland, no sci-fi armor, no gore, no photorealism, no anime style, no single citadel with a few tents, no empty wasteland around one fortress, no readable text, no written signs.
```
