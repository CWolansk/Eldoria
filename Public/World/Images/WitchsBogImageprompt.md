# Witch's Bog Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create a dark and sinister swamp for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, ground-level perspective looking across the bog, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is the Witch's Bog, a murky swamp in the Orcish Wastes where dark magic is said to fester. Show the viewer standing at the bog's edge looking across a vast expanse of stagnant water, dead trees, and tangled vegetation. The water should be dark and opaque — a sickly greenish-black surface covered in patches of thick algae and pale floating scum. Dead trees rise from the water like skeletal hands, their bare branches draped in hanging moss and cobwebs.

Most important: the Witch's Bog must feel deeply wrong and unnatural — not just a normal swamp. Subtle magical corruption should be visible: a faint green-purple miasma or mist hovering just above the water's surface, bioluminescent fungi growing in unnatural spiraling patterns on dead wood, water that seems to glow faintly in the deepest pools, twisted trees that look almost like they're reaching or grasping, and strange circular patterns in the algae as if something stirs beneath. A barely visible path of half-submerged stepping stones leads deeper into the bog.

The surrounding terrain should transition from the dry wasteland at the edges to increasingly waterlogged ground, then standing water. The bog should feel vast — extending beyond the frame in every direction. Thick fog limits visibility to a few hundred feet at most. Gas bubbles should break the water's surface in places.

Mood: dread, corruption, and supernatural wrongness. Perpetual overcast twilight with no clear light source. Use a palette of sickly greens, murky blacks, putrid yellows, faint purple magical glow, and the bone-gray of dead wood. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No beautiful bayou, no sunny wetland, no mangrove paradise, no clear blue water, no living green trees, no friendly swamp creatures, no witch's hut prominently displayed, no futuristic elements, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs.
```
