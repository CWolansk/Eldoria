# Crestfall Image Prompt

## Prompt — Primary (High Aerial Atlas View)

```text
Create a sweeping regional landscape for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration. Medium-weight watercolor washes with crisp ink linework for roads, walls, and settlement details. Very high oblique bird's-eye view, atlas-like composition, camera pitched steeply downward about 55 degrees from the horizon. Extremely wide framing — the image should feel like a painted section of a world atlas. North at the top, west on the left, east on the right, south at the bottom. Detailed but not photorealistic. Warm golden light from the upper-left (northwest), casting long, faint shadows toward the lower-right.

This is Crestfall, a prosperous human kingdom of fertile plains, trade roads, lake country, vineyards, and frontier woods. The camera must be high enough that the viewer reads the entire regional geography in a single glance — this is not a close-up of any single settlement. Show the northern half of Crestfall with all landmarks in clear spatial relationship: readable distances between settlements, visible dirt road lines connecting them, and natural terrain transitions.

LOCK these exact geographic positions — do not deviate:

UPPER-LEFT (northwest): Ardenville — a modest coastal port town of roughly 40–50 tightly-packed stone-and-timber buildings hugging a natural harbor bay. The harbor shows 5–8 merchant ships with furled sails docked at wooden piers. The sea is visible only on the far western edge of the image, limited to the immediate harbor area. Lake Arden begins here and stretches eastward as a long, thin, irregularly-shaped body of pale gray-blue water across the top third of the image.

LEFT-CENTER (center-west, directly south of Lake Arden): Highreach — the dominant visual anchor of the entire image. Highreach sits atop a massive flat-topped limestone butte — a rough oval plateau roughly 600 feet across and 250 feet tall with near-vertical cliff faces on all sides. The city's walls run around the entire plateau rim. The royal castle occupies the highest central ground with a single large keep tower. From this height, the city interior shows 80–100 rooftops in Tudor-style packed rows. At the northern base of the cliffs, directly lakeside, show a small warehouse-and-dock district: 8–12 squat stone buildings on a narrow shelf of land, connected to the plateau city above by a large wooden crane-and-pulley cargo elevator mounted to the cliff face, with a platform visible mid-lift.

UPPER-RIGHT (far northeast): Fairfield — a crossroads trade town on gently elevated farmland. Smaller than Highreach. Show 30–40 buildings arranged around a central market square, four roads meeting at the center, and merchant wagons visible on the approach roads.

LOWER-LEFT (southwest, south of Highreach): Hillcrest — a small agricultural village of 15–20 buildings nestled among narrow terraced vineyard strips carved into gently rolling hillsides. The terracing pattern should be clearly visible from above — parallel curved lines of vines following the hillside contours. Apple orchards fill the low ground between Hillcrest and the Highreach road.

LOWER-CENTER/RIGHT (southeast of Highreach): Stonehaven — a small rectangular fortified outpost. Show a wooden palisade wall forming a clear rectangle, a single tall square watchtower at the northwest corner, and 10–12 simple buildings inside. Stonehaven sits at the immediate northwest edge of the Blackwood Forest — the first tree line begins within 200 feet of its eastern palisade wall.

BOTTOM-RIGHT (entire southeast quadrant): The Blackwood Forest — a dense, airless-looking, uniformly dark forest canopy filling the entire southeast corner of the image. The canopy texture should be matte dark-green with no light penetrating, clearly distinguishable from the open golden farmland. The forest edge is irregular and jagged, not a clean line.

ROAD NETWORK (all roads are pale dust-colored dirt lines with slightly wider cleared verges): (1) A road running east from Ardenville along the southern shore of Lake Arden to Highreach. (2) A road running south from Highreach to Hillcrest. (3) A road running southeast from Highreach to Stonehaven, entering the Blackwood Forest and disappearing under the canopy. (4) The Great Western Road running northeast from Highreach through open farmland toward Fairfield.

FARMLAND TEXTURE (fills all open ground between settlements): Irregular patchwork of buff-gold wheat fields, pale green pasture, dark brown plowed earth, hedgerow lines, scattered tree clusters, small creek ribbons with tiny stone bridges, and isolated farmstead clusters of 3–5 buildings.

Mood: prosperous, civilized, and organized — a kingdom at its peak. Bright clear daytime, soft atmospheric haze sitting on Lake Arden (slightly softening detail at the water surface), crisp sharp visibility across the farmlands. Palette: warm harvest golds and yellows for grain fields, spring greens for pasture, soft dusty earth browns for roads, cool gray-blue for the lake, pale cream for limestone cliff faces, deep shadow-greens for the Blackwood. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No city built in the middle of Lake Arden, no Highreach inside or on the lake, no lake south of Highreach, no Ardenville on the southern shore, no giant foreground mountain range framing the scene, no huge open ocean dominating the composition, no desert, no frozen tundra, no tropical jungle, no futuristic elements, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no empty wilderness with no settlements visible, no Highreach shown as a small village.
```

## Alternative POV A — Lakeside View of Highreach from the Shore

*Use this if the full regional overview produces an unsatisfying composition. This POV is simpler to generate and emphasizes Highreach's dramatic geography.*

### Prompt

```text
Create a dramatic fantasy landscape for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration. Medium watercolor washes with precise ink linework. Horizontal wide-format composition, ground-level perspective from a rowboat or small dock on the southern shore of Lake Arden, camera facing south-southwest. The viewer is on the water looking toward Highreach. Detailed but not photorealistic.

In the CENTER of the image, Highreach's plateau rises dramatically from flat ground on the far shore of the lake. The plateau is a roughly oval flat-topped limestone butte approximately 250 feet tall with near-vertical pale cream cliff faces on all sides. The city of Highreach fully crowds the plateau top — ring walls run the entire plateau rim, with Tudor-style rooftops packed inside. A royal castle keep rises from the highest center point, flying blue and gold banners visible even at distance. The plateau's northern face (facing the viewer) drops directly to a narrow shelf of land at the waterline. On this shelf: a small warehouse district of squat stone buildings barely visible above the waterline, and a large wooden crane-and-pulley cargo elevator mounted to the cliff face, mid-lift with a loaded cargo platform.

The lake fills the lower third of the image — calm pale gray-blue water with gentle ripples, reflecting the pale cream of the cliffs above. The far lakeshore is visible behind and to both sides of the plateau, hinting at farmland. The sky is bright blue with a few high cirrus clouds. Atmospheric distance haze softens the far shoreline.

Mood: awe-inspiring and grand, the sense of a city built impossibly high. Bright warm afternoon light from the upper-right, illuminating the city's western walls and the cliff face. Palette: pale limestone cream for cliffs, warm honey-stone for city walls, blue and gold banners, gray-blue lake water, warm sky blue. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

### Negative Prompt

```text
No lake in the sky, no Highreach at ground level, no cave or underground setting, no futuristic elements, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no empty lake with no city.
```

## Alternative POV B — The Cliffside Cargo Elevator District

*Use this for a unique close-up of one of Highreach's most distinctive features — the cliffside warehouse district at the base of the plateau.*

### Prompt

```text
Create a detailed fantasy architectural scene for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration. Watercolor washes with crisp ink detailing. Medium-close view from water level, looking north at the base of Highreach's northern cliff face. Camera at eye level from a small wooden dock, tilted slightly upward. Detailed but not photorealistic.

The cliff face fills most of the frame from left edge to right edge. Pale cream limestone rises vertically, the cliff top cut off above the image frame — the full height is implied rather than shown. Carved into and built against the cliff base: 8–10 squat stone warehouses with low slate roofs, iron-banded wooden doors, and barrels and crates stacked on loading platforms. Wooden catwalks connect buildings along the cliff face at different heights.

CENTERPIECE: A massive wooden cargo elevator system mounted to the cliff face. Two thick rope-and-pulley towers are bolted directly into the stone, 30 feet apart, rising to brackets anchored 80 feet up the cliff face. A wooden platform (large enough for a loaded cart) hangs on the elevator, loaded with three large crates, currently mid-lift — halfway up the cliff face. A counterweight (a large stone block) hangs on the opposite side, slightly lower than the platform. At the top of the elevator's reach, a wooden dock platform protrudes from the cliff face with two workers visible as small silhouettes.

The lake is visible in the foreground — the dock the viewer stands on extends into calm gray-blue water. A single flat-bottomed barge is tied up at the warehouse dock, half-unloaded. The narrow strip of sky visible above the cliff top is bright blue. Morning light from the left, casting the elevator ropes in warm gold.

Mood: industrious, slightly cramped, dwarfed by the scale of the cliff. Palette: pale limestone cream, weathered gray timber, iron brown, rope tan, warm morning light. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

### Negative Prompt

```text
No full city visible, no underground setting, no futuristic crane machinery, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs.
```
