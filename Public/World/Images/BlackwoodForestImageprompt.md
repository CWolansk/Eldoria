# Blackwood Forest Image Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Create a dark ancient forest scene for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, ground-level perspective looking into the forest depths, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is the Blackwood Forest, a dark and ancient woodland stretching south from the frontier outpost of Stonehaven toward the Ironpeak Mountains. Show the viewer standing at the forest's edge looking inward, where massive ancient tree trunks — far wider than a person — rise into an interwoven canopy that blocks nearly all sunlight. The forest interior should exist in perpetual twilight, with only thin shafts of pale light penetrating the canopy in rare gaps.

Most important: the Blackwood must feel genuinely old, oppressive, and dangerous — not a pleasant woodland hike. The trees should be enormous, gnarled, and ancient, with thick roots erupting from dark soil. The canopy should be so dense that the forest floor is dimly lit even at midday. A narrow path — barely more than a game trail — should wind between the trunks, disappearing into the gloom. Show hints of danger without explicit monsters: strange claw marks on bark, an abandoned camp with scattered belongings, eyes reflecting faintly from the undergrowth, and mist curling between the trunks.

The outer edge (foreground) should show the cleared perimeter of Stonehaven — stumps, a palisade wall partially visible, and the abrupt transition from open ground to dense forest. Deeper in, the trees grow larger and the light fails. Thick moss, shelf fungi, and hanging lichen cover everything. The ground is layered with decaying leaves and twisted roots.

Mood: foreboding, ancient, and claustrophobic. Overcast sky barely visible through the canopy, with mist pooling in low areas. Use a palette of deep shadow greens, dark browns, charcoal blacks, and rare sickly-pale shafts of filtered light. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
No bright sunny forest, no autumn colors, no magical glowing trees, no elven architecture, no tropical jungle, no clear-cut logging area, no futuristic elements, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no monsters prominently displayed.
```
