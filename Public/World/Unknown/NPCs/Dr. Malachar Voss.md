## Appearance
Pale, gangly human man with wild grey-streaked hair and dark circles under his eyes. Wears a stained leather apron over rumpled clothes. Fingers are discolored from chemicals. Adjusts spectacles that are perpetually crooked. Smells of vinegar and something unidentifiable.

## What We Know
A self-proclaimed alchemical genius who claims to be on the verge of "creating life." Sets up makeshift workshops in basements, alleys, and abandoned buildings. Expelled from some academy he won't name. Locals mostly consider him a harmless nuisance — loud, dramatic, and always underfoot.

## First Impressions
Immediately and overwhelmingly theatrical. Monologues about his genius before you've said a word. Seems completely harmless but absolutely exhausting.
