## Appearance
Before the betrayal: A kindly bald old man with a long gray beard, wearing fine red robes with gold inlay around the seams.

After the betrayal: Sunken eyes and hollow, gaunt cheeks. Bluish-green veins bulge against his bald head. His well-groomed beard is now thick with dirt and spit. His fine robes are dirty and filled with holes.

## What We Know
His home and current whereabouts are unknown to the party.

Although he portrayed himself as a kind old man, he is now recognized as an ancient and evil wizard who was sealed away by the hero Diego. His power was stolen from him and sealed within seven artifacts spread across the world.

## First Impressions
Disarmingly warm and helpful — almost too good to be true.

## Interactions

Welcomed the party to the world.

Stole the Seeker's Eye from the party and then commanded his Fly Demon to kill them.
