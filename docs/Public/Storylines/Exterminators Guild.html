Exterminators Guild

Summary

After the duplirat crisis in Highreach, the Exterminators Guild moved quickly to pull the party into its orbit. What looks like a reward may also be a test, and it now points straight toward Ardenville.

What the Party Knows

  • The Exterminators Guild praised the party after the Highreach rat crisis.
  • Don Gnocianno is the guildmaster who offered them membership and work.
  • The current induction task is a sealed package delivery tied to Exterminators Guild business in Ardenville.
  • The package is heavier and more carefully handled than normal pest-control supplies.
  • The party's choice to deliver, inspect, or refuse it will shape their standing with the guild.

Key People

  • Don Gnocianno - flamboyant guildmaster and quest giver.
  • Pip Whiskerwind - senior guild figure tied to Ardenville business.

Key Places

  • Highreach - where the guild first approached the party.
  • Ardenville - destination of the current delivery and next major pressure point.

Open Questions

  • What is actually inside the sealed package?
  • Is the guild offering honest work, or something more dangerous?
  • Who is waiting for the delivery in Ardenville?
  • Is this really an induction, or a loyalty test?
