Overview

A picturesque wine-making village of about 400 souls nestled in the Crestfall countryside. Rolling vineyards stretch across gentle hillsides, and the scent of fermenting grapes hangs in the air. Lately, a mysterious blight threatens the harvest.

Geography

  • Rolling hillsides covered with vineyards
  • Rich, fertile soil ideal for viticulture
  • Countryside setting within the Crestfall Kingdom

Infrastructure

  • Extensive vineyard estates and wine cellars
  • Village center with shops and a guildhall
  • Well-maintained roads connecting to Highreach

Economy

  • Viticulture and wine production drive the local economy
  • Renowned for heritage grape varieties and vintage wines
  • Recent vineyard blight threatens the harvest season
