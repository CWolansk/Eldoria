Muscular and sturdy with a square jaw, short blonde military-cut hair, and steel-gray eyes. Always in polished town guard mail with the Stonehaven wolf emblem.

Captain of the Stonehaven Town Guard. Came from the Highreach city guard five years ago and professionalized the local militia into a disciplined unit. Strict, honor-bound, and fiercely protective of the town's citizens.
