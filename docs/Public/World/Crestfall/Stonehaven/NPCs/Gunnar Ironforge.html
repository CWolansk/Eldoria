Gunnar Ironforge

Region
Crestfall
Location
Stonehaven
Status
Alive
Profession
Blacksmith

Appearance

Broad-shouldered even for a dwarf. Thick brown beard streaked with gray, often singed. Soot-stained leather apron, powerful arms covered in small burns and scars. Sharp blue eyes.

What We Know

Runs Ironforge Smithy on Stonehaven's eastern edge. Came from the Ironpeak Mountains thirty years ago. Creates weapons, armor, and traps for the town guard and rangers. Gruff but devoted to his craft.

First Impressions

Blunt and no-nonsense — judges people by how they handle tools, not by how they talk.
