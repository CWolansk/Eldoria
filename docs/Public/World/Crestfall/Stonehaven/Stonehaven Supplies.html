Stonehaven Supplies

Region

Stonehaven

Description

A well-maintained wooden building on the main street with large windows displaying goods. Organized aisles with clearly marked sections make shopping efficient. A polished counter with ledger books and a merchant's scale.

Proprietor

Erik Timber — friendly, organized, and community-minded

Services & Wares

  • Fresh produce (seasonal), preserved foods, beverages
  • Farming tools, crafting supplies, household items
  • Camping equipment, rope, bedrolls, travel rations
  • Basic hunting gear and simple weapons
  • Bulletin board for local notices and trade opportunities

Reputation

Serves virtually every household in Stonehaven. Erik extends credit to reliable customers and genuinely cares about keeping the community supplied.
