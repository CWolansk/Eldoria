Description

A robust stone-and-timber workshop on Stonehaven's eastern edge. The constant ring of hammer on anvil echoes all day, and the glow of multiple forges can be seen through reinforced windows.

Proprietor

Gunnar Ironforge — gruff dwarven smith, 30 years in Stonehaven

Services & Wares

  • Custom weapons and armor (superior quality, slightly higher cost)
  • Armor repair and modification
  • Trap creation for hunting and defense
  • Specialized ranger equipment and arrowheads
  • Tools for miners, craftsmen, and hunters

Reputation

The frontier's most reliable blacksmith. Everything Gunnar makes is built to last.
