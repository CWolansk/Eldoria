Location

Highreach Mines

Region
Crestfall

Description

An abandoned iron mine carved into the mountainside beneath Highreach. The entrance is dark and cold, with old rail tracks disappearing into the blackness.

Known Features

  • Multi-level mine, descending deep into the mountain
  • Iron ore veins long depleted — abandoned by dwarves and men years ago
  • Goblin activity reported in the upper levels

Rumors

  • Merchants on the road near Highreach have been attacked — some blame creatures from the mines
  • The Adventurers Guild has taken notice and posted jobs related to the problem
  • Some say the deeper levels hold something far worse than goblins
