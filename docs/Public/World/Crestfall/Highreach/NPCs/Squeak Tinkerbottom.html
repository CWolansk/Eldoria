Appearance

Young gnome with ink-stained fingers and wild, unkempt hair. Always tinkering with some gadget or scribbling diagrams.

What We Know

Squeak manages equipment and supplies for the Exterminators Guild. She's a talented artificer who builds custom pest-control devices and is always eager to test new inventions. She takes a keen interest in adventurers' gear.

First Impressions

Bubbling with nervous energy — talks fast, asks a dozen questions about your equipment before you can answer the first.
