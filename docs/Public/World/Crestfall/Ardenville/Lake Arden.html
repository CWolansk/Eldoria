Location

Lake Arden

Region
Crestfall

Description

A vast briny lake in northern Crestfall, shrouded in persistent mist. Kelp-choked shallows along the northern shore form a natural breakwater near Ardenville. Visibility drops sharply past the shallows.

Known Features

  • Rich fishing grounds sustaining the region's economy
  • Watchtowers along the shoreline for navigation and defense
  • Southern cliff face connecting to Highreach via a cargo elevator
  • Natural breakwater of kelp-choked shallows near Ardenville

Rumors

  • Strange creatures lurk in the deeper waters — sailors speak of serpents and worse
  • The mist grows denser at the center of the lake; some boats that venture too far don't return
  • Fishermen avoid certain kelp beds, whispering about eels the size of a man
