Appearance

Cheerful halfling with salt-and-pepper curls, flour-dusted apron, and an infectious laugh. Smells faintly of fish stew and driftwood smoke.

What We Know

Innkeeper of The Netted Nymph for twenty years. Knows everyone in Ardenville and most of their business. Connects people with work, keeps sailors fed, and runs the town's unofficial message service.

First Impressions

Warm and welcoming — has a bowl of stew in front of you before you've finished sitting down. Knows more than she lets on.
