Description

Low-beamed ceilings, thick stone walls, three driftwood hearths. Fishing nets and glass floats hang from the ceiling. The legendary fish stew — simmering for thirty-seven years straight — fills the air.

Proprietor

Mira Softstep — cheerful halfling innkeeper who knows everyone's business

Services & Wares

  • Lodging: shared room 8 cp/night, private room 3 sp/night
  • The perpetual fish stew (2 cp/bowl)
  • Meals, local ales, imported wines, hot cider
  • Job board for fishing crews and odd jobs
  • Message service — leave and pick up mail

Reputation

The heart and soul of Ardenville. Mira's stew is famous along the entire lakeshore. Start a fight and you're banned for a month — the regulars enforce it.
