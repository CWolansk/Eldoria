Region

Ironpeak Mountains

Overview

The dwarven homeland of the Ironpeak Mountains rises in jagged granite peaks veined with precious metals and rare gems. Master craftsmen forge legendary weapons and armor in halls carved from living rock, while the clang of hammers echoes through deep tunnels.

Geography

  • Massive mountain range with rich mineral deposits — gold, iron, adamantine, mithral, gemstones
  • Natural hot springs near Steamhammer
  • Treacherous eastern slopes where hostile tribes roam
  • Deep caverns beneath ForgePeak with dangerous creatures

Known Settlements

  • ForgePeak — dwarven capital, seat of Lord Thane Thronin Stonefist
  • Gemstone Hollow — gemcutting and jewelry village
  • Ironroot — ancient shrine village with dwarven monks
  • Steamhammer — hot springs village
  • Stoneforge — mining village
