FrostGlade Image Prompt

Prompt

Create an elven capital city for a DND campaign in the style of mature, serious fantasy concept art. It should be an intricate hand-painted illustration with sophisticated, realistic detailing, strictly avoiding any cartoonish or exaggerated proportions. High oblique bird's-eye view, readable city layout, elevated watercolor-and-ink style concept art, highly detailed but not photorealistic.

This is FrostGlade, the capital of the Silverleaf Lands, an arcane sanctuary where elven architecture, sacred nature, and winter light blend seamlessly. Show the viewer looking down from above at a high oblique angle over a snow-dusted enchanted forest — but the city must be clearly visible and readable. Elven halls, crystal walkways, plazas, and residential districts should stand out in the gaps between ancient trees and along illuminated paths, not hidden behind the canopy. The city fills most of the frame, with the enchanted forest giving way to distant winter mountains at the edges.

Most important: FrostGlade must unmistakably read as a full capital city, not one palace in a magical forest. Show a broad urban footprint with 100 or more distinct structures spread across multiple elven districts: a central ceremonial district, academic and library quarters, residential groves, shrine precincts, bridges, plazas, and enough homes, halls, and secondary crystal-lit paths that the city clearly feels populated and complete.

Use sweeping Art Nouveau architecture with organic curves adapted for extreme cold, featuring magical glasshouses, geothermal heating, and deep snow. White stone, frost-glass, and luminous crystal pathways, ice sculptures, sacred groves, crystal-clear springs, reflective pools, and elegant towers with glowing runes. The centerpiece is a sprawling Art Nouveau palace complex of white stone and arching frost-glass, radiating light-blue magical warmth into the snowy air. Around it, include arcane academies, libraries, shrine groves, crystal bridges, and a majestic Adventurers Guild hall of white stone and enchanted wood.

The city should not feel like a dense human metropolis, but it must not disappear into forest either. Leave room for ancient trees, open courtyards, glowing paths, and ritual spaces between districts, while keeping every building and path clearly readable. FrostGlade should feel otherworldly and serene, not like a human fortress-city or a dwarven industrial capital. Snow should rest lightly on rooftops and branches, while crystal lamps and magical ambience illuminate the scene with cool silver-blue light. The whole city should convey harmony between civilization, magic, and nature.

Mood: an air of elven superiority and comfort. Snowy weather. Use a palette of brilliant whites and light blues, luminous crystals, and touches of warm magical heating. No readable text, no banners with words, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.

Negative Prompt

No cartoonish style, no simple vector art, no exaggerated proportions, no comic book style, no gritty human slums, no industrial smoke, no massive stone walls dominating the image, no dark evil fortress, no tropical forest, no futuristic architecture, no photorealism, no anime style, no single palace with only a few surrounding buildings, no empty forest with one landmark, no readable text, no shop signs with lettering.
