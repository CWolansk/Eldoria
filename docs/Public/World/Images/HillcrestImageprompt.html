Hillcrest Image Prompt

Prompt

Create a medieval fantasy wine village for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable village layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is Hillcrest, a picturesque, world-renowned wine-making village of about 400 people nestled deeply in a lush valley of the Crestfall countryside. Show the viewer looking down so the village center fills the vibrant valley floor, completely surrounded by perfectly manicured, endlessly rolling hills. The terraced vineyards cascade down the surrounding hillsides toward the village in sweeping, beautiful geometric patterns. The settlement should take up most of the image.

Most important: Hillcrest must feature a stunning, deeply historical visual hook that makes the viewer say "Wow," grounded in master craftsmanship rather than overt magic. The centerpiece is the "Grand Press," a magnificent, palace-sized Tudor-style chateau and abbey at the center of the valley floor. It features colossal, multi-story oak wine casks built seamlessly into its heavy timber and bright stone architecture, and is fed by a beautiful network of elevated wooden aqueducts that channel fresh spring water down from the surrounding hills into open-air fermentation vats and courtyard fountains.

The village itself radiates outward from the Grand Press across the valley floor. Show 30-50 Tudor-style wood and stone homes, circular stone wine cellars, cooperages, and workers' cottages lining charming cobbled streets. The rolling hills enclosing the town are blanketed in lush, sun-drenched vineyards, vividly demonstrating the immense scale of their famous wine production. Hillcrest should feel incredibly wealthy, rustic, and masterfully engineered, not like a generic or poor farm town.

Because Hillcrest is under threat, show a few darkened blight patches creeping down the surrounding hillsides toward the valley village: unnatural purple-black vines, sickly ash-grey soil, and corrosive discoloration that sharply contrasts with the beautiful, sun-warmed, golden-green landscape. The blight must look like a direct, creeping threat descending upon the pristine vineyards and the Grand Press. Mood: a feeling of justice and order, clear sunny day illuminating the valley, bright blue and gold banners popping against the green vines and Tudor architecture, with a faint ominous undertone from the blight. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.

Negative Prompt

No giant city walls, no harbor, no desert, no tropical vineyard, no modern farm machinery, no photorealism, no anime style, no isolated farmhouse scene, no tiny hamlet with almost no buildings, no readable text, no written signs.
