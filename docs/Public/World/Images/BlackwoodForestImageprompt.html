Blackwood Forest Image Prompt

Prompt

Create a dark ancient forest for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable landscape layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is the Blackwood Forest, a dark and ancient woodland northeast of the frontier outpost of Stonehaven, sprawling across the central borderlands between Crestfall, the Ironpeak Mountains to the north, and the Orcish Wastes to the east. Show the viewer looking down from above at a vast, dense forest canopy stretching across the landscape. The canopy should be so thick and interwoven that almost no forest floor is visible — an unbroken sea of dark twisted branches and deep green-black foliage. The forest should fill most of the image, making its immense scale clear.

Most important: the Blackwood must feel genuinely old, oppressive, and dangerous — not a pleasant woodland. From above, the canopy should look gnarled and ancient, with enormous tree crowns far larger than normal trees, their branches tangled together into a suffocating mat. A narrow path should be barely visible as a faint dark line cutting through gaps in the canopy, disappearing deeper into the forest. Show hints of danger readable from above: a thin column of smoke from an abandoned camp visible through a canopy gap, a clearing where something large has flattened the undergrowth, and pale mist pooling in low areas between the trees.

The southwestern edge should show the cleared perimeter of Stonehaven — the palisade walls, stumps, and the abrupt transition from open ground to dense forest. The forest should darken and thicken toward the northeast, with the oldest and largest trees deep in the interior. Beyond the forest to the north, distant mountain peaks (Ironpeak range) should be faintly visible on the horizon. To the southwest, the open farmland of Crestfall contrasts sharply with the forest's edge.

Mood: foreboding, ancient, and vast. Overcast sky casting flat gray light over the canopy, with mist pooling visibly in low clearings. Use a palette of deep shadow greens, dark browns, charcoal blacks, and rare sickly-pale patches where light reaches through. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.

Negative Prompt

No bright sunny forest, no autumn colors, no magical glowing trees, no elven architecture, no tropical jungle, no clear-cut logging area, no futuristic elements, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no monsters prominently displayed.
