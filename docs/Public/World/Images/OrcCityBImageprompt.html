Orc City B Image Prompt

Prompt

Create an orcish wasteland city for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable city layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is Orc City B, an orcish fortress-city on the edge of a desolate salt lake in the Orcish Wastes. Show the viewer looking down over a fortified settlement sprawling across cracked, sun-baked earth beside a flat, shimmering, lifeless salt lake. Sparse, tough desert scrub and twisted dry vegetation cling to the outskirts. Utilitarian yurt camps with sprawling hide awnings and jagged bone and iron stakes anchor the layout. The centerpiece is a colossal, multi-tiered command yurt stitched from scorched orange hides, raised on thick iron stakes at the heart of the settlement. The city fills most of the image, with the sad, glittering salt flat and dry cracked wasteland visible at the edges.

Most important: Orc City B must clearly read as a complete fortress-city, not one fort beside a lake. Show 80 or more structures spread across the dusty terrain — multiple districts, secondary streets, living quarters, war yards, a marketplace strip, and defensive perimeter towers so the whole place feels populated, urban, and strategically vital.

The city should feel like a lifeline settlement for a warlike culture, not a random camp. Include crowded barracks, beast pens, forge yards, marketplaces under hide awnings, training grounds on the flatter ground, and rough masonry built from scavenged ancient ruins and local stone. The salt lake should look flat, white-crusted, and inhospitable. Beyond the settlement walls, the land is dry, hostile, and windswept, with red-brown earth, broken ridges, and patches of scraggly desert vegetation.

Mood: an atmosphere of raw, unyielding brutality. Blistering hot desert weather. The muddy river cuts through the blistering heat. Use scorched reds and intense oranges, dusty air, and jagged desert stakes. Keep it grounded and coherent, with strong urban structure and obvious survival logic. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.

Negative Prompt

No comedy orcs, no random tent camp with no structure, no gore, no sci-fi wasteland, no photorealism, no anime style, no single stronghold with almost no surrounding city, no tiny camp pretending to be a city, no readable text, no written signs.
