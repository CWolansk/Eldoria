Steamhammer Image Prompt

Prompt

Create a dwarven hot-springs tourist town for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable town layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is Steamhammer, a bustling dwarven tourist town in the Ironpeak Mountains, built to profit from the spectacular natural hot springs visible in the nearby mountain valley just beyond the town. Show the viewer looking down over the town filling the foreground and middle ground, with the steaming, beautiful natural hot springs pools clearly visible in the rocky valley just past the town's edge, steam rising dramatically from the landscape. The town exists entirely because of those springs.

Most important: Steamhammer must clearly read as a thriving, money-hungry tourist trap town designed to separate visitors from their gold. Show 25-45 grand Brutalist stone buildings: impressive multi-story hotels with steaming hot fountains in their courtyards, bustling merchant shops selling souvenirs and bathing supplies, packed taverns and restaurants, a caravan station where wagons offer rides out to the natural springs, and exclusive walled-off premium spa resorts that pipe hot spring water directly into private baths via heavy iron and copper pipes running from the springs into the town.

Water is the main visual feature of the entire town. Grand, steaming hot-water fountains dominate the major intersections and plazas as tourist attractions. Channels of warm water run alongside the main streets. The architecture is imposingly grand Brutalist — heavy, thick-walled, impressively scaled stone and iron buildings designed to awe visitors with dwarven engineering mastery, featuring ornate copper pipe-work, polished stone facades, and warm glowing lanterns. The town should feel like it exists purely to impress, entertain, and monetize every tourist who walks through.

Mood: a clean, bright, cheerful, and inviting atmosphere like a fantasy theme park. Clear, beautiful weather with bright sunlight illuminating the polished stone and sparkling water features. Use warm golds, rich coppers, clean gray stone, and crystal-clear steaming turquoise water. Thick plumes of clean white steam rise cheerfully from fountains and pipes throughout the town against a bright blue mountain sky. The whole town should feel like a meticulously maintained, happy, welcoming fantasy resort that happens to be built by master dwarven engineers. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.

Negative Prompt

No dark gloomy atmosphere, no rain, no storm, no mud, no soot, no grimy industrial look, no hot springs inside the town center, no natural pools in the middle of town, no giant capital city, no lava rivers, no tropical spa resort, no futuristic bath complex, no photorealism, no anime style, no isolated bathhouse scene, no tiny hamlet with almost no buildings, no readable text, no written signs.
