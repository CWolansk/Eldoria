Gemstone Hollow Image Prompt

Prompt

Create a dwarven gemcraft village for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable village layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is Gemstone Hollow, a dwarven village specializing in gemcutting and jewelry-making in the Ironpeak Mountains. Show the viewer looking down into a sheltered mountain ravine where the village fills the canyon floor and climbs carved terraces up both rock walls. The sparkle of cut gems and polished stone catches the light throughout the scene. The settlement should fill most of the image, with rugged mountain ridges and sparse pine forest visible at the upper edges.

Most important: Gemstone Hollow must clearly read as a complete dwarven craft village, not just one gem workshop. Show 30-45 homes, work halls, gemcutting studios, storage rooms, market courts, carved lanes, bridges, and residential terraces so the viewer sees a full lived-in settlement.

The focus should be craftsmanship rather than heavy industry. The architecture is hardcore Brutalist, prioritizing function over form. The centerpiece is a massive, blocky Brutalist gem-sorting hall of black iron and sharp red stone, slick with the pouring rain. Surrounding it are blocky concrete and metal workshops and stone houses built into the mountain slope, narrow bridges, small mine entrances, and market courtyards where dwarven artisans display intricate jewelry and gemwork. Use blocky black concrete, red iron, crystal lanterns, and gem-colored highlights to make the village visually distinct.

Gemstone Hollow should feel prosperous, precise, and beautiful in a dwarven way — not like a heavy industrial forge town or a generic cave. Mood: a vibe of relentless hard work and grit. Severely rainy and windy weather. Use intense black and red colors, wet blocky concrete, and the sparkle of gems. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.

Negative Prompt

No giant molten forge metropolis, no generic cave, no tropical village, no futuristic lighting, no photorealism, no anime style, no isolated workshop with no surrounding village, no tiny hamlet with almost no buildings, no readable text, no written signs.
