ForgePeak Image Prompt

Prompt

Create a dwarven capital city for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable city layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is ForgePeak, the capital of the Ironpeak Mountains, carved from living rock deep inside a colossal mountain. Show the viewer looking through the mountain's monumental front gate into a vast interior cavern that stretches back and upward, as if standing at the entrance and seeing the entire city laid out before them in one grand vista. The massive cavern ceiling arches high above, supported by carved pillar-mountains of stone, and the city fills the cavern floor and terraced walls from edge to edge. It should feel monumental, engineered, and ancient.

Most important: ForgePeak must clearly read as a full dwarven capital, not one forge hall in a cave. Show a vast interior metropolis with 100 or more distinct halls, homes, bridges, stairways, workshops, vaults, clan quarters, and civic structures filling every terrace and level of the cavern. Multiple districts should be clearly visible: an industrial forge district lit by massive clusters of glowing crystals sourced from Gemstone Hollow, residential clan quarters carved into the walls, a grand central plaza, and commercial levels connected by heavy stone bridges and broad stairways.

The centerpiece is a colossal Brutalist fortress-forge of dark concrete and jagged black iron, glowing with red furnace light through the lashing rain. Nearby, the Adamant Anvil serves as a master smithy and armory with immense hearths, anvils, weapon racks, and masterwork craftsmanship on display. Deep veins and massive exposed clusters of glowing crystals mined from Gemstone Hollow are embedded throughout the lower industrial terraces, providing power and casting a radiant light up across the city's dark stone faces.

The city should feel like the heart of dwarven industry and power, not like a natural cave with a few buildings inside. Every surface should show hardcore Brutalist architecture prioritizing function over form, built from blocky concrete, stone, and metal. The monumental gate at the front of the image should frame the scene, with severely rainy and windy weather spilling in from behind the viewer, contrasting with the warm forge glow deep inside.

Mood: a vibe of relentless hard work and grit. Severely rainy and windy weather outside the gate. Use intense black and red colors, brutal blocky concrete, black iron, forge smoke, and the radiant glow of subterranean crystals. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.

Negative Prompt

No steampunk gears everywhere, no sci-fi machinery, no generic natural cave with no city structure, no bright modern lighting, no tropical elements, no photorealism, no anime style, no single forge chamber with a few buildings, no empty cave with one throne hall, no readable text, no written signs.
