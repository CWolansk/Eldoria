Highreach Image Prompt

Prompt

Create a medieval fantasy capital city for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable city layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is Highreach, the capital of Crestfall, an awe-inspiring city perched atop a natural plateau with steep cliffs on three sides. Show a circular fortified city crowning the plateau, built in a magnificent Tudor style with bright stone and heavy dark timber. A majestic Tudor-style royal castle crowns the central high ground, draped in brilliant blue and gold banners. The viewer should see the entire plateau city from above, with the cliffs falling away dramatically and the wider countryside visible beyond. The city itself should fill most of the image, making the urban layout clear.

Most important: Highreach must clearly read as a full capital city, not one castle on a hill. Show a broad urban footprint with 100 or more distinct structures spread across multiple districts: a noble district with elegant stone homes, a merchant district, a blacksmith district, and wide main roads. The city should feel dense and packed within its circular walls.

The city should feel wealthy, orderly, and busy. Include a castle complex, a large central plaza, broad main thoroughfares, a noble district with elegant stone homes and gardens, a merchant district full of stalls and guild buildings, and a blacksmith district marked by multiple forges and smoke. Make the skyline varied and memorable with the Highreach Adventurers Guild, the refined Merchants Guild hall, the Arcane Enclave, a dwarven forge district, and busy market roads lined with banners, awnings, and colorful rooftops.

Highreach should read as the strongest human city in the region: ancient fortifications mixed with living commerce, clean district divisions, and a clear sense of hierarchy from the royal castle down to the packed commercial quarters. Mood: a feeling of justice and order, bright, prosperous, elevated, and grand. Clear sunny day. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.

Negative Prompt

No harbor town, no lowland village, no ruined city, no futuristic elements, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no random modern buildings.
