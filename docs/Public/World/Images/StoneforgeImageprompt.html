Stoneforge Image Prompt

Prompt

Create a dwarven mining village for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, high oblique bird's-eye view, readable village layout, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is Stoneforge, a proud and thriving dwarven mining *town* in the Ironpeak Mountains. This is not a dreary labor camp; it is a joyful, bustling community where dwarven miners and their families live with immense pride, supplying the finest metals to the capital's master blacksmiths. Show the viewer looking down over a broad, sunlit mountain ledge where the town sprawls in beautifully organized geometric terraces. Impeccably carved mine entrances are framed by grand stone arches behind the town. The settlement should take up most of the image, with the majestic mountain face rising behind and a sweeping alpine valley dropping away in front.

Most important: Stoneforge must clearly read as a pristine, happy, and masterfully engineered dwarven family town, not a messy industrial slum or a crude work camp. Show a sprawling, brilliantly organized civic layout with 35-60 buildings including finely carved family stone-homes, bustling town squares, lively taverns, pristine workshops, and grand avenues.

Dwarven engineering here is a breathtaking work of art. The architecture is a showcase of flawless stonemasonry, gleaming metal trim, and geometric perfection, entirely lacking in mud, soot, or crude industry. The centerpiece is a magnificent, gleaming transit station and ore-lift built of polished bronze, perfectly cut granite, and warm glowing lanterns, operating with flawless clockwork precision in the center of the town square. Everything about the town screams master-craftsmanship, wealth, and vibrant community pride.

Mood: A vibe of immense dwarven pride, happiness, and masterful engineering. Clear, crisp mountain air with bright, cheerful sunlight illuminating the majestic stonework. Use a warm, inviting palette of rich slate grays, gleaming bronze, bright gold, and the cheerful glow of hearths. No mud, no soot, no dreary rain, no depressing industrial gloom. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.

Negative Prompt

No mud, no soot, no dreary labor camps, no gloomy rain, no crude or messy industry, no chaotic slums, no depressing industrial gloom, no giant royal city, no magical crystal utopia, no tropical setting, no futuristic mining machines, no photorealism, no anime style, no isolated mine with no village around it, no tiny hamlet with almost no buildings, no readable text, no written signs.
