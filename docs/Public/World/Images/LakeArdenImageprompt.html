Lake Arden Image Prompt

Prompt

Create a vast misty lake for a DND campaign in the style of a clean hand-painted fantasy illustration, wide panoramic eye-level perspective from the shoreline, watercolor-and-ink concept art, detailed but not photorealistic.

This is Lake Arden, a vast briny lake in Crestfall. The camera point of view is standing on the far eastern shore of the lake, looking straight ahead (west) out across the center of the water toward the distant sea. The layout must follow exact left, right, and center placements.

On the left side of the image (the southern shore of the lake), there must be a massive steep natural plateau rising dramatically from the waterline. Atop this plateau sits a large city ringed by high stone walls. At the base of this plateau by the water, show a small cliffside port with a cargo elevator.

On the right side of the image (the northern shore of the lake), the land forms a coastline holding a bustling fishing town. Wooden piers, docks, and clustered buildings stretch along the waterline. Tall wooden defensive watchtowers are positioned along this right-side shore, with their tops disappearing into thick, low-hanging fog.

In the center of the image, the dark, greenish-gray water stretches out toward the horizon, leading to a small outlet out into the sea, which is partially obscured by thick rolling mist. The water itself is active: scatter several wooden fishing boats across the lake with people actively fishing and pulling nets. In the immediate foreground shallows right in front of the camera, show dark tangled masses of kelp, with the shadowy silhouettes of fish just barely visible gliding beneath the water's surface.

Mood: atmospheric, vast, and eerie yet inhabited. Overcast and misty, with diffused light creating no clear shadows. Use a palette of slate grays, deep blue-greens, misty whites, and the warm brown of wooden docks and boats as the only warm accent. No readable text, no signs, no labels, no lettering anywhere in the image.

Negative Prompt

No bird's-eye view, no extreme aerial shots, no tropical blue lagoon, no clear sunny water, no small pond, no ocean with huge crashing waves, no sandy beach resort, no futuristic boats, no photorealism, no anime style, no readable text, no written signs, no giant sea monsters bursting from water.
