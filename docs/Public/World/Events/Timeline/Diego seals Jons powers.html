Diego Seals Jons Powers

Region
Orcish Wastes

Orcish Wastes

What Happened

  • During the Wizarding Wars, the hero Diego defeated the lich Jon the Summoner
  • Diego sealed Jon's power into artifacts and scattered them across Eldoria
  • The artifacts were protected so only someone from Diego's world could unlock them

Key Takeaways

  • The artifacts the party seeks are fragments of the lich's sealed power
  • This is why the lich has been summoning heroes from another world
