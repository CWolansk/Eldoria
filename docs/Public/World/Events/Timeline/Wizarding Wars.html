What We Know

An ancient conflict among powerful wizards that devastated parts of Eldoria. The wars ended when Diego sealed the powers of the last surviving wizard — Summoner Jon.

Key Facts

  • Multiple wizards fought for supremacy across the land
  • Diego, a legendary hero, ended the wars by sealing Jon's powers
  • Jon is the last surviving wizard from the conflict
  • The party learned about this history during their adventures
