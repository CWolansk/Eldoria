Mine History Discovered at Library

Region
Crestfall
Location
Highreach

What Happened

  • The party researched the Highreach Mines at the Adventurers Guild library
  • Learned the mines were a joint dwarf-human operation that was abandoned ~10 years ago when the iron vein ran dry
  • The mines are 3 levels deep and once had a mithral vein
  • Dwarves and humans fought over ore rights — dwarves believe all underground metal is part of their heritage

Key Takeaways

  • The dwarven kingdom's dislike of the human king stems partly from this dispute
  • Mithral was once mined here — could still be found deeper
