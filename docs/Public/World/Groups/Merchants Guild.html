Merchants Guild

Region
Crestfall
Location
Highreach

Overview

The primary trade organization in Highreach, overseeing merchant licensing, market regulations, and trade route coordination across Crestfall.

Reputation

Powerful and well-connected. Membership is practically required for serious commerce in the region. Some grumble about fees, but few challenge the guild openly.

Known Members

  • Alistair Goldman — Guildmaster
  • Evelyn Marroway — Receptionist

Services

  • Merchant licensing and trade permits
  • Access to guild-regulated trade routes
  • Dispute mediation between merchants
  • Market stall and warehouse access
