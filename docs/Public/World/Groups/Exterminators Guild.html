Overview

A gnome-operated pest control guild based in Highreach. Founded by gnome refugees, the guild has grown into the city's primary solution for infestations, tunnel maintenance, and urban wildlife management.

Reputation

Efficient and professional. Recently expanded membership beyond gnomes, earning praise for inclusivity. Well-regarded by city officials and property owners.

Known Members

  • Don Gnocianno — Guildmaster
  • Brix Ironwhistle — Captain of Security

Services

  • Rat and pest extermination
  • Preventive maintenance inspections
  • Emergency pest response
  • Tunnel and sewer mapping
  • Specialized creature removal
