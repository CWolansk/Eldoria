Store

The Whispering Weave

Region
SilverleafLands
Location
FrostGlade

The shop occupies a graceful platform suspended between three massive silver-bark trees. Translucent curtains of enchanted silk hang from branches, shifting colors from silver to blue to deep purple and whispering when the wind passes.

Proprietor

Lysara Starweaver (High Elf, Wizard/Artificer) — one of the finest arcane clothiers in the Silverleaf Lands. 800-year family tradition of enchanted weaving.

Services & Wares

  • Robe of Useful Items (500 gp)
  • Cloak of Protection (1,000 gp)
  • Cloak of Elvenkind (800 gp — cultural item, requires approval)
  • Boots of Elvenkind (500 gp)
  • Masterwork arcane focuses: wands, staffs, orbs (50-200 gp)
  • Blank spellbooks (50-100 gp)
  • Custom enchantment on existing gear (300-1,500 gp, 1-4 weeks)
  • Attunement consultation (25 gp)

Reputation

Lysara has strong opinions about proper magical attunement. She won't sell her best work to someone she deems unworthy, and she never rushes for anyone.
