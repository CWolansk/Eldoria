Appearance

Tall, shirtless wizard with an oiled chest and gold rings on every finger. Wears a fur-lined cloak draped over one shoulder and low-slung trousers. Speaks with a thick Germanic accent.

What We Know

Leader of a group called the Sexy Wizard Gang. They've been spotted in various towns across the region causing trouble. Günther seems to fancy himself a charming rogue — heavy on the charm, light on the rogue.

First Impressions

Absurdly confident. Flexes while talking. The accent is real but the showmanship is cranked to eleven.
