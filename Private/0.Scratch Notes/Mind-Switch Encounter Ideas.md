# Mind-Switch Encounter Ideas

## Concept
A "Freaky Friday" encounter where the party's minds swap into each other's bodies, forcing players to literally swap character sheets at the table. Builds on the Session 12 slime clone experience where players already inhabited copied bodies.

## Vault Connections

- **Session 12 residue** — Jon's simulacrums used Modify Memory + living ooze. The party already experienced "waking up in a wrong body." A mind-swap is the natural escalation.
- **Jon the Summoner** has demonstrated body-manipulation magic and is actively studying the party. He summoned them, cloned them — learning how their minds work is a logical next step.
- **Lady Elara Moonshadow** (Highreach) is an **Enchantress** — mind magic is her domain. Natural "fix it" NPC for reversing a swap.
- **Hilda Ravenshadow** (Stonehaven) specializes in divination and curse-breaking — alternate cure path.
- **JP and Justin are both cursed** (can't taste). Whoever inherits their body inherits the curse — immediate mechanical consequence.
- **JP faces racial prejudice** as a half-orc. A non-orc player in JP's body would experience that discrimination firsthand. Huge RP moment.
- **Randi is Aarakocra** — whoever gets Randi's body can fly. Randi in a human body *can't*. Big mechanical/identity shift.
- **Claire is Water Genasi** — underwater breathing transfers with the body. Relevant if the boat race is still in play.
- **Garrick Lightfinger** (Ardenville magic shop) "always has exactly the oddity you didn't know you needed" — could be the unwitting source of something cursed.
- **The boat race** — running it while mind-swapped would be chaos in the best way.

## Spark Options

### 1. Jon's Psychic Tethers
The simulacrum process from Session 12 left invisible psychic links between the party (Jon was mapping their neural pathways). He triggers them remotely — minds snap into each other's bodies. It's deliberate: Jon wants to see if they can function broken apart because he's building something *better* next time. Cure requires severing the tethers — finding whoever/whatever is anchoring them.

### 2. The Freaky Friday Wizard
A rogue transmuter, maybe a former student of Lady Elara's who went too far. Ambushes the party as a "field test" of consciousness-transfer research. Not evil exactly — just completely unethical. Watching from nearby, taking *notes*. Party has to track them down and force a reversal, but the wizard genuinely doesn't know if the spell *can* be reversed yet. Elara would be furious and helpful in equal measure.

### 3. Fey Crossing Prank
As the party approaches Silverleaf Lands, they pass through a fey-touched area. An Archfey or powerful fey trickster swaps them for entertainment — or as a "lesson." Won't reverse it until the party completes a task *in each other's bodies*, proving they understand each other. Comedy-forward, Saturday-morning-cartoon tone.

### 4. Artifact Trap
One of the remaining artifacts (Shadowstride Boots or Aetherstone Compass in Silverleaf Lands) has a psychic defense mechanism. When the party gets close, it scrambles their consciousnesses as a ward against thieves. Only way to claim the artifact is to solve the puzzle while mind-swapped. Swap reverses on success (or they find Elara/Hilda if they brute-force it).

### 5. The Lake Below Ardenville
During the boat race, ships pass over something ancient beneath the water. A dormant ward, sealed entity, or sunken elven relic pulses — party blacks out, waking up in each other's bodies mid-race, boats still moving, rivals still fighting. Immediate chaos, no time to process. The "why" unfolds after they survive the race and investigate.

## Comparison

| Spark | Tone | Duration | Best If You Want... |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jon's Tethers | Dark, escalating | Multi-session arc | Continuity with Session 12, advancing main villain |
| Freaky Friday Wizard | Comedic + tense | 1-2 sessions | Self-contained story with a quirky antagonist |
| Fey Crossing | Comedy-forward | 1 session | Lighthearted fun, break from main quest tension |
| Artifact Trap | Puzzle/cooperative | Part of a session | Baked into artifact quest, no new villain needed |
| Lake Below Ardenville | Chaotic, mysterious | 1 session + mystery hook | Immediate action during boat race + lore hook |
