# Merchants Guildmaster Regional Boss

## Core Idea

- [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Alistair Goldman|Alistair Goldman]], guildmaster of the [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Groups/Merchants Guild|Merchants Guild]], has secretly worked with [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Orcish Wastes/Wizards Tower/NPCs/Summoner Jon AKA the wizard|Summoner Jon]] from the beginning.
- His motivations remain unclear.
- Intended as a regional boss.

## Boss Gimmick

- Jon augmented Goldman with a slime's healing powers.
- Can tank nearly any hit and regenerate almost immediately.
- Ordinary base stats; his real combat strength comes from the large collection of magical items carried on his person.
- Fight identity: reveal and counter his items while finding a way to suppress or overwhelm his regeneration.
