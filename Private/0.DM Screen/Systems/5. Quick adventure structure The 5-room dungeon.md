# 

The **5-room dungeon** is a beat structure for a short adventure. It does not have to be an actual dungeon. It can be a heist, negotiation, wilderness trip, haunted house, royal banquet, dream sequence, or rescue mission.

The standard structure is:

1. **Entrance and guardian**
2. **Puzzle or roleplaying challenge**
3. **Trick or setback**
4. **Climax, battle, or major conflict**
5. **Reward, revelation, or twist**

Johnn Four’s guide presents the 5-room dungeon as a quick method for building sessions, adventures, and even campaigns, with those five rooms as the core format.

Use it when you need a session fast.

Example social version:

1. **Entrance and guardian:** The party must get into the masquerade.
2. **Puzzle/social challenge:** Identify which masked noble has the information.
3. **Trick/setback:** Their ally is exposed as a fraud.
4. **Climax:** Public debate, duel, arrest attempt, or blackmail exchange.
5. **Reward/twist:** They get the treaty, but learn the ambassador is already dead.

This is your “I need a playable session tonight” system.
