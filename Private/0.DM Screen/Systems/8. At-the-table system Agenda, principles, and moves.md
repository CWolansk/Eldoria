

This is your live DM brain.

Dungeon World formalizes GMing into **agenda, principles, and moves**: the agenda is what you are trying to accomplish, principles guide how you do it, and moves are the concrete things you say or do when play turns to you.

Even if you run D&D, this is extremely useful.

## Your table agenda

Keep this in front of you:

1. Make the world feel real.
2. Make the characters’ choices matter.
3. Present danger, opportunity, and consequence.
4. Play to find out what happens.

## Your default loop

Use this constantly:

1. **Describe the situation.**
2. **Ask, “What do you do?”**
3. **Resolve the action.**
4. **Show the changed situation.**
5. Repeat.

## GM moves when you are stuck

When players look at you, or when they fail, hesitate, or hand you an opportunity, do one of these:

- Reveal an unwelcome truth.
- Show signs of an approaching threat.
- Offer a hard bargain.
- Put someone in a spot.
- Separate them.
- Use up a resource.
- Turn their move back on them.
- Show the cost of success.
- Reveal a new opportunity.
- Advance a front or clock.

This prevents blank-page panic during play.
