# Merchants Guild Corruption

![[Public/Storylines/Merchants Guild Corruption]]

## DM Notes
- This thread is the party's direct conflict with Goldman rather than the entire guild's normal operations.
- The clean arc so far is job, betrayal, cover-up murder, bandit retaliation, then dockside influence in Ardenville.
- Ardenville is the next place where the guild can pressure the party without open war.

## Secrets
- Goldman used the assassins guild to kill Jarek after the party spared him.
- Guild fees and enforcement are partly a shakedown operation backed by the Ironbrow twins.
- Boat-race sponsorship and dock control let Goldman turn civic life into leverage.
- The guild's corruption can intersect with other criminal threads instead of remaining a standalone enemy.

## Future Plans
- Use the boat race and Ardenville warehouse to bring Goldman back onstage.
- Push the party toward choosing exposure, leverage, or negotiated coexistence.
- Tie Justin's dues and merchant standing to future pressure points.
- Let the Fisherman's Guild conflict sharpen the moral contrast.
