# Exterminators Guild

![[Public/Storylines/Exterminators Guild]]

## DM Notes
- This is the party's current organized-crime pressure valve disguised as guild advancement.
- The delivery to Ardenville is the cleanest early reveal point for the guild's true nature.
- Don is testing whether the party can be useful, pliable, or both.

## Secrets
- The Exterminators Guild is the gnome crime family's legitimate front.
- The package contains laundered money and criminal correspondence, not pest-control supplies.
- The party's heroic reputation is being used as cover for guild expansion.
- Internal tension already exists between Don's expansionism and Pip's stay-hidden instinct.

## Future Plans
- Force a decision at delivery: obey, investigate, or expose.
- Use Ardenville to show the guild's network extends beyond Highreach.
- Escalate into turf pressure with the Merchants Guild if the party keeps pulling on the thread.
- Offer morally useful criminal help to tempt the party into compromise.

## Vault Gaps
- Ratcatcher Finn is part of the induction job but has no canonical note yet.
- The quest file treats induction as still in progress, while Overall/event language already reads as a completed induction.
