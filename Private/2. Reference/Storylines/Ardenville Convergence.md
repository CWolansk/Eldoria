# Ardenville Convergence

![[Public/Storylines/Ardenville Convergence]]

## DM Notes
- This is an umbrella storyline for the campaign pivot into Ardenville rather than a single quest chain.
- Use it to track the place where JP's ship issue, Merchants Guild pressure, Exterminators delivery, and boat-race politics overlap.
- Ardenville works best as a hub where the party chooses which tension to touch first.

## Secrets
- The Exterminators package makes Ardenville a meeting point for multiple criminal networks.
- Goldman is using race sponsorship, dock fees, and warehouse control to expand influence on Lake Arden.
- JP's impounded vessel and the race prize create a personal stake inside the town's larger power struggle.
- The Fisherman's Guild conflict is a natural counterweight to merchant control and a likely party alliance point.
