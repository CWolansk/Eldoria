# The Artifact Wars

![[Public/Storylines/The Artifact Wars]]

## DM Notes
- Main campaign spine: Jon manipulated the party into recovering the first artifact for him.
- The current handoff is from Highreach fallout into the Silverleaf leg of the chase.
- The quest file already tracks the artifact sequence and should stay the mechanical source of truth.

## Secrets
- Jon is an ancient lich using the party because they come from Diego's world.
- Diego's artifacts are part of the seal on Jon's power, and Jon needs the full set to escalate his war plans.
- The next regions are already staged in outline form: Silverleaf, Ironpeak, then Orcish Wastes.

## Future Plans
- Silverleaf Lands: Shadowstride Boots and Aetherstone Compass.
- Ironpeak Mountains: Tempest Lantern and Obsidian Blade.
- Orcish Wastes: Crystal of True Sight and Celestial Shield.
- Jon escalation: more agents, experiments, and cross-kingdom pressure as the party closes in.
