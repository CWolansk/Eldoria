## Overview

A solemn but hopeful celebration symbolizing endurance and the return of light. Held in the darkest days of winter.

## Traditions

- Towns illuminated with lanterns and bonfires
- Families gather to share stories and gifts
- Honoring ancestors
- Offerings made — spirits believed to roam more freely this night
