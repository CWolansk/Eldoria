## Overview

As the final harvest is brought in, the people gather to honor the bounty of the earth. A time to reflect on the year's hardships and prepare for winter.

## Traditions

- Massive feasts in every town and village
- Best produce of the year on display
- Offerings made to the gods for protection during the cold months
