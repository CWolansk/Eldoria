## Overview

A personal and guild-wide celebration when a young blacksmith finishes their first true masterpiece. Considered a rite of passage for a dwarf.

## Traditions

- Blacksmith's forge blessed by the elders
- Work displayed in the grand halls of Forgepeak
- Competitions in forging — dwarves show off smithing and crafting skills
