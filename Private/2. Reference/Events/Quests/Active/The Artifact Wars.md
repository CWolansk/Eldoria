## Quest Overview

**Quest Giver**: [[Jon Brightman]] (lore reveal), legacy of [[Diego Comes to Eldoria|Diego]]
**Reward**: Artifacts needed to defeat [[Summoner Jon AKA the wizard|Jon the Summoner]]
**Status**: Active — 1 of 7 artifacts recovered (and stolen)

Race to collect 7 ancient artifacts before the lich Jon the Summoner uses them to break his confinement and conquer Eldoria. The artifacts were created by Diego, a hero from the party's world, who sealed Jon's power into them during the Wizarding Wars. Only someone from Diego's world can unlock them — which is why Jon summoned the party.

## Artifacts

| 2 | Aetherstone Compass | Ruined outpost, Silverleaf Lands | Not recovered | Guides safest path to Jon's stronghold |
| 3 | Tempest Lantern | Ancient forge, [[Ironpeak Mountains]] | Not recovered | Control weather |

| 5 | Crystal of True Sight | Ruins, [[Orcish Wastes]] | Not recovered | See through Jon's illusions |
| 6 | Celestial Shield | Secluded temple, Orcish Wastes | Not recovered | Protection from Jon's most powerful spells |

## Objectives
- Travel to the Silverleaf Lands and secure the Shadowstride Boots and Aetherstone Compass before Jon
- Journey to Ironpeak Mountains for the Tempest Lantern and Obsidian Blade
- Venture into the Orcish Wastes for the Crystal of True Sight and Celestial Shield
- Confront Jon the Summoner in his tower with all artifacts assembled

## Potential Outcomes

### Success
- Jon the Summoner defeated, artifacts secured, Eldoria safe

- Possible path home for the party

### Failure / Ignored

- Eldoria falls under lich rule
- Party trapped in a hostile world with no way home
