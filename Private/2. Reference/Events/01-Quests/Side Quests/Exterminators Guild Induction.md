## Quest Overview

**Quest Giver**: [[Don Gnocianno]] / [[Exterminators Guild]]  
**Reward**: Full Guild Membership, Rat-Themed Headdress (wondrous item granting 60ft darkvision, once-per-day advantage on smell-based Perception)  
**Status**: 🔄 **IN PROGRESS**  
**Difficulty**: Medium  

The party has been offered membership in the [[Exterminators Guild]] and has begun the induction ceremony. To complete their initiation, they must prove their worth by completing tasks assigned by [[Don Gnocianno]].

## What the Party Knows

**From Session 11:**
- The [[Exterminators Guild]] has offered them membership
- [[Don Gnocianno]] is the flamboyant guildmaster with a meticulously waxed rat-tail beard
- The guild handles pest control in [[Highreach]] and wants to expand services
- Party has been tasked with delivering a "special pest control shipment" to [[Ardenville]]
- The package is suspiciously heavy and carefully sealed
- Payment of 50 gp offered for what seems like a simple courier job

**What They May Not Know:**
- The guild is actually a front for the gnome crime family
- The package likely contains laundered money and criminal correspondence
- This delivery is a test of their willingness to become complicit in criminal activities
- Don Gnocianno specifically recruited them for their heroic reputation as cover

---

## The Package

**Description**: A sealed wooden crate, roughly the size of a wine case but noticeably heavier. Bears an official-looking Exterminators Guild seal.

**Official Story**: "Advanced rat control supplies" for expanding guild services to Ardenville.

**Actual Contents** (if investigated):
- Laundered money
- Coded criminal correspondence
- Documents establishing criminal network connections

**Investigation Options**:
- Investigation or Insight (DC 15): Detect inconsistencies in Don's story
- Sleight of Hand or Investigation (DC 16): Secretly examine package contents
- Magical examination (DC 14): Sense metallic contents (coins) and paper (documents)

## Potential Outcomes

### Complete Delivery (No Questions Asked)
- 50 gp payment received
- Full guild membership granted
- Don Gnocianno's trust established
- Future "special jobs" unlocked
- Party becomes unknowing (or knowing) criminal accomplices

### Investigate and Complete Anyway
- Same rewards as above
- Party aware of criminal nature
- Moral complexity: are they now complicit?
- Knowledge of guild's true operations

### Refuse or Report
- Guild membership revoked
- Don Gnocianno becomes suspicious/hostile
- Potential enemy made of gnome crime family
- Lose access to guild resources and tunnel network

### Open Package
- If detected: Don Gnocianno knows they can't be trusted
- If undetected: Party has evidence of criminal activities
- Decision point: expose guild or keep secret for leverage?

## DM Notes

### Moral Complexity
This quest deliberately places the party in a morally gray situation:
- They can remain ignorant and benefit from guild membership
- They can investigate and choose whether to continue
- Their choices establish their relationship with organized crime

### Future Implications
- Guild relationship will affect interactions in [[Highreach]] and beyond
- Criminal connections could be useful OR dangerous depending on campaign direction
- Don Gnocianno plays the long game—gradually involves them deeper

### Party Integration
