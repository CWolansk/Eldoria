## Quest Overview

**Quest Giver**: Self-initiated
**Status**: Completed

Justin's mule [[Dr. Brule the Mule]] bolted from Highreach after the invasion. Hoofprints along the main road lead toward the Ardenville crossroads. Recovering the mule may reveal clues about Diego and prevent traders from selling him.

## DM Notes

Dr.Brule was captured by looters but was able to kick his way out and escaped. The party should be able to find him at a crossroads. If they look for dr.Brule in [[Highreach]] they should eventually hear some rumours of a mule that was seen running along the main road. They may be able to catch up if they hurry.

## Resolution

The party was able to find Dr Brule at the crossroads on their way to [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Crestfall/Ardenville/Ardenville|Ardenville]] where they got attacked by Cannibal bandits. The party saved dr brule after defeating all of the bandits.
