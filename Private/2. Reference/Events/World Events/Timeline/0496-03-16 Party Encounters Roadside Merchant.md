## Setup
[[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Crestfall\Fairfield\NPCs\Bram Copperkettle|Bram Copperkettle]] is parked on the Great Western Road just short of Fairfield's western gate — close enough to catch travelers before customs, far enough to claim he's just "resting his mule."

His cart: mismatched crates, bundled tools, a rack of weapons with hand-scrawled prices. The weapons are junk — soft iron, fake rune stamps, tourist-grade knockoffs of dwarven style. One greatsword sits among them that is clearly different.

## The Sword
- A +2 greatsword with competent (not flawless) dwarven-style rune work — better forgery than the rest
- Genuinely magical; Jon's agent slipped it into Bram's stock on the road from Ardenville
- **Detect Magic / Arcana DC 10:** glows blue-white, reads as a +2 greatsword; Identify returns clean
- **Arcana DC 30 / nat 20:** something layered underneath the enchantment — not readable, just wrong
- Bram has it priced at 30gp with the rest of his "quality surplus"

## Running Bram
> "Genuine dwarven surplus, friend, straight from the Ironpeak passes. Can't tell you exactly where — trade secret, you understand — but I'll tell you this sword there cost me more than I'm asking."

- He genuinely doesn't know the sword is special
- If the party lowballs hard, he gets mildly offended ("I've got overhead, you know")
- If they ask about the supplier: short dwarven fellow, cloaked, west of Ardenville, paid fair, didn't chat
- He can be talked down but not dramatically — he thinks 30gp is already generous for "genuine dwarven work"

## What the Party Should Feel
They spotted something Bram has no idea about. They're outsmarting a clueless hustler and walking away with a +2 sword for 30gp or less. The score of a lifetime.

## Consequences
- Julie attunes to a cursed greatsword — curse TBD, activates when DM decides
- Bram has no further information about the sword's origin; the trail ends at a description of Jon's agent
