## DM Notes
- Party chose Ardenville for the boat race over heading directly to Silverleaf Lands
- One-shot style session — players unknowingly played as simulacrums of themselves
- Jon the lich created clones in a wizard's lab using Simulacrum + Modify Memory
- Clones woke in glass tubes filled with green slime, no gear, no class features, 10+CON HP
- The Hunger mechanic forced clones to kill and eat each other (Wis DC 15)
- Eating a dead clone = +10 HP, acid/cold/lightning/slashing immunity, blindsight 60ft, mutations
- **Liz was the last clone alive** — became the Failed Ooze Doppelganger
- Cut to real party at campsite — attacked by melting Liz-shaped creature
- Failed Ooze Doppelganger: AC 10, HP 80, immune to acid/cold/lightning/slashing, blindsight 60ft
- Arcana DC 15 on the corpse: magically altered ooze/simulacrum — hints at Jon's involvement
