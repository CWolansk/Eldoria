## DM Notes
- Party solved the Spanish Bible verse riddle on the marble barrier
- [[Seekers Eye]] revealed on stone pedestal — light blue pulsating orb
- Wizard (Jon the Unbroken) appeared, demanded the orb
- Party tried to hold onto it — failed DC 19 Reflex save
- Wizard yanked it with invisible force, crushed it, absorbed the power, then vanished
- Chasme (AC 15, HP 84) + 3 Larvae spawned for the fight
  - Phase 1: Chasme gets two turns, larvae swarm backline
  - Phase 2: Chasme absorbs dead body + ichor, grows larger, gets 1 turn
  - Phase 3: Chasme explodes → mine collapse begins
- Party had 7 rounds to escape through Floors 3→2→1
- Completes [[Goblins in the Mine]] quest
- [[Retrieve the Seekers Eye]] technically completed but artifact stolen
- [[Party due date for Alistair Goldman]] now impossible to fulfill
- Party leveled up
