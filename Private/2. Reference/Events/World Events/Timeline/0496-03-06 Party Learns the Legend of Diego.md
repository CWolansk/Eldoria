## DM Notes
- Party visited Jon Brightman at the Wizard's Tower
- Party did NOT reveal they were summoned from another world
- Jon's full account:
  - During the Wizarding Wars, Diego was summoned from another realm — world's most talented swordsman
  - Diego ended the wars by defeating lich Jon and sealing his power into artifacts
  - Artifacts protected so only someone from Diego's world can unlock them
  - First riddle (Seekers Eye) was in Spanish — a language from Diego's world
- Party realizes: they are from Diego's world, were summoned specifically because they can unlock artifacts, the lich has been manipulating them
- Each artifact retrieved potentially makes Jon the lich stronger
- Seekers Eye (already stolen by Jon) helps him locate remaining artifacts
- Next two artifacts in the Silverleaf Lands
