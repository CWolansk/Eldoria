## Consequences
- Jon the Summoner's power scattered into 7 artifacts hidden across Eldoria
- Each artifact can only be unlocked by someone from Diego's world
- Jon trapped in his tower, unable to unleash full power
- Jon begins summoning heroes from Diego's world to unknowingly retrieve the artifacts for him

## DM Notes
- Revealed to the party in [[Session 11 notes]] via [[Jon Brightman]]
- The Seekers Eye riddle was written in Spanish — proof of the Diego's-world lock
- Jon has been running this scheme for years; the current party is just the latest attempt
