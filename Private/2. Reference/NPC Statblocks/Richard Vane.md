# Richard Vane

## Design Intent
Richard is not a fair duelist; he weaponizes confidence, money, and nearby hired muscle.

## Stat Block
**Armor Class** 15 (fine breastplate under guildwear)  
**Hit Points** 44 (8d8 + 8)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 17 (+3) |

**Saving Throws** Wis +2, Cha +5  
**Skills** Deception +7, Insight +2, Persuasion +7  
**Senses** passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Common, Dwarvish  
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP) **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits
- **Guild Authority:** Richard has advantage on Charisma checks against creatures who believe the Merchants Guild can reward or punish them.
- **Protected Ego:** The first time Richard is hit each round while an ally is within 5 feet of him, he can reduce the damage by 4.

### Actions
- **Rapier:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.
- **Humiliating Order:** One creature Richard can see within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.
- **Command the Crew:** One allied creature within 60 feet that can hear Richard can use its reaction to move up to half its speed or make one weapon attack.

### Reactions
- **No, Him:** When a creature Richard can see targets him with an attack, Richard chooses one allied creature within 5 feet of him. The attack targets that ally instead if the ally is within the attack's reach or range.

## Tactics
- Opens by ordering Me'Shelle or Fran to pressure the most dangerous-looking opponent.
- Stays behind crew members and uses **Command the Crew** whenever an ally can get a clean hit.
- Turns vicious once mocked, bloodied, or publicly embarrassed.
- Bargains if reduced below 12 hit points and not cornered.

## Counterplay
- Richard is much weaker without nearby allies.
- Public mockery or proof of cowardice can make him reckless.
- He relies on reaction protection; forcing him to spend it early opens him up.
