# Cutlass

## Design Intent
Cutlass is a flashy sailor who hits hard when people are watching and makes risky moves for attention.

## Stat Block
**Armor Class** 14 (flashy leathers)  
**Hit Points** 33 (6d8 + 6)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) |

**Skills** Acrobatics +4, Performance +4  
**Senses** passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Common  
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP) **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits
- **Showboat:** Once per turn, Cutlass deals an extra 3 (1d6) damage to a creature if at least one ally can see him.

### Actions
- **Multiattack:** Cutlass makes two Cutlass attacks.
- **Cutlass:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.
- **Flashy Feint:** One creature within 5 feet must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.

## Tactics
- Opens with a flourish and targets whoever seems most impressed or annoyed.
- Tries to finish wounded enemies where the crew can see it.
- Competes with Saber even when it is tactically foolish.
- Runs if isolated and no one important is watching.

## Counterplay
- Cutlass is weaker when separated from spectators and allies.
- He can be baited into flashy but poor positioning.
- Rivalry with Saber can disrupt coordinated tactics.
