# Saber

## Design Intent
Saber is a precise duelist who protects his pride with polished technique and irritating corrections.

## Stat Block
**Armor Class** 15 (padded dueling coat and gloves)  
**Hit Points** 33 (6d8 + 6)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 11 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) |

**Skills** Acrobatics +5, Perception +3  
**Senses** passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Common  
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP) **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits
- **Exacting Footwork:** Saber does not provoke opportunity attacks from creatures he has hit this turn.

### Actions
- **Multiattack:** Saber makes two Saber attacks.
- **Saber:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.
- **Corrective Cut:** If Saber hits a creature that missed him since the end of his last turn, the attack deals an extra 3 (1d6) slashing damage.

### Reactions
- **Parry:** Saber adds +2 to his AC against one melee attack that would hit him. To do so, Saber must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

## Tactics
- Picks one opponent and tries to make the fight look like a duel.
- Uses **Exacting Footwork** to reposition beside Richard or Me'Shelle.
- Corrects allies loudly when they miss obvious openings.
- Breaks discipline if Cutlass is praised more than him.

## Counterplay
- Saber is proud and can be drawn into one-on-one challenges.
- Grapples and forced movement weaken his clean footwork.
- Mocking his precision can make him overcommit.
