# Caber

## Design Intent
Caber is a simple heavy sailor who solves combat problems by moving people and objects with too much force.

## Stat Block
**Armor Class** 13 (work leathers)  
**Hit Points** 38 (7d8 + 7)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 9 (-1) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) |

**Skills** Athletics +5  
**Senses** passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Common  
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP) **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits
- **Heavy Hauler:** Caber has advantage on Strength checks to lift, drag, shove, or break objects.

### Actions
- **Multiattack:** Caber makes two Dock Club attacks.
- **Dock Club:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.
- **Shoulder Check:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be pushed 5 feet.

## Tactics
- Goes after the closest obvious threat.
- Tries to shove enemies into water, cargo, railings, or Fran's reach.
- Follows Me'Shelle's orders unless confused or isolated.
- May hesitate if reminded of Salty Pete's old command style.

## Counterplay
- Caber is predictable and easy to bait.
- He struggles against enemies who refuse to stand where he can shove them.
- Social reminders about Salty Pete can split his focus.
