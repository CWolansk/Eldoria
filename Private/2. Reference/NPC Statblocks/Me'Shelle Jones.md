# Me'Shelle Jones

## Design Intent
Me'Shelle is the rival crew's tactical brain: calm, efficient, and dangerous when others follow his orders.

## Stat Block
**Armor Class** 15 (studded leather and quick footwork)  
**Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +5, Wis +3  
**Skills** Athletics +3, Insight +3, Intimidation +4, Perception +3  
**Senses** passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Common, Thieves' Cant  
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP) **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits
- **Crew Tactician:** Allied sailors within 30 feet of Me'Shelle add +1 to attack rolls while they can hear him.
- **Correct the Line:** Me'Shelle has advantage on checks to shove, grapple, or resist being shoved or grappled while on a dock, ship, or rope-lined terrain.

### Actions
- **Multiattack:** Me'Shelle makes two Cutlass attacks.
- **Cutlass:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.
- **Hard Command:** One allied creature within 30 feet can move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.

### Reactions
- **Rope Snare:** When a creature Me'Shelle can see moves within 10 feet of him, it must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or have its speed reduced to 0 until the end of the turn.

## Tactics
- Starts by positioning Fran or Caber between Richard and the party.
- Uses **Hard Command** to set up flanks, grapples, or retreats.
- Targets spellcasters and obvious leaders with pressure rather than speeches.
- Surrenders if Richard falls and the crew is clearly beaten.

## Counterplay
- Me'Shelle's strongest value is coordination; silence, distance, or chaos weakens him.
- He respects competent seamanship and may bargain if offered a practical out.
- His loyalty to Richard can be used against him.
