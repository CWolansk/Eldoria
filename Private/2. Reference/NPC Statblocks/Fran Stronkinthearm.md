# Fran Stronkinthearm

## Design Intent
Fran is the rival crew's intimidation piece: hard to move, hard to scare, and built to turn the dock itself into a weapon.

## Stat Block
**Armor Class** 14 (thick leathers)  
**Hit Points** 68 (8d8 + 32)  
**Speed** 25 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 (+4) | 10 (+0) | 18 (+4) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 11 (+0) |

**Saving Throws** Str +6, Con +6  
**Skills** Athletics +8, Intimidation +2  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11  
**Languages** Common, Dwarvish  
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP) **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits
- **Anchored Stance:** Fran has advantage on saving throws and ability checks against being knocked prone or moved against her will.
- **Dockbreaker:** Fran deals double damage to unattended wooden objects and structures.

### Actions
- **Multiattack:** Fran makes two Heavy Belaying Pin attacks.
- **Heavy Belaying Pin:** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
- **Heave Aside:** One Medium or smaller creature within 5 feet must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be pushed up to 10 feet and knocked prone.

### Reactions
- **Hold Fast:** When Fran would be moved, knocked prone, or forced off a dock or ship, she can reduce the forced movement to 0.

## Tactics
- Opens by shoving a front-line character into a bad position.
- Tries to pin the strongest-looking opponent in place.
- Breaks railings, barrels, oars, and dock planks if it creates an advantage.
- Stops fighting if the contest becomes dishonorable in a way Richard cannot justify.

## Counterplay
- Fran is slow and direct; range, difficult terrain, and mobility frustrate her.
- She respects clean strength contests and can be baited into proving herself.
- She is less committed to Richard's cheating than the rest of the crew.
