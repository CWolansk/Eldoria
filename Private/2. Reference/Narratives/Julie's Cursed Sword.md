# Julie's Cursed Sword

## Goal
Julie picks up a +2 greatsword from a shady roadside merchant outside Fairfield, thinking she's ripping him off. She is not.

## Connections
- [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Crestfall/Fairfield/Fairfield|Fairfield]] — party's next destination; merchant camps just outside the customs gate
- [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Orcish Wastes/Wizards Tower/NPCs/Summoner Jon AKA the wizard|Summoner Jon]] — had one of his agents slip the sword into the merchant's stock
- [[Private/1. The Party/Players/Julie]] — currently carries a +1 greatsword from [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Crestfall/Highreach/NPCs/Alaric Emberfell|Alaric Emberfell]]; been angling for an upgrade
- [[Private/2. Reference/Storylines/The Artifact Wars|The Artifact Wars]] — Jon is escalating pressure on the party between artifact legs
- Fairfield already has a counterfeit magic items problem (Shadowmere's diluted potions) — a fake dwarven sword fits the existing threat landscape

## Sparks
- Merchant [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Crestfall/Fairfield/NPCs/Bram Copperkettle|Bram Copperkettle]] is camped outside the walls — too seedy to pass customs, selling "reclaimed battlefield surplus"
- One greatsword in his cart is clearly better than the rest. Jon's agent dumped it there on the road from Ardenville.
- Low DC to detect magic (+2 greatsword, reads clean on Identify). DC 30 / nat 20 to sense the curse underneath.
- Party should feel like they spotted something Bram has no idea about and scored a bargain.

## Beats (if planned)
- **Seed**: Party spots Bram's cart on approach to Fairfield
- **Hook**: Detect Magic or sharp eye notices the sword glowing faintly among the junk
- **Encounter**: Haggling / roleplaying Bram — he's clueless and a bit insulted if they lowball too hard
- **Payoff (delayed)**: Curse activates — timing TBD by DM

## DM Notes
- Bram got his stock from a "dwarven fellow" who approached him on the road west of Ardenville. Short, hooded, paid in coin, didn't linger. That's all Bram knows.
- The rune work on the sword is a decent forgery of Ironpeak dwarven style — passable to non-smiths, wrong to anyone who knows real Stonefist marks.
- Jon's motive: he's just an asshole. This isn't a grand scheme — it's a petty dig at a party he's been watching since the Seekers Eye.
- Curse effect: open-ended — decide at the table when it feels right.
