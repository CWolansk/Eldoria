## Personality
- **Ideal:** Creation — he will birth life from nothing and prove every doubter wrong
- **Bond:** "The Work" — his ongoing homunculus project, which he refers to exclusively in the third person as if it's already alive
- **Flaw:** Staggeringly incompetent but absolutely convinced he's a genius; interprets every failure as evidence he's "ahead of his time"
- **Quirk:** Monologues at length about his brilliance to anyone who will listen — and to many who won't — frequently losing his train of thought and restarting from the top

## DM Notes
Location-agnostic — drop into any basement, back-alley workshop, or abandoned building. Pure comedy encounter. Play him as a man who watched one too many evil wizards give speeches and decided that was his calling, except he has no talent, no resources, and no self-awareness. Think Smiling Friends villain energy: absurdly dramatic, utterly harmless, takes himself dead seriously.

**How He Acts:**
- Found in a cluttered, foul-smelling workshop surrounded by jars of dubious liquids, anatomical sketches that are clearly wrong, and a worktable with something vaguely flesh-colored and lumpy sitting under a cloth
- Reacts to intruders with theatrical outrage — "You DARE enter the laboratory of DR. MALACHAR VOSS?!" — dramatic pause, waits for a reaction, visibly deflated if he doesn't get one
- Monologues constantly — about his genius, about the fools at the academy who expelled him, about how his homunculus will change the world — loses his place every 30 seconds, backtracks, starts over
- If the party expresses any interest in the homunculus, he whips the cloth off the worktable to reveal… a sad, inert lump of alchemical paste vaguely shaped like a person, roughly the size of a loaf of bread — "ISN'T IT MAGNIFICENT?!"
- Insists it moved once ("Last Tuesday — a twitch! A DEFINITIVE twitch!") — it did not move
- If challenged or mocked, gets increasingly dramatic — paces, gestures wildly, threatens to "unleash forces beyond your comprehension," knocks things over, accidentally breaks his own equipment
- **Never actually dangerous.** His most threatening act is throwing a flask of alchemical goo that smells terrible but does nothing

**Encounter Escalation:**
- **DC 8 Persuasion** — tell him the homunculus looks promising and he weeps with gratitude, offers to name it after you
- **DC 12 Insight** — he's not evil, just lonely and obsessive; the "creation" is his attempt to make something that won't leave
- **DC 10 Arcana** — the "homunculus" is alchemical paste with zero magical properties; it will never come to life through his methods
- **If attacked** — shrieks "NOT THE FACE!", throws the nearest jar (it contains pickle brine), and hides behind the worktable while begging for mercy
- **Combat stats:** Commoner stat block, no weapons, AC 10, will surrender immediately and loudly

**Recurring Chaos:**
- Can reappear in later sessions with increasingly absurd "improvements" — the lump now has glass eyes glued on, he's given it a wig, he's knitted it a tiny outfit
- Might set up shop in whatever city the party is in — always in the worst possible location (a sewer, someone else's basement, the back of a moving cart)
- Occasionally claims the homunculus spoke to him in a dream — quotes it at length — the "quotes" are transparently just his own opinions
- If the party helped or encouraged him previously, he considers them his "patrons" and sends them updates via courier (the letters are unhinged)

## Secrets & Motivations
- Expelled from a legitimate alchemist's academy years ago for "unsanctioned experimentation" — the experiment was mixing random things together and insisting the results were groundbreaking
- The homunculus project is real to him — he's spent years and every coin he has on it; the obsession is equal parts delusion and desperate loneliness
- Completely harmless — he has no actual magical ability, no combat skill, and no minions; his "evil lair" is wherever he's currently squatting

## Plot Hooks
- **Accidentally right** — his latest batch of alchemical paste is, by sheer dumb luck, a real alchemical reagent worth a fortune to the right buyer; he has no idea and is trying to sculpt it into feet for the homunculus
- **Someone's interested** — a real practitioner of dark magic has heard rumors of a man trying to create life in the back alleys, and sends agents to investigate; Dr. Voss is about to get attention he can't handle

## What the Players Can Do
- **Humor him:** Praise the homunculus, fund his "research," become his patrons — he's a loyal (if exhausting) contact who somehow always ends up near interesting trouble
- **Help him:** A wizard who actually knows what they're doing could teach him basic alchemy — he'd be pathetically grateful and might actually learn something
- **Ignore him:** He shows up later anyway, having moved his workshop into a barrel behind their favorite tavern
- **Rob him:** He has nothing of value except the paste and his misplaced confidence — taking the homunculus would genuinely break his heart
