## Description
Tall, shirtless human wizard with an oiled chest and a thick Germanic accent. Wears low-slung trousers, gold rings on every finger, and a fur-lined cloak draped over one shoulder — never both. Smells of cheap cologne and arcane reagents.

## Personality
- **Ideal:** Power — and looking good while wielding it
- **Bond:** The Sexy Wizard Gang — his crew, his family, his audience
- **Flaw:** Vanity so extreme it borders on delusion; genuinely believes he's irresistible
- **Quirk:** Flexes mid-conversation, especially when making a point or casting a spell

Location-agnostic villain — drop into any town or situation as needed. Can be scaled up or down depending on party level.

## Secrets & Motivations
- Gang leader of the Sexy Wizard Gang — a group of charismatic thugs and minor spellcasters who run extortion and kidnapping schemes
- Uses charm magic and sheer audacity more than raw power; prefers to talk his way out before fighting
- Deeply insecure underneath the bravado — the shirtless act started as a dare and now he can't stop because the gang expects it

## Plot Hooks
- Kidnaps a mayor's daughter in whatever town the party is in; can be resolved through charm/persuasion or combat — Günther respects boldness and might release her if sufficiently impressed or outwitted
