## Reputation

Thorin Ironheart is the current master of the Adamant Anvil, a position his family has held for twelve generations. He learned smithing from his mother, who learned from her father, in an unbroken chain going back to the founding of ForgePeak. His work is considered some of the finest in all three kingdoms.

He takes commissions rarely and only for those he deems worthy—warriors who will use the equipment properly, not collectors who'll mount it on a wall. He's gruff, demanding, and utterly uncompromising about quality. If you want his best work, you'll need to prove you deserve it.

## Services & Wares

**Heavy Armor (Dwarven-Forged):**
- *Plate Armor* - 1,500gp (standard quality, still exceptional by non-dwarven standards)
- *Plate Armor +1* (Rare) - 5,000gp (requires proving worthiness, 3-6 months wait)
- *Splint Armor* - 200gp
- *Half Plate* - 750gp
- *Shields* (all types) - Standard prices, but noticeably higher quality
- Custom-fitted armor (adds 1-3 months and 25% cost, but fits perfectly)

**Masterwork Weapons:**
- Any martial weapon - Standard price +50gp, counts as "dwarven-forged" (mostly cosmetic, but merchants pay extra)
- *Weapon +1* (Uncommon) - 500-1,000gp depending on weapon type (limited availability)
- *Weapon +2* (Rare) - 5,000-10,000gp (requires commission, months of wait, proof of worthiness)
- Dwarven-specific weapons (warhammers, battleaxes) receive special attention

**Specialty Items:**
- *Adamantine Armor* (Uncommon) - 1,500gp + armor base cost (long wait list)
- *Mithral Armor* (Uncommon) - 800gp + armor base cost (requires special ore procurement)
- Clan weapons (engraved with family symbols, commissioned for important occasions)

**Repairs & Modifications:**
- Armor repair - 10-50% of original cost, depending on damage
- Weapon resharpening and rebalancing - 5-25gp
- Adding enchantments to existing items - Thorin doesn't do this, but can recommend enchanters
- Battle damage assessment (free) - Thorin can tell you exactly what happened to your equipment

## Current Inventory

- Basic armor and weapons available for immediate purchase
- 1-2 +1 weapons currently finished and available
- Multiple custom commissions in various stages of completion
- The "Wall of Honor" - legendary pieces Thorin has made, not for sale, displayed as examples
- **Obsidian Blade Connection:** Thorin's grandfather helped forge the blade's hilt. He has records of its creation.

## Public Knowledge

- **Prove Your Worth:** Thorin won't sell his best work to anyone who can't prove they'll use it honorably and skillfully. Expect tests, questions, or tasks.
- **Dwarven Time:** Rush orders don't exist here. Quality takes time. You can pay extra to move up the queue, but Thorin won't compromise craftsmanship.
- **Respect Martial Skill:** Thorin respects warriors who've seen real combat and know how to care for their equipment. Show him battle-damaged gear and explain how you survived, and you'll earn his attention.
- **No Nonsense:** He doesn't haggle, doesn't accept bribes, and doesn't care about titles or fame. Your coin and your character are what matter.
- **The Ironheart Standard:** Any piece that leaves his forge meets his exacting standards. If it's not perfect, it gets melted down and reforged.
- **Teaching Tradition:** Thorin has seven apprentices at any given time. Watching the teaching process is permitted, but interrupting lessons will get you thrown out.

## Party Connections

- **Vanessa (Human Fighter):** As a dedicated warrior, Vanessa can earn Thorin's respect by:
    - Showing him her current equipment and explaining her fighting style
    - Demonstrating proper care and maintenance of weapons/armor
    - Sharing battle stories that highlight tactical thinking, not just glory
    - Potential for a masterwork commission if she proves worthy
- **Julie (Human Fighter/Archaeologist):** Thorin will be fascinated by Julie's archaeological work. If she can share historical information about dwarven crafting techniques, he'll:
    - Offer substantial discounts
    - Share family records and ancient techniques
    - Possibly help research the **Obsidian Blade**'s location
- **JP (Half-Orc Monk):** Thorin judges by skill, not race. If JP shows him the discipline of his monastic training and respect for craftsmanship, he'll receive fair treatment. Dwarves appreciate dedication.
- **Claire (Cleric):** Thorin can forge holy symbols and ritual items. He respects divine warriors.

## Quest Hooks

- **The Obsidian Blade:** Thorin's grandfather kept detailed notes about forging the blade's hilt. He'll share them—if the party completes a task for him first.
- **Ore Expedition:** Thorin needs rare adamantine ore from the deep caverns beneath ForgePeak. The mines are dangerous, infested with creatures and unstable. He'll reward successful retrieval with a masterwork item.
- **The Stolen Hammer:** Thorin's personal smithing hammer, a family heirloom passed down for 12 generations, has been stolen by a disgraced former apprentice. He wants it back, intact.
- **Proving Grounds:** To earn the right to commission a legendary item, Thorin requires the party to complete a challenge in the dwarven "Proving Grounds"—a combat trial that tests skill, endurance, and teamwork.
- **The High King's Commission:** The High King has commissioned a ceremonial set of armor for an important diplomatic event. Thorin needs rare gemstones and precious metals—and guards to retrieve them safely.
