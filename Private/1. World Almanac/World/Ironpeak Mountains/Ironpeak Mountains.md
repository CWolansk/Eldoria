## History
- **Stonefist Founding.** The legendary Stonefist clan carved ForgePeak from living rock, drawn by rich veins of precious metals and gems
- **Master Craftsmen.** Dwarven forges here produce the finest weapons and armor in Eldoria
- **Mountain Threats.** Cannibalistic human tribes roam the eastern slopes, attacking travelers near Ironroot
- **Artifact Connection.** The Obsidian Blade artifact is tied to the dwarven forges — Thorin Ironheart's grandfather helped forge its hilt

## Factions & Powers
- **Lord Thane Thronin Stonefist.** Rules from ForgePeak, respected for craftsmanship and warfare expertise
- **Ironheart Forge Dynasty.** The Adamant Anvil's master smiths, 12 generations of legendary crafting
- **Dwarven Monks of Ironroot.** Contemplative order maintaining ancient shrines
