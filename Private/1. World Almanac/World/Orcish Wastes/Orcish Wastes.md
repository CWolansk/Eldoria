## History
- **Tribal Territory.** Harsh lands controlled by warring orc tribes, hostile to outsiders
- **War with Crestfall.** Ongoing conflict with the human kingdom drives border tensions
- **The Wizard's Tower.** Jon the lich operates from a tower in this region, manipulating events across Eldoria
- **Ancient Ruins.** Remnants of a pre-orc civilization dot the wastes, hiding powerful artifacts
