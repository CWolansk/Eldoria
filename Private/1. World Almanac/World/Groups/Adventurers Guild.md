## Overview
Highreach's premier guild for mercenaries, bounty hunters, and adventurers-for-hire. Provides contract work, training facilities, and a network of contacts across Crestfall. Founded and run by [[Thaldrin Stormblade]].

## Goals
- Maintain Highreach's reputation as a hub for capable adventurers
- Secure lucrative contracts for guild members
- Protect the city from external threats through organized response

## Structure
- **Guildmaster:** [[Thaldrin Stormblade]] — founder and leader
- **Senior Member:** [[Aric Stormblade]] — Thaldrin's brother, veteran fighter and tactical advisor
- **Receptionist:** [[Ingrid Hightower]] — manages contracts, schedules, and walk-ins
- **Members:** [[Kara Nightshade]] (Rogue), [[Lyra Dawnbringer]] (Cleric), [[Nyla Windrider]] (Ranger)

## Notable Members
- [[Thaldrin Stormblade]] — Guildmaster, founded the guild
- [[Aric Stormblade]] — Senior fighter, Thaldrin's brother
- [[Ingrid Hightower]] — Receptionist, face of the guild
- [[Kara Nightshade]] — Rogue, intelligence and scouting
- [[Lyra Dawnbringer]] — Cleric, healer
- [[Nyla Windrider]] — Ranger, scout and archer
- [[Father Eldon Lightbringer]] — High Priest of Pelor, serves the church nearby

## Relations
- **[[Exterminators Guild]]** — Professional courtesy, shared member recruitment
- **City Guard** — Cooperative relationship for security contracts
- **[[Merchants Guild]]** — Trade connections, escort contracts
