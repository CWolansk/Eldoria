## Overview
The dominant trade organization in Highreach, controlling merchant licensing, trade route access, and market regulations across Crestfall. Led by the calculating [[Alistair Goldman]].

## Goals
- Maintain monopoly on trade regulation in Crestfall
- Collect membership fees and enforce compliance
- Expand guild influence over regional commerce

## Structure
- **Guildmaster:** [[Alistair Goldman]] — controls all guild operations
- **Receptionist:** [[Evelyn Marroway]] — manages appointments, ledgers, and member records
- **Enforcer:** [[Gareth Ironbrow]] — handles the guild's dirty work
- **Bodyguard:** [[Garrick Ironbrow]] — Goldman's personal protection
- **Ardenville Guildmaster:** [[Richard Vane]] -- local face of guild power on Lake Arden
- **Ardenville Consigliere:** [[Me'Shelle Jones]] -- sailor-advisor and race tactician

## Notable Members
- [[Alistair Goldman]] — Guildmaster
- [[Evelyn Marroway]] — Receptionist, uncanny memory for names and dealings
- [[Gareth Ironbrow]] — Enforcer, handles shakedowns
- [[Garrick Ironbrow]] — Goldman's personal bodyguard
- [[Richard Vane]] -- Ardenville guildmaster and boat-race sponsor
- [[Me'Shelle Jones]] -- Richard's loyal right hand
- [[Cutlass]], [[Saber]], [[Caber]] -- poached sailors on the Ardenville race crew
- [[Fran Stronkinthearm]] -- hired ringer and intimidation piece

## Relations
- **[[Adventurers Guild]]** — Source of escort and security contracts
- **[[Exterminators Guild]]** — Territorial friction over pest control services
- **[[Fishermans Guild]]** -- Local resistance to Merchants Guild dock control in Ardenville
- **Crown** — Useful but watched; guild influence is a political concern

## DM Notes
- Ardenville chapter uses the boat race to turn guild money into public prestige.
- Richard Vane is the polished front; Me'Shelle Jones is the operational brain.
- The poached crew is meant to humiliate [[Salty Pete]] and weaken Fisherman's Guild morale.

## Secrets
- The Ironbrow twins are secretly brothers — not common knowledge
- Goldman employs strong-arm tactics through Gareth to keep merchants in line
- Guild fees system is partly a shakedown — merchants who resist face "complications"
- Richard's charm collapses into bullying when someone denies him a win.
