## Overview
A secretive society of protectors dedicated to safeguarding the common people who form the bedrock of civilization. Operates across kingdom lines — Crestfall, Silverleaf, and Ironpeak.

## Goals
- Protect civilians and maintain social stability from the shadows
- Identify and recruit heroes who prioritize common folk over glory
- Provide intelligence and resources for humanitarian threats

## Structure
- Decentralized cell structure across multiple kingdoms
- Local coordinators handle recruitment and operations
- No known central leader — hierarchy is compartmentalized

## Notable Members
- [[Elder Rowan]] — Recruiter and local coordinator in Highreach
- [[Justin]] — Inducted for escorting elderly civilians during the Duplirat Crisis
- [[Vanessa]] — Inducted for prioritizing civilian protection during the Duplirat Crisis

## Philosophy
- **Core Belief:** The strength of any kingdom lies in its common people, not its nobles
- **Mission:** Protect civilians from the shadows without recognition
- **Values:** Humility, service, quiet heroism

## Insignia & Recognition
- **Symbol:** A plain, unremarkable rock — representing overlooked foundation stones
- **Badge:** Small plain rock pendant, appears ordinary to outsiders

## Secret Catchphrases
- **Primary:** "What do the common people smell?" / "Only what the rock is cooking."
- "The foundation stands ready." / "The rock never yields."
- "Upon what do the towers rest?" / "Upon the stone that does not break."
- "What holds when all else falls?" / "The foundation that endures."

## Recruitment
- **Recruiter:** [[Elder Rowan]] — observes candidates during crises
- **Qualification:** Must demonstrate prioritizing civilian safety over personal glory
- **Initiation:** Quiet ceremony during public celebrations; rock badge presented with secret catchphrase

## Operations
- Intelligence gathering, safe house networks, protective services
- Non-monetary support: information, sanctuary, connections
- Focus: humanitarian aid and threat assessment against civilian populations
- Network spans Crestfall, Silverleaf, and Ironpeak kingdoms

## Relations
- **Merchant guilds** — Works with those who rely on civilian trade
- **Kingdom politics** — Strictly neutral, focused solely on civilian welfare

## DM Notes
Can offer missions involving civilian protection, disaster relief, or countering threats to non-combatants. Intelligence network provides information about threats to common people.

## Secrets
- Entire organization is secret — public has no knowledge of its existence
- Recruitment is by observation only — Elder Rowan watches for those who protect civilians during crises
- Initiation happens during public celebrations to provide cover
- Network is far larger than the Highreach cell suggests

## Notable Operations
- **Highreach Duplirat Crisis (496-03-06)** — Identified and inducted heroes who protected civilians during the sewage crisis and monster invasion
