## Overview
Founded three generations ago by gnome refugees from the Orcish Wastes. Serves as the legitimate front for the most powerful gnome crime family in Highreach. Under [[Don Gnocianno]]'s flamboyant leadership — both guildmaster and actual mafia Don — the guild runs money laundering, smuggling, and protection operations through the city's tunnel network while maintaining perfect cover through genuine pest control services.

## Goals
- Maintain pest control front for criminal operations
- Secure royal pest control contract for palace access (the ultimate score — 500+ gp/month, access to treasures, state secrets, political intelligence)
- Expand criminal territory through "legitimate" inclusivity policy
- Eliminate competing pest control services from Merchants Guild
- Gradually integrate the party as unwitting criminal assets

## Structure
- **Guildmaster/Don:** [[Don Gnocianno]] — actual mafia Don using flamboyant persona as cover
- **Underboss/Master Exterminator:** [[Pip Whiskerwind]] — Don's predecessor's daughter, handles sensitive "pest problems"; leads Old School faction (stay small, stay hidden)
- **Consigliere/Chief Scout:** [[Nimble Nackfinger]] — tunnel network mastermind, smuggling coordinator, manages city guard relationships
- **Enforcer/Quartermaster:** [[Squeak Tinkerbottom]] — equipment and "enforcement", magical innovations for both pest control and B&E
- **Captain of Security:** [[Brix Ironwhistle]] — unknowingly compromised, believes guild is entirely legitimate

## Notable Members
- [[Don Gnocianno]] — Guildmaster and mafia Don
- [[Pip Whiskerwind]] — Underboss, poison specialist
- [[Nimble Nackfinger]] — Consigliere, smuggling coordinator

## Internal Politics
- **Old School** (Pip): Stay small, stay hidden, maintain perfect cover — recruiting outsiders threatens operational security
- **New Opportunities** (Don): Heroic reputation opens impossible criminal ventures; royal favor protects expansion
- **External Pressure**: Merchant Guild competition threatens both cover and territory; some merchants suspect true nature

## Services
### Legitimate Front
- Rat extermination: 5-15 gp per building (prices inflated to launder profits)
- Preventive maintenance: 2 gp/month per property (protection racket)
- Emergency response: 25 gp flat (covers contraband movement during "crises")
- Tunnel mapping: 10 gp/district (intelligence for criminal ops)

### Criminal Operations
- "Magical Pest Control": 50+ gp (witness/competitor elimination)
- "Urban Reconnaissance": 25-100 gp (surveillance and intelligence)
- "Lost Item Recovery": 10% of value (theft and fencing)
- "Secret Passage Location": 20-50 gp (smuggling route access)

## Membership Benefits
### Mechanical
- **Rat-Themed Headdress** (wondrous item): 60 ft darkvision, 1/day advantage on smell-based Perception
- Advantage on Investigation checks in Highreach's underground
- Free travel through guild-controlled tunnel systems
- 20% off alchemical pest control supplies (including lockpicks, poisons, "specialty" tools)

### Social & Resource
- Professional recognition from city guards and property owners
- Legal protection for members accused of B&E during jobs
- Tunnel maps of Highreach's underground infrastructure
- Contact network: city officials, property owners, underground informants
- Emergency distress response within tunnel system

## Headquarters: The Warren
Located 30 ft beneath the Noble District, accessed through hidden grate near the Royal Gardens. Central meeting hall with rat banners concealing family crests; oversized cheese wheel table with hidden compartments for weapons and contraband. Equipment storage (legitimate supplies mixed with criminal tools, forged documents, money counting area). Pip's laboratory (potion/poison brewing, headdress enchantment, lockpick enhancement). Tunnel command center with maps for pest control and smuggling routes; emergency evidence destruction protocols.

**Atmosphere:** Cheese and soap aroma masks poison brewing and money bleaching. Magical rat-oil lamps dim for clandestine meetings. Training rat squeaking covers conversations.

**Notable Features:**
- **Hall of Tails** — trophies from eliminated "rats" (criminal targets and snitches)
- **Communication Tubes** — connect to legitimate entrances and hidden safe houses citywide
- **Emergency Cache** — fake identities, emergency funds, escape route provisions
- **Shrine to Garl Glittergold** — conceals criminal ledger documenting transactions

## DM Notes
The guild functions as legitimate ally, moral challenge, or eventual antagonist depending on party choices. Party inducted as unwitting assets — rat-themed headdresses mark them as under Don's protection for other criminal organizations. Don plays the long game, gradually involving them in increasingly questionable "pest control" jobs.

## Secrets
- Entire guild is a front for the most powerful gnome crime family in Highreach
- Founded by gnome refugees from the Orcish Wastes; evolved from survival into organized crime
- "Inclusivity" recruitment of the party is calculated criminal strategy — their reputation provides cover
- Royal pest control contract is the ultimate score: palace access for treasures, secrets, intelligence
- Brix Ironwhistle doesn't know about the criminal operations
- Tunnel network serves dual purpose: pest control routes and smuggling corridors
- [[Salty Pete]] might be guild informant rather than innocent sewer worker

## Plot Hooks
- **Package Delivery to Ardenville** — "Special pest control shipment" (actually laundered money + criminal correspondence) to "Ratcatcher" Finn at the Salty Sailor. 50 gp courier job. Suspiciously heavy, sealed, strict instructions. Tests party's willingness; success builds trust for more questionable "deliveries."
- **Turf War** — Competing merchants discover guild's true nature, forcing escalating conflict. Party must choose sides as evidence mounts. Don needs their reputation for plausible deniability.
- **Criminal Network** — Guild connections could provide intelligence for tracking artifacts, but at moral cost. Demon cult activities may intersect gnome mafia territory, forcing uncomfortable alliances.
