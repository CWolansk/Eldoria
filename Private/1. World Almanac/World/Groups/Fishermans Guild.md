## Goals
- Protect fishing crews' livelihoods and traditions on Lake Arden
- Resist Merchants Guild encroachment on docking fees and lake access
- Maintain safety standards and weather prediction for all sailors
- Secure or demolish the storm-wrecked warehouse threatening waterfront safety

## Structure
- **Guildmistress:** [[Edda Netsong]] — 40-year veteran, final word on all guild matters
- Informal hierarchy; senior fishers mentor apprentices
- Membership: 10 sp/month — equipment repair, weather forecasts, emergency aid

## Notable Members
- [[Edda Netsong]] — Guildmistress, weather oracle, fierce protector of her people
- [[Salty Pete]] — respected member and competitive sailor

## Relations
- **[[HighReach Merchants Guild]]** — bitter rivals; resents their rising dock fees and control of the cliffside port
- **Adventurers Guild Outpost** — friendly; adventurers escort boats through dangerous waters
