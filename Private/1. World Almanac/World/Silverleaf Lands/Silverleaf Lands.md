## History
- **Ancient Elven Realm.** The Silverleaf Lands are one of the oldest civilizations in Eldoria, governed by wisdom and deep connection to nature
- **Queen Elara Starbreeze.** Revered ruler with connections to ancient spirits of the Everwood
- **Starfall Accords.** Formal trade agreements with Crestfall established centuries ago, Fairfield serves as the border gateway
- **Artifact Connection.** The Shadowstride Boots and Aetherstone Compass are hidden somewhere in these lands

## Settlements
- [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Silverleaf Lands/Frostglade/FrostGlade|Frostglade]] — capital city, arcane sanctuary and seat of the Council of Silverleaf
- [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Silverleaf Lands/Moonshade/Moonshade|Moonshade]] — hidden village of ancient lore, rumored location of the Shadowstride Boots
- [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Silverleaf Lands/Serenity Hollow/Serenity Hollow|Serenity Hollow]] — sacred grove village for spiritual retreats
- [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Silverleaf Lands/Stardew/Stardew|Stardew]] — agricultural village, peaceful farming community
- [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Silverleaf Lands/Starfall/Starfall|Starfall]] — artisan village near the Crestfall border, sister city to Fairfield

## Factions & Powers
- **Queen Elara Starbreeze.** Ruler of the Silverleaf Lands, connected to ancient spirits
- **Council of Silverleaf.** Wise elders and revered druids guiding the realm from the Hall of Elders in Frostglade
- **Elven Artisan Guilds.** Master weavers, jewelers, and enchanters throughout the settlements
