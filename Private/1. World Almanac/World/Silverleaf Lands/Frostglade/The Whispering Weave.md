## Reputation

Lysara is considered one of the finest arcane clothiers in the Silverleaf Lands, combining traditional elven weaving techniques with innovative enchantment methods. Her work is sought after by wizards, sorcerers, and bards across all three kingdoms, though her prices reflect her skill and her elven sense of time (she doesn't rush for anyone).

She has strong opinions about "proper" magical attunement and believes that a spellcaster's equipment should enhance their natural abilities, not compensate for lack of skill. She won't sell her best work to someone she deems unworthy.

## Services & Wares

**Enchanted Robes & Light Armor:**
- *Robe of Useful Items* (Uncommon) - 500gp (custom patches available)
- *Cloak of Protection* (Uncommon) - 1,000gp
- *Cloak of Elvenkind* (Uncommon) - 800gp (High Elf cultural item, requires approval)
- *Boots of Elvenkind* (Uncommon) - 500gp
- *Pearl of Power* (Uncommon) - 1,200gp (limited stock)
- Custom enchanted robes (1-3 weeks, 500-2,000gp depending on complexity)

**Caster Equipment:**
- **Arcane Focuses:** 
    - Masterwork wands, staffs, and orbs - 50-200gp
    - Crystal orbs grown in the Everwood - 100gp
    - Living wood staffs that continue to sprout leaves - 150gp
- **Component Pouches:**
    - Elven-made silk component pouches (higher quality) - 50gp
    - Blessed pouches that preserve components longer - 75gp
- **Spellbooks:**
    - Blank spellbooks with waterproof, tear-resistant pages - 50-100gp
    - Pre-owned spellbooks (contents vary, expensive)

**Specialty Services:**
- **Custom Enchantment:** Lysara can add minor enchantments to existing clothing or armor (1-4 weeks, 300-1,500gp)
- **Attunement Consultation:** She can examine a character's magical items and suggest optimal attunement choices - 25gp
- **Repairs:** Can repair damaged magical clothing or armor - Price varies
- **"Aesthetic Adjustments":** Will alter the appearance of magical items without changing function - 50-200gp

## Public Knowledge

- **Elven Standards:** Lysara works on elven time scales. Rush orders are possible but expensive (double cost, minimum).
- **Worthiness Test:** She won't sell her finest work to just anyone. She'll quiz spellcasters about their training, philosophy, and intentions.
- **Liz's Advantage:** As an Elf and a Bard (College of Lore), **Liz** will receive respectful treatment. Lysara appreciates scholarly pursuits and elven cultural connection.
- **Randi's Challenge:** **Randi** (Aarakocra Wizard) is an oddity—a non-elf practicing arcane arts seriously. Lysara will be curious and might test Randi's knowledge before selling major items.
- **No Armor for Brutes:** She doesn't make heavy armor and considers those who rely solely on martial strength to be "limited in vision."
- **The Starweaver Legacy:** Lysara's family has been weaving enchanted cloth for over 800 years. She carries that tradition with pride.
- **Cultural Items:** Some items (like Cloaks of Elvenkind) are culturally significant to elves. She'll require a good reason to sell them to non-elves.

## Party Connections

- **Liz (Elf Bard):** Lysara will recognize Liz as kin and fellow scholar. She'll:
    - Offer Liz first choice on new items
    - Provide 10% discount for cultural solidarity
    - Share information about Silverleaf politics and the **Shadowstride Boots**
    - Might commission Liz to perform at elven gatherings
- **Randi (Aarakocra Wizard):** Lysara is intrigued by Randi's aerial perspective and magical dedication. She might:
    - Offer to create custom robes designed for flight
    - Quiz Randi extensively about aerial spellcasting techniques
    - Charge standard prices but offer magical advice freely
- **Julie (Archaeologist Fighter):** If Julie shows interest in the historical significance of elven weaving, Lysara will warm to her and might share ancient techniques.

## Quest Hooks

- **The Shadowstride Boots:** Lysara's grandmother once repaired the enchantments on the boots before they were hidden in Silverleaf Lands. She has family records about their location.
- **The Stolen Pattern:** Someone has stolen one of Lysara's ancient weaving patterns—a design that could be used to create powerful illusion magic. She needs discreet retrieval.
- **Silk from the Starlight Spiders:** Lysara needs silk from the rare Starlight Spiders that live in the deepest parts of the Everwood. It's dangerous work, but the reward is a custom enchanted item.
- **Cultural Tension:** Lysara's traditionalist views about "who deserves elven magic" puts her at odds with younger elves who want to share knowledge with other races. The party might get caught in this debate.
