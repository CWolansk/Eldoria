```
The Netted Nymph is the heart and soul of Ardenville. Built in the traditional cottage style with low, dark-beamed ceilings and thick stone walls, it feels more like someone's home than a commercial establishment. The moment you duck through the doorway (mind your head if you're taller than a dwarf), you're enveloped by warmth from not one but three separate hearths crackling with driftwood fires.

The common room is small and cozy, filled with mismatched tables and chairs that have clearly been repaired many times. Fishing nets hang decoratively from the ceiling alongside dried kelp and colorful glass floats. The walls are covered with carved names, maritime knots tied as decoration, and a few faded paintings of ships on calm waters.

The dominant scent is the legendary fish stew—a massive iron pot that hangs over the central hearth, constantly simmering. According to local legend, it's never been allowed to go empty; ingredients are simply added as needed, creating a flavor that's been building for decades.

Behind the bar, Mira Softstep—a cheerful halfling woman with salt-and-pepper curls and flour-dusted aprons—greets everyone like family. Her laugh is infectious, and she seems to know everyone's business before they tell her.
```

## Reputation

If the docks are Ardenville's body, the Netted Nymph is its soul. Mira has run it for twenty years, and in that time it's become the unofficial town hall where fishermen share catches, merchants negotiate deals, and sailors swap tales. The fish stew is famous along the entire lakeshore—sailors from Highreach make the trip down just to have a bowl.

Mira herself is known for three things: her endless supply of fish stew, her encyclopedic knowledge of everyone in town, and her ability to find jobs for those who need work. She's helped more down-on-their-luck sailors and fishermen than anyone can count.

## Services & Wares

- **Lodging:** Small, clean rooms upstairs (8 cp/night for a shared room, 3 sp for private). Modest but comfortable.
- **Meals:** The famous perpetual fish stew (2 cp per bowl, includes bread). Simple fare—grilled fish, bread, cheese. (2 cp to 5 sp)
- **Drinks:** Local fish-house ale, imported wines, hot cider. (1 cp to 3 sp per mug)
- **Job Board:** Postings for fishing crews, cargo haulers, and odd jobs. Mira personally vets the listings.
- **Message Service:** Leave messages for people or mail to be picked up. Mira keeps it organized.

## Current Services

## Public Knowledge

- **The Eternal Stew:** The fish stew has been cooking continuously for thirty-seven years. Locals swear it gets better with age. Trying it is practically a rite of passage.
- **Information Central:** Want to know anything about Ardenville? Ask Mira. She knows everyone, their business, their families, and their secrets (though she's discreet).
- **Job Connections:** Looking for work on a fishing boat? Need cargo hauled? Want to hire a boat? Mira can connect you with the right people.
- **Credit for Locals:** Ardenville fishermen and their families can run tabs, payable when the catch comes in. Mira trusts her people.
- **Sailor's Refuge:** If you're a sailor down on your luck, Mira will feed you for free and help you find work. She has a soft spot for maritime folk.
- **Message Hub:** Since many sailors and merchants pass through, the Netted Nymph serves as an unofficial mail drop. Leave a message for someone, and Mira will make sure they get it.
- **Local Matchmaker:** Mira has a knack for knowing who would work well together, whether for business partnerships or romance. Many Ardenville couples met through her introductions.
- **No Fighting:** Mira runs a peaceful establishment. Start a fight and she'll ban you for a month. The regulars enforce this—they like their peaceful pub.
