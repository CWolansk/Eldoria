## DM Notes
Appears friendly, polite, charming, smart, and handsome, with tight curled hair and a neat mustache. Under pressure, reveals the truth: rude, arrogant, childish, condescending, sadistic, disrespectful, egotistical, greedy, selfish, obnoxious, and viciously competitive.

## Secrets & Motivations
- Poached Salty Pete's crew to make the Fisherman's Guild look weak.
- Wants the boat race to prove Ardenville belongs to guild money, not local sailors.
- Uses charm first, humiliation second, and contracts whenever possible.
