## DM Notes
Richard's large, jacked, bald Black sailor consigliere. Loyal, competitive, sometimes aggressive and fierce; he supports Richard publicly and translates Richard's ego into workable pressure tactics.

## Secrets & Motivations
- Handled the actual poaching of Salty Pete's old crew.
- Knows Richard is a liability when angry, but prefers steering him to replacing him.
- Has enough sailing sense to be the true tactical brain of the rival crew.
