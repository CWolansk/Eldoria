## Reputation

The Fisherman's Guild is the oldest organization in Ardenville—older than the town itself, some say. It exists to help fishermen survive the dangerous work of harvesting Lake Arden's bounty. The guild provides nets, shares knowledge of the best fishing spots, predicts weather, and ensures that no fisher's family goes hungry when the catch is poor.

Edda Netsong has been guildmistress for forty years, and under her leadership, the guild has thrived. She's known for her uncanny ability to predict weather, her encyclopedic knowledge of the lake, and her fierce protection of her guild members.

## Services & Wares

- **Net Repair:** Expert net mending and new net construction.
- **Boat Maintenance:** Minor repairs, caulking, sail mending.
- **Weather Predictions:** Edda provides daily fishing forecasts and storm warnings.
- **Fishing Licenses:** Official permissions to fish Lake Arden commercially.
- **Maritime Supplies:** Hooks, line, floats, and other fishing essentials at cost for members.
- **Apprenticeships:** Training young people in the fishing trade.
- **Emergency Aid:** Support for fishermen's families during hard times.

## Public Knowledge

- **Membership Benefits:** Guild members (10 sp per month) get access to equipment repair, weather forecasts, and emergency aid. Most Ardenville fishermen belong.
- **Edda's Weather Sense:** Edda can predict weather with uncanny accuracy. If she says a storm is coming, believe her. Many lives have been saved by heeding her warnings.
- **Lake Knowledge:** Need to know about dangerous currents, good fishing spots, or hidden hazards? The guild maps everything. Members share this knowledge freely.
- **Superstition Respected:** The guild takes maritime superstitions seriously. Mock them at your own risk—the lake is dangerous, and tradition keeps people safe.
- **Community Support:** When a fisher is lost or injured, the guild ensures their family doesn't starve. It's a true community organization.
- **Boat Blessing:** For a small fee, Edda will perform a traditional blessing for new boats. Most fishermen insist on it before first launch.
- **The Pike Skulls:** Each skull represents a legendary catch by a guild member. Having your catch's skull mounted on the hall is the highest honor.
- **Training the Young:** The guild runs apprenticeships for youngsters who want to learn fishing. They're always looking for hardworking kids to teach.
- **Non-Members Welcome:** You don't have to be a guild member to buy supplies or get advice, but members get better prices and priority service.
