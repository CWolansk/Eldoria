## Read-Aloud
> A vast, briny lake shrouded in persistent mist. The water darkens quickly past the shallows — kelp forests choke the depths, and visibility drops to nothing farther out. Watchtower silhouettes fade into fog along the shore.

## Hazards & Inhabitants
- Slime eels infest the kelp beds
- River trolls and lake serpents lurk in deeper waters
- Dangerous currents shift unpredictably
- Fog can disorient even experienced sailors
