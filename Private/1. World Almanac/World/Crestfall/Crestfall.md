## History
- **Kingdom Founded.** Human realm established centuries ago, built around the capital of Highreach
- **Crestfall Council.** Governed by King Alaric Stormhelm and a five-member council overseeing military, arcane, treasury, diplomacy, and borders
- **Orc Wars.** Currently at war with orc tribes from the Orcish Wastes, driving military tension across the kingdom
- **The Artifact Wars.** Recent events revealed the lich Jon's manipulation — the Highreach Mines collapsed, releasing duplirats and sparking a citywide crisis

## Settlements

## Factions & Powers
- **The Crown.** King Alaric Stormhelm rules from Highreach with the Council of Crestfall
- **[[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Groups/Adventurers Guild|Adventurers Guild]].** Major presence in Highreach, party's primary faction
- **[[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Groups/Merchants Guild|Merchants Guild]].** Controls trade routes, involved in bandit operations exposed by the party
- **[[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Groups/Exterminators Guild|Exterminators Guild]].** Gnome-operated pest control — secretly a criminal organization
- **[[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Groups/Foundation|Foundation]].** Secret society that protects civilians, party recently inducted
