## Secrets & Motivations
- Has no idea the greatsword in his cart is genuinely magical or cursed — he bought his stock in bulk from a "dwarven fellow" on the road west of Ardenville (one of Jon's agents, hooded, paid in coin, didn't linger)
- Camps outside Fairfield's gates deliberately — his goods wouldn't survive customs inspection (fake maker's marks, undeclared stock)
- If pressed hard about where the sword came from: "Short fella. Dwarven type. Paid fair, didn't haggle, moved on. That's all I know, friend."
