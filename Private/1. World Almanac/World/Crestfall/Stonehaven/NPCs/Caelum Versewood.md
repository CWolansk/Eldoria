## Description
Lean half-elf, sun-weathered, ink-stained knuckles. Longsword on one hip, leather journal on the other. Quiet voice that carries when he recites verse. Smells of pine resin and old paper.

## Personality
- **Ideal:** Truth
- **Bond:** A half-finished epic poem about Stonehaven's founding
- **Flaw:** Can't walk away from a good story, even into danger
- **Quirk:** Mouths words silently after someone says something interesting
