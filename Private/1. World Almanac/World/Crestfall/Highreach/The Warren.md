## Hidden Operations
- **Central Meeting Hall** — rat banners conceal family crests; oversized cheese wheel table with hidden compartments for weapons and contraband
- **Equipment Storage** — legitimate pest control supplies mixed with criminal tools, forged documents, and money counting area
- **Pip's Laboratory** — potion brewing doubles as poison creation; headdress enchantment workshop includes lockpick enhancement and surveillance equipment
- **Tunnel Command** — detailed maps for pest control and smuggling route coordination; emergency protocols include evidence destruction procedures

## Notable Features
- **The Hall of Tails** — wall trophies from eliminated "rats" (criminal targets and snitches)
- **Communication Tubes** — speaking tubes to legitimate entrances and hidden safe houses throughout the city
- **Emergency Cache** — fake identities, emergency funds, escape route provisions
- **Shrine to Garl Glittergold** — traditional altar concealing the family ledger of criminal transactions

## Atmosphere
Magical rat-oil lamps provide normal lighting but dim for clandestine meetings. Training rat squeaking covers conversations from eavesdropping. Immaculate cleanliness serves professional pride and evidence elimination.

## Organization
See [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Groups/Exterminators Guild|Exterminators Guild]] for full organizational details.

## DM Notes
Perfect location for monitoring wealthy targets in the Noble District. The cheese and soap aroma masks chemical scents from poison brewing and money bleaching. Every "feature" serves a dual purpose — legitimate pest control and criminal operations.
