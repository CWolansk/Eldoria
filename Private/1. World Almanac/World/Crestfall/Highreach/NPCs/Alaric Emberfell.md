# Alaric Emberfell (DM Notes)
**Public File**: [[Alaric Emberfell]]

## The Truth
Alaric's pride is his greatest strength and weakness. He genuinely believes no one in the three kingdoms can match his skill. He is secretly frustrated by the Merchants Guild's control over rare material imports, viewing them as price-gouging bureaucrats who don't respect the art. He is looking for adventurers who are reliable enough to bypass the guild and acquire materials directly.

## Roleplay Guide
- **Ideal**: "The quality of the work is the only thing that matters."
- **Tactics**: In social encounters, he is dismissive until the party proves they are serious customers with an appreciation for fine craft. He will not be rushed or intimidated.
- **DC Checks**:
  - *Persuasion DC 18*: To get him to consider a commission on a payment plan.
  - *Insight DC 16*: To notice his frustration when discussing material costs, hinting at his dislike for the Merchants Guild.
  - *History/Investigation DC 15*: To recognize his maker's mark on a high-quality item found elsewhere.

### Party Interactions
- **JP (Half-Orc)**: As a dwarf, Alaric is inherently suspicious of orc-kind. He is gruff and watchful of JP, expecting him to be brutish. He will be impressed by any display of discipline or unexpected knowledge (e.g., from JP's time as a sailor).
- **Justin (Merchant)**: He views Justin with professional skepticism, seeing him as a typical "all talk, no substance" merchant. He'll respect Justin more if he demonstrates shrewd knowledge of trade routes for rare metals.
- **Liz (Elf)**: He respects the elven tradition of long-lived craft, but also sees them as rivals. He will engage with Liz on a technical level, trying to prove dwarven smithing is superior.

## Plot Hooks
- [ ] **Quest: Rare Ore**: If the party earns his trust, he will offer a massive discount on a future commission if they can retrieve a shipment of Adamantine ore from a dangerous, monster-infested section of the [[Highreach Mines]], bypassing the [[Merchants Guild]].
- [ ] **Secret**: He has a friendly rivalry with the master smith in [[Forgepeak]] and would pay well for information on new techniques they are developing.

## Resources & Capabilities
- Master-level craftsmanship in magical armor and weapons (Rare to Very Rare)
- Access to rare materials through unofficial channels
- Deep knowledge of metallurgy and magical infusions
- Can identify magical items and their enchantments
- Connections to dwarven suppliers in Ironpeak Mountains
