## Key Features
- 4 levels descending deeper into the mountain
- Iron ore veins long since depleted — the mine was abandoned by dwarves and men
- Goblin-rigged traps and sentries throughout upper levels
- Lower levels grow twisted and unnatural — carved walls warp into organic shapes
- Red fog and signs of dark magic on the deepest levels

## Hazards & Inhabitants
- **Level 1** — low-level goblins, minor puzzles and sentries ([[Level 1 Outline]])
- **Level 2** — mine walls begin to warp, partial transition to natural cavern ([[Level 2 Outline]])
- **Level 3** — red fog, twisted passages, bodies of [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Groups/The Nightstalkers|The Nightstalkers]] found here ([[Level 3 Outline]])
- **Level 4** — large chamber with an opaque wall and a carved message on the floor

## DM Notes
- Goblins on Level 1 have been raiding merchants and travelers — complaints filed with the [[Highreach Adventurers Guild]]
- [[Thoren Ironfist]], leader of [[Private/1. World Almanac/World/Groups/The Nightstalkers|The Nightstalkers]], was killed in [[Room 10 Level 1]] (the boss room) while trying to escape
- Thoren left journal notes at floor entrances/exits during his escape to warn future adventurers
  - [[Room 10 Level 1]] — final note, describes party wipe
  - [[Room 1 Level 2]] — hidden note with partial clue to Room 5 puzzle
  - Room 1 Level 3 — note location
